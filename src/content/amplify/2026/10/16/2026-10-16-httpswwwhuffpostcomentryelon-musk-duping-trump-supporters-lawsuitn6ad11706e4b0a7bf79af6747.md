<p>Intermittent trillionaire Elon Musk settled a class action lawsuit Thursday over allegations that his political action committee engaged in fraud in the 2024 presidential election by falsely telling voters they had a chance to randomly win $1 million when the winners were actually predetermined.</p>
<p>“The Parties have reached a Settlement and respectfully request that the Court vacate all deadlines and stay further proceedings for a period of thirty (30) days to allow the Parties to finalize and execute the settlement agreement and file a Motion for Preliminary Approval,” a one-page filing reads, according to online court records.</p>
<p>As part of an effort to get President <a href="https://www.huffpost.com/news/topic/donald-trump">Donald Trump</a> elected, Musk set up the committee ― America PAC ― a month before the election. Using his PAC, Musk told Trump supporters in key battleground states they <a data-vars-item-name="had a chance to win $1 million" data-vars-item-type="text" data-vars-position-in-subunit="0" data-vars-subunit-name="article_body" data-vars-subunit-type="component" data-vars-target-content-id="67351000e4b0958bad3dffd3" data-vars-target-content-type="buzz" data-vars-type="web_internal_link" data-vars-unit-name="6ad11706e4b0a7bf79af6747" data-vars-unit-type="buzz_body" href="https://www.huffpost.com/entry/elon-musk-lawsuits-america-pac_n_67351000e4b0958bad3dffd3">had a chance to win $1 million</a> if they gave away their personal identifying information and signed an online petition in support of “free speech and the right to bear arms.”</p>
<p>While Philadelphia District Attorney Larry Krasner went to court to accuse Musk of running an illegal lottery scheme, a <a data-vars-item-name="Pennsylvania judge allowed" data-vars-item-type="text" data-vars-position-in-subunit="1" data-vars-subunit-name="article_body" data-vars-subunit-type="component" data-vars-target-content-id="6728f92ae4b05debb72b7a69" data-vars-target-content-type="buzz" data-vars-type="web_internal_link" data-vars-unit-name="6ad11706e4b0a7bf79af6747" data-vars-unit-type="buzz_body" href="https://www.huffpost.com/entry/judge-lets-elon-musks-1-million-daily-voter-giveaway-proceed_n_6728f92ae4b05debb72b7a69">Pennsylvania judge allowed</a> the payouts to continue after attorneys for Musk’s PAC successfully argued that it wasn’t actually a lottery because winners were selected “based on their suitability” to serve as spokespeople for the PAC and had earned the $1 million as payment.</p>
<p>So while Musk doled out millions to more than a dozen winners, none of them were randomly selected.</p>
<p>Multiple lawsuits were filed against Musk in the days after the election. Joy Havrick of Arizona alleged in a complaint that she was treated as a “marketing prop.”</p>
<p>“Had Plaintiff been aware that the lottery was not random, or that she did not have a fair chance to win the prize, or that her selection hinged on her perceived favorability to Defendants as a marketing prop, she would not have signed or supported the America PAC petition and would not have provided her PII [Personal Identifying Information] to Defendants,” the complaint said.</p>
<p>Attorney Mark Bankston, who has <a data-vars-item-name="previously represented Sandy Hook families" data-vars-item-type="text" data-vars-position-in-subunit="2" data-vars-subunit-name="article_body" data-vars-subunit-type="component" data-vars-target-content-id="62ebdf73e4b09fecea4bf60e" data-vars-target-content-type="buzz" data-vars-type="web_internal_link" data-vars-unit-name="6ad11706e4b0a7bf79af6747" data-vars-unit-type="buzz_body" href="https://www.huffpost.com/entry/alex-jones-verdict-sandy-hook-defamation_n_62ebdf73e4b09fecea4bf60e">previously represented Sandy Hook families</a> and successfully sued <a data-vars-item-name="conspiracy theorist Alex Jones" data-vars-item-type="text" data-vars-position-in-subunit="3" data-vars-subunit-name="article_body" data-vars-subunit-type="component" data-vars-target-content-id="6735022fe4b0958bad3decf5" data-vars-target-content-type="buzz" data-vars-type="web_internal_link" data-vars-unit-name="6ad11706e4b0a7bf79af6747" data-vars-unit-type="buzz_body" href="https://www.huffpost.com/entry/onion-alex-jones-infowars-auction_n_6735022fe4b0958bad3decf5">conspiracy theorist Alex Jones</a> for defamation, represents Havrick. Bankston told HuffPost he is “unable to comment at this time.”</p>
<p>Details of the settlement, including how much Musk will ultimately have to pay, were not immediately disclosed.</p>
<p>A lawyer for Musk did not immediately respond to a request for comment.</p>
