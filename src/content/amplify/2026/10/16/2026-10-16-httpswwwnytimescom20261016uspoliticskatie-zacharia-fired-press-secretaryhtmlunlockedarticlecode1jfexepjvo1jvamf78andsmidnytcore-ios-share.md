title: Katie Zacharia Is Out After 5 Days as White House Press Secretary
---
<p>President Trump’s new press secretary, Katie Zacharia, was fired Friday, after less than a week on the job, according to three people familiar with the matter.</p><p>Neither the White House nor Ms. Zacharia responded to requests for comment.</p><p>But Mr. Trump told reporters on Friday that he encouraged Ms. Zacharia to “reconsider” the job.</p><p>“Honestly, if I were you, I’d reconsider,” he told reporters, recounting his conversation with his press secretary.</p><p>He added: “Katie, do you need this? I don’t think you need this.”</p><p>Ms. Zacharia had gotten off to <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/13/us/politics/katie-zacharia-white-house-first-briefing.html">a bumpy start</a> with her first and final press briefing on Tuesday, followed by an onslaught of headlines about whether she had <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/10/14/us/politics/trump-press-secretary-katie-zacharia-lawyer.html">misrepresented herself</a> as a lawyer — the president called her “an attorney” — despite failing her bar exam.</p><p><em>This is a developing story.
