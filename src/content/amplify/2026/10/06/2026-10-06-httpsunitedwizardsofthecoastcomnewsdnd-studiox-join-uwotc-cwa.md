<p>Today, October 6, 2026, the designers, artists, writers, producers, and workers behind <em>Dungeons &amp; Dragons, Magic: The Gathering</em>, <em>Duel Masters</em>, <em>D&amp;D Beyond</em>, and countless other games are uniting in solidarity to form a union with the Communications Workers of America (CWA)!</p>
<p>As of this morning, both the <em>Dungeons &amp; Dragons</em> team (which includes the workers behind <em>Dungeons &amp; Dragons </em>and <em>D&amp;D Beyond</em>) and Studio X (which includes the tabletop <em>Magic: The Gathering </em>and <em>Duel Masters </em>teams) informed Wizards of the Coast leadership of our intent to unionize, and called on the company to voluntarily recognize our union. We’re calling leadership to voluntarily recognize our union by October 13th, and you can <a href="https://actionnetwork.org/forms/uwotcpetition/"><u>sign our petition</u></a> to show your support for our efforts.</p>
<p>We’re proud to follow in the footsteps of <em>MTG Arena</em>’s own union, who recently <a href="https://unitedwizardsofthecoast.com/news/2026-06-23-we-have-a-union"><u>won their union election</u></a>, and have worked with the <em>MTG Arena</em> union in preparation for today. Now, we get to share it with you all!</p>
<p>We are stewards of our games who deserve to be treated with all the respect and dignity that entails. And as the teams responsible for so much of Hasbro and Wizards of the Coast’s profits, we deserve the following:</p>
<ul><li>Wages that reflect our work and defined career progression,</li><li>Job security against mounting industry instability,</li><li>Reliable and easily accessible healthcare for us and our families,</li><li>Sustainable workloads and practices, including remote and hybrid working,</li><li>Protection against the encroachment of AI,</li><li>Full ownership of the work we create outside of the office,</li><li>And additional needs outlined in our individual units’ goals.</li></ul>
<p>While today’s announcement is certainly exciting, it’s just the start. To the fans of D&amp;D, <em>Magic</em>, and more, we ask that you <a href="https://actionnetwork.org/forms/uwotcpetition/"><u>sign our petition</u></a> asking for Wizards of the Coast to voluntarily recognize our union and engage in good-faith bargaining. The community support for <em>MTG Arena</em>’s union was vital to their monumental victory, and we hope that you show the same support for our mission here today. You can also show your support on social media and share this statement with your communities. The more eyes on our campaign, the better!</p>
<p>There is so much excitement from our internal teams for this union, not just for the protections it offers us, but for the chance to make our lives and the games we create even better. In preserving our rights and establishing long-awaited protections for our workers, we hope to make all worlds—both the ones we create and the one we live in—better places. Once recognized, we will begin our bargaining and share additional details with you all as they develop.</p>
<p>The games we create are foundations of the gaming industry. Just as D&amp;D has inspired countless TTRPGs and <em>Magic</em> sparked the TCG revolution, we hope to catalyze real change within our industry and build upon the groundwork laid by other gaming unions, creating a space where all workers are protected and treated fairly.</p>
<p>The workers of Wizards of the Coast have shown time and time again that we change the game. This is one of those times, where we get to redefine what is possible and what can be created—and this time, we’re inviting you to join us! Sign our petition, share it with your communities, and ask Wizards of the Coast and Hasbro to voluntarily recognize our union. Let’s change the gaming world together!</p>
<p>From,</p>
<p>The <em>Dungeons &amp; Dragons </em>team and Studio X</p>
