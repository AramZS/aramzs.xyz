<p>THOUSANDS of Asos customers have been sent a phone alert saying the retail giant has been hacked.</p>
<p>Customers received a mobile app notification on Tuesday morning, titled “Asos hacked”, which directed them to a Telegram account.</p>
<p>The message read: “Dear ASOS DPO and IT, we have full compromised the Snowflake instance. Engage with us, or we will leak it,” followed by the Telegram link.</p>

<p>Shares in the company tumbled by more than 10% as a result of the alleged hacking incident.</p>
<p>The alleged hack refers to cloud firm Snowflake, which stores data for many major companies.</p>
<p>It has been the reported subject of a number of data breaches in recent years, including an attack on Ticketmaster which saw customer details stolen.</p>

<p>Asos, which also owns brands including Topshop and Miss Selfridge, has 17 million customers globally.</p>
<p>The UK is the group’s largest market, representing 49% of all revenues in the first half of the latest financial year.</p>
<p>The fast fashion firm is currently undergoing a major turnaround programme in a bid to halt declining sales and return to profit.</p>
<p>It comes after raft of UK retailers were targeted by cyber attackers over the past two years, including Marks &amp; Spencer and Harrods.</p>
