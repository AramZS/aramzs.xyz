<h1>Wretched Domains: nine adventures for Hollows</h1>
<p>The items below will be released on the specified date.</p>
<p>Scour nine repugnant micro-realities with sword and shot with this set of <em>Hollows</em> scenarios.</p>
<p><em>Wretched Domains</em> is a set of nine scenarios for <em>Hollows</em>, each one imagined by a different pair of gifted writers and artists. The scenarios can stand alone or be played one after another, providing enough material for an entire <em>Hollows</em> campaign.</p>
<p>Each scenario, as well as a compelling story and a set of creatively nasty opponents, provides a glimpse into a slice of <em>Hollows</em>’ setting, The Isles. Peer behind the curtain at education, the exploitation of the natural world, the wreckage of imperial commerce, art, love, family and idealism – and of course war, The Isles’ national pride as well as its downfall.</p>
<p>Every Wretched Domain includes a storyline that explains its Lord’s suffering and motivation, a mapped out Hollow for Hunters to explore, and stats and tactics for four Entities including the Hollow’s Lord.</p>
<p>These Hollows range in challenge level from beginner-friendly to particularly cruel amusements suited to experienced Hunters, but they’ll all provide three or more sessions of play.</p>
<h2>What's in the box?</h2>
<p><em>Wretched Domains</em> includes:</p>
<ul>
<li><em>The Forcross Remedial Foundation</em> by Chris Taylor, with art by Letty Wilson. The headmistress of a reform school’s worst fears spin out as nightmares inspired by the fairytales she detests.</li>
<li><em>The Laboratory of Erasmus Ward</em> by Kieron Gillen, with art by Daniel Vega. A scientist dreams of the future of war, imprisoned in a nightmare of ever bleaker and more bloodthirsty killing fields.</li>
<li><em>Bleake Hill Academy</em> by Jay Dragon, with art by Johan Nohr. A child banished to boarding school becomes trapped in their own trauma, necessitating a rescue by a team of Hunters more used to resolving their problems with murder.</li>
<li><em>The Polychrome Sea</em> by Minærva McJanda, with art by Carly Allen-Fletcher. A trailblazing painter turns violent maelstrom in this perilous odyssey through an artist’s psyche.</li>
<li><em>In Watern Wood</em> by Jinn Hermiston, with art by James Fenner. A group of Hunters learn that there is nothing more fearsome than nature.</li>
<li><em>Whitcliffe Moor</em> by David Chapman, with art by Valin Mattheis. Star-crossed lovers reenact their tempestuous affair across a Hollow of windswept moors and embodied loneliness.</li>
<li><em>The Vengeance of Alfie Reede</em> by Sasha Sienna and Helen Gould, with art by Morgan Robles. Explore the rotting remains of a once-proud shipping industry and the thousands who worked in it in this waterlogged horror story.</li>
<li><em>Grimlock Manor</em> by Eleanor Hingley, with art by Felix Abel Klaer. A fratricidal aristocrat tends a veritable garden of sin and corruption in a warped version of his ancestral home.</li>
<li><em>The Enlightened Alain Cooke</em> by Samuel Clarice Mui Shen Er, with art by Kolokomiks. A would-be philosopher mourns the years he lost chasing a dream never his to pursue.</li>
</ul>
<p>You will need a copy of the <a href="https://rowanrookanddecard.com/product/hollows-ttrpg/"><em>Hollows</em></a> core rules to play.</p>
<p><em>Hollows</em> features mature themes and is recommended for adult players.</p>
<h2>About Hollows</h2>
<p><em>Hollows</em> is a fast-paced, intricate and flavourful tactical combat roleplaying game that puts players in the role of deathless Hunters sworn to cleanse their home of spiritual rot. By descending into the nightmares of the brutal and broken, they can destroy monstrous Entities and claw back some control over the ruins of their nation. Using a unique tactical grid system, <em>Hollows</em> focuses on the big monsters players are fighting and cuts out much of the minutiae of combat whilst remaining dynamic, tactical and vicious; Hunters delve into capsule ghost stories to purge them of the infection within, risking their own humanity one battle at a time. <a href="https://rowanrookanddecard.com/product/hollows-ttrpg/">You can get your own copy here.</a></p>
