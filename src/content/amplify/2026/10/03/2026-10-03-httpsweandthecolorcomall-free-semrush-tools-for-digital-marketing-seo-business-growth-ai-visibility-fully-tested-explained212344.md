<h3>How Does a Gate Rating Sort the Free Tools?</h3>
<p>A Gate Rating measures the friction between you and a result. So I use four levels.</p>
<ul> <li><strong>Open</strong> tools run without an account and advertise no cap. The Sitemap Generator claims unlimited uses.</li> <li><strong>Metered</strong> tools run without an account but count your runs. The AI Visibility Checker allows three per day.</li> <li><strong>Linked</strong> tools send you into the main <a href="https://semrush.sjv.io/Gb3Gy9">Semrush app</a>, where free account limits apply.</li> <li><strong>Gated</strong> tools need an account before they show anything.</li> </ul>
<p>However, a free account isn’t a loophole. Semrush’s <a href="https://www.semrush.com/pricing/">pricing page</a> lists it at one demo project and 10 reports per day. I call that the 10-Report Ceiling.</p>

<p><a href="https://semrush.sjv.io/Gb3Gy9">Semrush</a> models those numbers from aggregated browsing data plus partner and public sources. It calls the results directional rather than exact. Still, I agree with that label.</p>

<p>Type any domain and you get an Authority Score from 1 to 100. The report adds backlinks, referring domains, estimated organic traffic, and 12 months of link growth. It also lists top pages by backlinks and top competitors.</p>
<p><a href="https://semrush.sjv.io/Gb3Gy9">Semrush</a> builds the score from eight weighted signals. One covers link power, and one covers organic traffic. The other six hunt for spam, such as zero Google top-100 rankings or more than 90% dofollow referring domains. Semrush also refreshes scores every two weeks. That design punishes bought links, so a link farm with no traffic scores low. Semrush calls that combination harder to game. Meanwhile, it says Ahrefs’ Domain Rating ignores traffic and spam signals.</p>
<p>Use it to vet outreach targets before you pitch. Compare a prospect’s score with your own, because links from higher-scoring sites help most.</p>

<h2>Where Do the Free Semrush Tools Fall Short?</h2>
<p>Every limit above traces back to one cause. Snapshot Data answers what’s true today, while Stream Data answers what changed and why. Think of Stream Data as a stored series of snapshots that alerts you when numbers move.</p>
<p>My thesis is simple. The <a href="https://semrush.sjv.io/YV34jR">free Semrush tools</a> serve decisions you make once, and paid tools serve decisions you revisit weekly.</p>
<p>For example, take the AI Visibility Checker. You can run it three times a day. Starter tracks 50 prompts every day. That gap is the whole business model, and it’s a fair one.</p>
<p>Estimates also carry error. Authority Score isn’t a Google metric, and AI answers vary between runs. None of that makes the tools useless. It means you should log results with dates and compare trends, not single readings.</p>
<p>The 10-Report Ceiling disappears quickly. So run six keyword lookups and four domain checks, and your day ends.</p>

<h2>How Do You Run a Rank-Mention-Click Audit With Free Semrush Tools?</h2>
<p>A Rank-Mention-Click audit compares where you rank, whether AI names you, and whether AI sends visitors. The <a href="https://semrush.sjv.io/YV34jR">free Semrush tools</a> cover all three lenses inside their daily meters.</p>
<ol> <li>First, run the Keyword Rank Checker with your best keyword and URL. Note your position.</li> <li>Next, run the SERP Checker on the same keyword. Check the volatility chart and whether AI Overviews appear.</li> <li>Then run the AI Search Visibility Checker on your domain. Record the score and the top cited pages.</li> <li>After that, run the AI Traffic Checker on your domain. Record estimated AI visits and the leading platform.</li> <li>Run the Competitor Finder, then repeat steps three and four on your closest rival.</li> <li>Finally, log every number in a spreadsheet with the date. Repeat monthly.</li> </ol>
<p>That spreadsheet is homemade Stream Data. It’s clumsy, but it works.</p>
<p>Read the results through the Citation Gap. A Citation Gap is a page that ranks well in Google yet earns no AI mentions.</p>
<ul> <li>High rank and low mentions signals a Citation Gap. Google likes the page, but AI systems skip it.</li> <li>Low rank and high mentions means AI trusts a page Google buries. Protect it, then build its links.</li> <li>High mentions and low clicks means AI answers satisfy readers without a visit. Instead, offer something the answer can’t.</li> <li>Low scores everywhere call for keyword basics first.</li> </ul>

<h3>Do these free Semrush tools need an account?</h3>
<p>Most don’t. The AI Overviews Visibility Checker sends visitors into a signup flow. Several others link into the main app, where free account limits apply.</p>
<h3>Is Authority Score the same as Domain Authority?</h3>
<p>No. Authority Score is Semrush’s 1 to 100 metric. Moz owns Domain Authority, and Ahrefs owns Domain Rating. Each uses a different formula, so numbers differ across tools.</p>
<h3>How much do paid Semrush plans cost in 2026?</h3>
<p>Monthly prices on the pricing page are $139 for SEO, $199 for Starter, $299 for Pro+, and $549 for Advanced. Annual billing saves up to 17%.</p>
<h3>How long is the Semrush free trial?</h3>
<p><a href="https://semrush.sjv.io/Gb3Gy9">Semrush</a> advertises seven days and says you can cancel anytime. Read the billing FAQ on the pricing page before you enter a card.</p>

<p>Three times per day, without registering or creating an account. One report covers ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Google AI Mode.</p>
