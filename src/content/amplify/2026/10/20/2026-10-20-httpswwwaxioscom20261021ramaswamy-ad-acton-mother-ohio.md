<p>Vivek Ramaswamy's campaign for Ohio governor is pouring $4 million into a <a data-vars-click-url="https://youtu.be/IJRXA4SywEE?si=OE8GprL4JWIOArbz" data-vars-content-id="8212405a-a118-458e-8b8a-30907d87eef1" data-vars-event-category="story" data-vars-headline="Exclusive: Ramaswamy doubles down on explosive ad featuring rival's mother, despite backlash" data-vars-item="in_content_link" data-vars-link-text="bombshell ad" data-vars-sub-category="story" href="https://youtu.be/IJRXA4SywEE?si=OE8GprL4JWIOArbz">bombshell ad</a> in which his opponent's estranged mother attacks her daughter — doubling down on the message despite fierce bipartisan backlash, Axios has learned.</p>
<p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> Ramaswamy is gambling that the deeply personal attack by Amy Acton's mother — who Acton has said sided with her stepfather after he sexually assaulted Acton when she was 12 — will resonate with voters.</p>
<ul><li>After the ad began appearing Tuesday on social media, many fellow Republicans urged Ramaswamy to distance his campaign from it.</li><li>They warned it could backfire at a time when the controversy over the <a data-vars-click-url="https://www.axios.com/politics-policy/epstein-files" data-vars-content-id="8212405a-a118-458e-8b8a-30907d87eef1" data-vars-event-category="story" data-vars-headline="Exclusive: Ramaswamy doubles down on explosive ad featuring rival's mother, despite backlash" data-vars-item="in_content_link" data-vars-link-text="Epstein files" data-vars-sub-category="story" href="https://www.axios.com/politics-policy/epstein-files">Epstein files</a> has elevated the sexual abuse of children as a national campaign issue.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Driving the news:</strong> Ramaswamy's campaign confirmed to Axios that it produced the ad, which began circulating on social media late Monday without language identifying its sponsor.</p>
<ul><li>Sources close to Ramaswamy's campaign said the ad tested well with "persuadable" voters, prompting the decision to launch a $4 million statewide campaign that began streaming Tuesday night.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Catch up quick:</strong> The minute-long commercial features Acton's estranged mother, Donna Arthur, accusing her daughter of lying about her childhood and urging Ohioans not to vote for her.</p>
<ul><li>"She lies about her past," Arthur says. "She's lied her entire life."</li><li>Arthur also disputes Acton's account of experiencing homelessness as a child.</li></ul>
<p><strong>The other side:</strong> Acton, a physician, has said she was sexually abused at age 12 by her stepfather, who faced criminal charges, and that her mother sided with him.</p>
<ul><li>Acton and her brother were removed from their mother's custody. According to her brother, their mother remained married to Acton's alleged abuser until his death.</li><li>Acton, 60, has said she hasn't seen her mother since childhood.</li></ul>
<p><strong>In a <a data-vars-click-url="https://www.facebook.com/amyactonoh/" data-vars-content-id="8212405a-a118-458e-8b8a-30907d87eef1" data-vars-event-category="story" data-vars-headline="Exclusive: Ramaswamy doubles down on explosive ad featuring rival's mother, despite backlash" data-vars-item="in_content_link" data-vars-link-text="statement" data-vars-sub-category="story" href="https://www.facebook.com/amyactonoh/">statement</a> posted to Facebook</strong> on Tuesday, Acton said that "between the ages of 10 and 12, I was sexually abused by her husband, while my birth mother did nothing to protect me."</p>
<ul><li> "I've always known that she was deeply sick and unwell, and it's sad to see Vivek Ramaswamy exploit her all for his own political gain."</li><li>Acton's brother, Philip Stearns, called Ramaswamy's ad "disgusting."</li></ul>
<p><strong>What they're saying:</strong> Despite bipartisan criticism, Ramaswamy's campaign continued to treat the mother's statements as campaign gold.</p>
<ul><li>"This is a campaign-ending moment for Amy Acton. The people who know her best keep warning Ohioans about her," Ramaswamy campaign manager Jonathan Ewing told Axios.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Several prominent</strong> <strong>Republicans</strong> and conservative commentators blasted the ad, arguing that it could generate sympathy for Acton.</p>
<ul><li>Republican strategist David Urban on CNN drew a parallel to the Epstein controversy, warning that voters are especially sympathetic to victims of sexual assault and that the ad could backfire.</li><li>"It seems crazy to me one would assume this would work and not backfire," conservative commentator <a data-vars-click-url="https://x.com/mkhammer/status/2112497170083430890" data-vars-content-id="8212405a-a118-458e-8b8a-30907d87eef1" data-vars-event-category="story" data-vars-headline="Exclusive: Ramaswamy doubles down on explosive ad featuring rival's mother, despite backlash" data-vars-item="in_content_link" data-vars-link-text="Mary Katharine Ham" data-vars-sub-category="story" href="https://x.com/mkhammer/status/2112497170083430890">Mary Katharine Ham</a> posted on X.</li></ul>
<p><strong>Yes, but:</strong> Some conservatives defended the commercial, including Donald Trump Jr., who <a data-vars-click-url="https://x.com/DonaldJTrumpJr/status/2112566681210122462?s=20" data-vars-content-id="8212405a-a118-458e-8b8a-30907d87eef1" data-vars-event-category="story" data-vars-headline="Exclusive: Ramaswamy doubles down on explosive ad featuring rival's mother, despite backlash" data-vars-item="in_content_link" data-vars-link-text="urged" data-vars-sub-category="story" href="https://x.com/DonaldJTrumpJr/status/2112566681210122462?s=20">urged</a> Ohio voters to "watch [it] and think about what it means."</p>
<p><strong>By the numbers:</strong> A mid-October <a data-vars-click-url="https://maristpoll.marist.edu/polls/midterm-elections-in-ohio-october-2026/" data-vars-content-id="8212405a-a118-458e-8b8a-30907d87eef1" data-vars-event-category="story" data-vars-headline="Exclusive: Ramaswamy doubles down on explosive ad featuring rival's mother, despite backlash" data-vars-item="in_content_link" data-vars-link-text="Marist survey" data-vars-sub-category="story" href="https://maristpoll.marist.edu/polls/midterm-elections-in-ohio-october-2026/">Marist survey</a> showed Acton leading Ramaswamy 50% to 43% among likely voters.</p>
<p><strong>What's next: </strong>Ramaswamy and Republican Sen. Jon Husted are set to campaign with Vice President Vance in Cincinnati on Wednesday.</p>
