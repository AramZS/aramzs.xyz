<p>This article will help users install and use the Internet Archive’s smart phone app for determining which books we want to add to our library.</p>
<p>The app can be downloaded from the App Store <a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/donate-books/id1668658774">here</a>, or from the Play Store <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.archive.donatebooks">here</a>.</p>
<h1>Installing the App</h1>
<ol> <li>Open the App Store on the device you would like to install the app on</li> <li>Search for “Donate Books”</li> <li>Select the Internet Archive “Donate Books” app</li> <li>Click install</li> <li>Open the app</li> <li>Grant camera permissions</li> </ol>
<h2>Android</h2>
<ol> <li>Open the Play Store on the device you would like to install the app on</li> <li>Search for “Donate Books”</li> <li>Select the Internet Archive “Donate Books” app</li> <li>Click install</li> <li>Open the app</li> <li>Grant camera permissions</li> </ol>
<h1>Using the App</h1>
<h2>Barcode scanning</h2>
<ol> <li>Select the barcode scanning mode from the bottom of the screen</li> <li>Aim your phone’s front camera at a book barcode</li> <li>Wait for a response to appear on the screen</li> </ol>
<p>If your barcode is not scanning properly, tap your phone screen to refocus your camera. Keep the book on a flat surface while scanning to reduce the need for refocus.</p>
<h2>OCR-ing ISBNs</h2>
<ol> <li>Select the OCR mode from the bottom of the screen</li> <li>Aim your phone’s front camera at a printed ISBN</li> <li>Wait for a response to appear on the screen</li> </ol>
<p>If your ISBN is not scanning properly, tap your phone screen to refocus your camera. Keep the book on a flat surface while scanning to reduce the need for refocus.</p>
<h1>Found a bug? Need help?</h1>
<p>E-mail us at info@archive.org with the subject “Donate Books App”!</p>
<p>Reporting a bug? Please include what device you are using to access the app and operating system version in your message.</p>
<h1>Ready to donate books?</h1>
<p>If you will donate books we need for our library, then the Internet Archive might pay for shipping– please contact us.</p>
