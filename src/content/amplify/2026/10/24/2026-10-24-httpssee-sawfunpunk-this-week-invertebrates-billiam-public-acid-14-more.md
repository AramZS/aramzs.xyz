Ragdoll has dropped another essential tape, Billiam reaches new heights, MR. [REDACTED] follow their unbelievable 2025 tape, and Paper Jam get the K pressing. Kohti Tuhoa and SIKM have some essential ones, too! A wild week.</p>

<p><strong>Invertebrates: </strong><a href="https://beachimpedimentrecords.bandcamp.com/album/accosted?ref=see-saw.fun"><strong><em><u>Accosted</u></em></strong></a><strong> [Beach Impediment]</strong></p>
<p>Invertebrates’ <em>Accosted </em>is as tightly wound as a seven-song hardcore 7” can get, and somehow, a new best for the Richmond band that put out an all-timer with <em>Sick to Survive</em>. The individual performances are unbelievable—the rapidfire guitar on “One for the Road,” that speedball percussion that always sounds like it’s about to trip off a cliff on “Detached Aggression,” the solo in the title track—in service of a record that feels oddly limitless. “Derelict Destiny” has that shout-along “it’s too late for me” chorus that just has to feel exceptional to scream in the pit.</p>
<p>Invertebrates sprint, pivot, gallop, and scream through so many distinct possibilities—sudden shifts that blend together beautifully. <em>Accosted</em> will impress upon you, again and again, that the fix is in. The music’s claustrophobic and sweaty; street punk with stellar hooks and hardcore relentlessness. If you listen real close (and you should), you can hear someone say “that’s the one” at the end of the 52-second obliterator “Poison Path.” It definitely was.</p>
