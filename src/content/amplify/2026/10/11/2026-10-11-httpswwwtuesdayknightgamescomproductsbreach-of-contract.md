<p><strong>First Edition Compatible</strong></p>
<h3 data-mce-fragment="1">Let slip the dogs of corporate oppression with this complete guide to interstellar contract law, featuring immersive tear-away contract handouts to enhance your game with legal and economic horrors.</h3>
<h3 data-mce-fragment="1"><em data-mce-fragment="1">For Mothership 1st Edition.</em></h3>
<p><strong data-mce-fragment="1">Breach of Contract is the definitive authority on dystopian corporate law</strong> for the <em data-mce-fragment="1">Mothership Sci-fi Horror RPG.</em> This book will teach you how to think, act and negotiate like a megacorporation; helping you realize the Company’s influence over your campaign world, establishing a tangible and ruthless presence in the PCs’ lives.</p>
<h3 data-mce-fragment="1"><em data-mce-fragment="1">The law is dead, long live the contract.</em></h3>
<p>Alongside general counsel on legal concepts, tools and mechanics, award-winning RPG designer and practicing attorney Emily Weiss (<em data-mce-fragment="1">Picket Line Tango</em>) presents 8 in-universe and customizable player handout contracts: from loans and licenses to terms of employment. We enter into evidence the following examples:</p>
<ul data-mce-fragment="1"> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <strong data-mce-fragment="1">Legitimate Salvage Declaration:</strong> Regulating the means by which ships and their contents may be legally reappropriated from former owners. </li> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <strong data-mce-fragment="1">Death Indemnity Policy:</strong> Establishing insurance benefits in the event of a crewmate’s untimely demise. Eulogize PC deaths with fond remembrances as you attempt to cash in their policy. </li> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <strong data-mce-fragment="1">Artificial Intelligence End-User License Agreement:</strong> Covering terms of use for your ship’s friendly onboard AI, including disaster protocols in the highly unlikely event of an ethics deviation. </li> </ul>
<p>These legal materials inject dry bureaucratic proceedings and transactions with opportunities for engaging decisions, thematic encounters and open-ended challenges.</p>
<h3 data-mce-fragment="1"><em data-mce-fragment="1">We make paperwork fun!</em></h3>
<p>You still haven’t signed yet? We’re prepared to offer the following supplementary benefits:</p>
<ul data-mce-fragment="1"> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <strong data-mce-fragment="1">The new Lawyer class,</strong> capable of navigating contractual mazes and thriving in high-stress situations. </li> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <strong data-mce-fragment="1">Stacks of legal tables and generators </strong>including lawyer NPCs, legal urban legends, contract-signing rituals and more! </li> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <strong data-mce-fragment="1">A secret, mythical bonus contract</strong> which may hold the key to toppling megacorporations. </li> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <strong data-mce-fragment="1">Form-fillable contract PDFs.</strong> </li> <li data-mce-fragment="1"> <em data-mce-fragment="1">And more!</em> </li> </ul>
<p>While written as a fully standalone <em data-mce-fragment="1">Mothership</em> supplement, Breach of Contract also includes direct hooks and references to our adventure anthology <em data-mce-fragment="1">Hull Breach Vol. 1</em>, expanding its setting with pre-filled contract artifacts.</p>
<p>86 pages, 5.5” x 8.5”, B&amp;W, wire binding.</p>
<p>Fully hyperlinked, bookmarked and screen-reader ready PDF included.</p>
<p>Compatible with Mothership 1st Edition.</p>
