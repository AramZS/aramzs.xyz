<p><strong>First Edition Compatible</strong></p>
<p><strong><em>Gradient Descent</em></strong> is a sci-fi horror megadungeon set in an abandoned android factory controlled by a powerful rogue AI known as "MONARCH." Built from the ground up for the <em>Mothership®</em> sci-fi horror roleplaying game.</p>
<p>Written by Luke Gearing (<em>Fever Swamp, Acid Death Fantasy</em>) and illustrated by RPG newcomer, Nick Tofani, it includes:</p>
<ul> <li>The CLOUDBANK Synthetics Production Facility, AKA "The Deep," a 12 level, 200+ room megadungeon. Clean layout with an eye towards usability at the table, the Deep is one of the easiest to reference and run megadungeons on the market.</li> <li>The Bell, a small scavenged camp orbitting the Deep, where your players can rest and recuperate from adventuring. Contains a small crew of NPCs with their own unique rumors and agendas. </li> <li>A d100 table of powerful Artifacts created by Monarch, each with their own unique effects and potential hazards.</li> <li>Campaign ready! A section on Fallout gives guidance on the potential short and long term consequences your players may face as a result of their actions in The Deep.</li> <li>One shot accessible: Quick tips and tables make it easy to run a 1-3 hour "dive" into The Deep for a single night's play.</li> <li>Supports different playstyles: Everything in the Deep wants something and can be reckoned or bartered or negotiated with. Great for Faction play, social roleplayers, or players who just want a difficult series of combat encounters.</li> <li>A unique new condition called "The Bends" that will have your players questioning whether they are human.</li> </ul>
