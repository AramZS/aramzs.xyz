<p>Tech billionaire Peter Thiel is escalating his attacks on critics of artificial intelligence, accusing former President Barack Obama and the pope of exploiting the public’s fears about AI, according to exclusive recordings of closed-door lectures obtained by <a href="https://www.politico.com/newsletters/decoded-by-politico/2026/10/08/peter-thiels-new-antichrist-warnings-01111396">Decoded by POLITICO</a>.</p>
<p>In a new series of talks in Nashville, Thiel singled out the two leaders as he sharpened his warnings about the Antichrist — a figure who he sees as stopping the progress of technology and advocating for a “one world government” of regulation.</p>
<p>“Many people know that AI is politically unpopular and want to leverage that to boost their own popularity,” Thiel said. “Both Obama and the pope fall into this category. Obama knows the Democrats have no ideas, but they can at least oppose AI.”</p>
<p>The former president recently <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2026/09/13/obama-ai-democrats-fundraiser-01073778">urged Democrats to come up with a more comprehensive plan</a> on AI.</p>
