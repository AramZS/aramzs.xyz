(Tom McCorkle/For The Washington Post; Food styling by Gina Nistico)</figcaption></figure><p>When you need dinner on the table relatively quickly — tonight, perhaps? — soup might not be the first thing that comes to mind. Sure, a big pot simmering on the stovetop is just the thing for a leisurely winter afternoon, but there’s a world of soups that are speedy without skimping on flavor.</p><p>This <a href="https://www.washingtonpost.com/recipes/fasolada-greek-white-bean-soup/" rel="noreferrer" target="_blank" title="https://www.washingtonpost.com/recipes/fasolada-greek-white-bean-soup/">Fasolada (Greek White Bean Soup)</a> from cookbook author Shaily Lipa is a great example. While the soup is traditionally made with dried beans cooked from scratch, canned cannellini beans streamline the process here, meaning dinner is on the table in just 45 minutes, 20 of which are while the soup simmers.
