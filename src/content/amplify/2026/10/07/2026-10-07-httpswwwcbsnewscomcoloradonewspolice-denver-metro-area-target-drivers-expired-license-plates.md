title: Police in Denver metro area target drivers with expired license plates

<p>Police across the Denver metro area are targeting drivers with expired license plates and vehicle registration. Police in Lakewood posted an update on how the campaign is going, using a temporary tag that had been altered as an example of what not to do.</p>
<p>The department said agents pulled over a Hyundai Sonata that had a temp tag with a key part ripped out: the expiration year.</p>

<p>Police posted, "Spoiler alert... arts-and-craftsing your temp tag doesn't make it valid, and our officers are expert detectives when it comes to spotting a DIY redaction!"</p>
<p>The so-called Registration Roundup is happening throughout October, with officers targeting drivers with expired tags in Lakewood and other metro area cities.</p>
<p>Deputies with the Douglas County Sheriff's Office will wrap up "<a data-invalid-url-rewritten-http="" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/colorado/news/colorado-deputies-crack-down-on-expired-license-plates/">Operation Tag Sweep</a>" later this month, which is a two-week period where expired license plate registrations and tags are in the spotlight.</p>
