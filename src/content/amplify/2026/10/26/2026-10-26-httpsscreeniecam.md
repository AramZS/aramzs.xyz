<details><summary>What can I record?</summary><p>A whole display, one window, or an area you drag out, with your camera, your microphone and the sound your Mac plays, each one optional. Press ⇧⌘1 from anywhere to start and stop.</p></details>
<details><summary>Does it take screenshots too?</summary><p>Yes. Press ⇧⌘2 to capture a display, a window or an area, then annotate and frame it like a recording, and copy it or save a PNG.</p></details>
<details><summary>Which Macs does it run on?</summary><p>Screenie is a native Mac app for Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 15 Sequoia or later. On macOS 26 its controls use Liquid Glass.</p></details>
<details><summary>Does anything leave my Mac?</summary><p>No. There's no account, and nothing is uploaded: recordings, projects and exports are files on your Mac.</p></details>
<details><summary>Where are my projects kept?</summary><p>Each recording and screenshot is a .screenie file in Movies › Screenie, saved as you edit, with a preview in Finder. Move it, back it up or send it like any file.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I edit a video I didn't record with Screenie?</summary><p>Yes. Choose Import Video on the start window, then edit it like any recording.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I combine recordings?</summary><p>Yes. Click the + at the end of the timeline to add another project after this one, with its cuts, zooms and annotations. It takes on this project's look.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I change the cursor after recording?</summary><p>Yes. Screenie records where the pointer went and which cursor was showing, then redraws it, so you can smooth it, resize it or hide it later.</p></details>
