<p>CNN is bringing its All Access subscription service to the U.K. and Canada as part of a broader push to turn its international audience into paying subscribers.</p>
<p>The immediate goal is subscriber growth, while CNN also sees All Access as part of its wider revenue-diversification strategy, including a cross-platform advertising offer.</p>
