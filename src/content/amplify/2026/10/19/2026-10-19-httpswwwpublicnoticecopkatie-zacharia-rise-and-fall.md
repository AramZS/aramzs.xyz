<p>Zacharia’s meteoric rise and even more meteoric fall is not a surprise. Trump regularly hires lying, incompetent and/or corrupt minions, then fires them if they are women and defends them forever if they are men. (The only tidbit of news Zacharia actually broke was the ousting of comically corrupt Trump retainer Kimberly Guilfoyle from her position as ambassador to Greece.)</p>
<p>Trump hires bad people because he is a megalomaniac and a fool who hires people on one criteria only: sycophantic loyalty to Donald Trump (for women like Zacharia, it helps if you look vaguely like his daughter Ivanka). And anyone who is willing to demonstrate sycophantic loyalty to a lying incompetent fascist is going to be a lying incompetent fascist themselves.</p>
<h3>The making of a failed press secretary</h3>
<p>Zacharia appears to have been grown in a lab expressly to fail as Trump’s press secretary.</p>
<p>She received her undergraduate and law degree from Christian conservative Pepperdine University, and then pursued a career in various parts of the right-wing griftosphere. She served as a law clerk for Advocates International, a Christian law firm, and as a legal advisor to Fix California, a right-wing California org trying to push the state Trumpward. She’s also been a senior communications advisor at Trump’s far right propaganda social media site Truth Social.</p>

<p>As you’d expect with that resume, Zacharia is an unhinged liar, bigot, and hyperpartisan conspiracy theorist. As <a href="https://popular.info/p/new-white-house-press-secretary-is">reported</a> by Judd Legum at Public Information, she posted repeatedly on Truth Social to promote Pizzagate and QAnon, the dangerous lie that Hillary Clinton, her 2016 campaign chairman John Podesta, and other Democrats and celebrities were involved in a cult involving child sexual abuse, cannibalism, and Satantic rituals.</p>
<p>“Democrats want to mask and rape your children. That’s their satanic platform,” Zacharia fumed in April 2022.</p>
<p>She also has embraced the QAnon theory that Trump is the all-knowing One chosen to fight the Democratic cabal. She’s speculated that he sends secret codes to supporters through signs like drinking from water bottles.</p>
<p>Obviously, this is exactly the sort of bizarre, debased sycophancy that Trump adores. But there are lots of bizarre unqualified sycophants to choose from. Trump can’t appoint them all to be press secretary, even if they only last five days each. Zacharia needed an extra something.</p>
<p>That extra something was being a Newsmax commentator and a guest on Fox.</p>
<p>Politico <a href="https://www.politico.com/newsletters/playbook/2026/10/13/high-on-hopium-01116439">reports</a> that Zacharia paid attention to Trump’s public schedule and to his Truth Social posts and figured out when he was likely to be mainlining his daily does of self-flattering lies from the right-wing liar bubble. She made sure she was on air to serve him those lies personally. Trump liked what he saw, inasmuch as Zacharia was flattering him on air and was conventionally <a href="https://slate.com/news-and-politics/2016/12/trumps-hiring-people-based-partly-on-their-looks.html">attractive</a>, like former Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt and Christofascist warmongering Defense Secretary <a href="https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/trump-pete-hegseth-cabinet-meeting-b2984660.html">Pete Hegseth</a>. Other MAGA anchors and commentators vouched for Zacharia as well.</p>
<p>Ineffectual and largely invisible White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles was <a href="https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/trump-katie-zacharia-fired-wiles-analysis-b3068392.html">reportedly</a> trying to secure CNN blowhard Scott Jennings for the press sec role. (Politico <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/16/susie-wiles-helped-set-up-fox-interview-ahead-of-press-secretarys-exit-01125176">reported</a> over the weekend that Zacharia was actually set up for her hit interview with Hannity by Wiles.) But Trump was impatient and/or impulsive and/or just hated being told what to do. So he decided to follow his shriveled self-obsessed heart and throw Katie Zacharia out onto the global stage.</p>
<p>Where she immediately faceplanted.</p>
<h3>An epic implosion</h3>
<p>Zacharia conducted one press briefing. It lasted 14 minutes. She read awkwardly from her notes and took three questions, from Breitbart, the Daily Mail, and Fox, all right-wing pro-Trump outlets that were studiously non-confrontational. She also bumblingly referred to her husband as the president.</p>
<p>Joe Biden’s former Press Secretary Jen Psaki <a href="https://x.com/jrpsaki/status/2110009294531813817">summed</a> up the response: “Well that briefing was basically an SNL skit … except it wasn’t.”</p>
