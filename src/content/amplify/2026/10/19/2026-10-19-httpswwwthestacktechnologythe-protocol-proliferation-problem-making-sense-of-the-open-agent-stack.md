<p>Given that the point of an AI agent is to complete tasks, not just answer questions, it’s no surprise there’s a range of options for connecting agents to the tools and services that allow them to take actions. But with the launch this summer of yet another open agent protocol,<a href="https://agenticresourcediscovery.org/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>Agentic Resource Discovery</u></a>, it might seem like everyone is<a href="https://xkcd.com/927/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>coming up with their own standard</u></a>.</p>
<p>MCP<a href="https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-context-protocol?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>came from Anthropic</u></a>,<a href="https://developers.googleblog.com/en/a2a-a-new-era-of-agent-interoperability/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>A2A from Google</u></a>, and the ARD spec authors are from Microsoft, Google and Hugging Face, with contributions from Cisco, Databricks, GitHub, GoDaddy, Nvidia, Salesforce, ServiceNow and Snowflake. That free-for-all makes sense: controlling how services can be found can also influences which services are findable easily (or possibly at all).</p>
<p>There’s also ACP (IBM’s Agent Communication Protocol, not to be confused with the<a href="https://agentclientprotocol.com/get-started/introduction?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>Agent Client Protocol</u></a> that connects coding agents to IDEs, or with the<a href="https://www.agenticcommerce.dev/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>Agentic Commerce Protocol</u></a> from OpenAI and Stripe),<a href="https://agntcy.org/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>AGNTCY</u></a> (a Linux Foundation project originally from Cisco for agent discovery and identity that builds on the Open Agent Schema Framework), the<a href="https://agent-network-protocol.com/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>Agent Network Protocol</u></a> stack, and AGTP, an<a href="https://agtp.io/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>Agent Transport Protocol proposal</u></a> to move agent traffic away from the HTTP that all the other protocols rely on.</p>
<p>“It's early, so there's a land grab of different organisations trying to put their stake in the ground with some protocol,” Forrester principal analyst<a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/davidmooter/?ref=thestack.technology"> <u>David Mooter</u></a> told <em>The Stack</em>. “They’re all tackling the problems [in] different ways.”</p>
