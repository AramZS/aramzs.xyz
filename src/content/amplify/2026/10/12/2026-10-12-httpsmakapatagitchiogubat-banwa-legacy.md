<h1>SWORD AND ENLIGHTENMENT. LOVE AND GLORY. VIOLENCE AND LIBERATION. </h1>
<p><strong>GUBAT BANWA</strong> is a Martial Arts Tactics and War Drama Tabletop RPG where you play as martial artists poised to change the world: Kadungganan: the cavalry, the wandering swordsmen, the tide turners, the knights-errant, the ones to call in darkest night in a world inspired and centering Southeast Asian folklore.</p>
<p>Witness, grand warriors, honorable gallants that trudge and toil under kings and haloes. Witness, KADUNGGANAN, that refulgent name. That blasted name: WITNESS NOW. The end of days is upon us: and the new world MUST BE BORN. Bear your blades, incant your magicks. Cut open your tomorrow from the womb of violence. Inscribe your name upon the very akasha of this world.</p>
<h1>A unique Southeast Asian-inspired Fantasy setting. </h1>
<p>With an emphasis on martial arts as a background for war drama and violence, from a Philippine-centric view. Garudas fight against unglu. Martial artist warriors master the Principle of Cutting by meditating upon the teachings of the Violent Bodhisattva. Rituals and superstitions must be performed or else risk the wrath of the ancestors. Vast kingdoms arise with God-Kings at their helm, claiming to be Shiva-Buddha incarnate. You and your warband stand at the center of the mandala of this violence!</p>

<h1>The Thundering Tactics Battle System </h1>
<p><strong>TTB is combat system that fuses modern narrative sensibilities with D&amp;D4e style tactical combat.</strong></p>
<p>The Turn Order is known as the <strong>Rhythm, </strong>and you don't <strong>activate </strong>a unit; a fighter <em><strong>riffs</strong></em>. The game heavily relies on terrain abilities and emphasizes movement, especially with it's <strong>3-Beat System, </strong>which lets you do <strong>3 Actions per turn </strong>(some actions might cost more than 1 Beat!). Finally, the techniques you have are made to <strong>setup</strong> other allies, creating synergistic actions and situations!</p>
<p>Engage in <strong>Martial Arts Tactics</strong> by <em>Final Fantasy Tactics, Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together, Fire Emblem, and Dungeons and Dragons 4e, </em>placing great emphasis on <strong>team roles</strong>, <strong>ability</strong> <strong>synergy</strong>, <strong>positioning</strong>, the <strong>joy</strong> of <strong>movement</strong>, and <strong>eme</strong><strong>rgent</strong> <strong>strategy</strong>. It also boasts a huge amount of <strong>build variety </strong>for all those that wish to create interesting mechanical interactions!</p>
<h1>The Discipline System</h1>
<p><strong>A Class system inspired by Martial Arts, Final Fantasy Tactics Jobs, Digital Devil Saga's Mantras, and Shin Megami Tensei Nocturne's Magatama. </strong></p>
<p>Mix and match your <strong>Techniques</strong>! Change Disciplines with a single Downtime Action! Be a Death Dancer that heals with alchemies, or a Tigpana (Spiritual Archer) that rides upon a crocodile! Create exceedingly weird builds by mixing and matching your <strong>Enlightenments</strong><strong>(Passives)</strong> and <strong>Liberations</strong> <strong>(Ultimate</strong> <strong>Attacks)</strong> alongside <strong>3</strong> <strong>Anting</strong> (<strong>Magic</strong> <strong>Items that function like Powerful</strong> <strong>Feats!)</strong></p>
<h1>A Narrative System that generates drama and violence.</h1>
<p>A narrative system made to help play out <strong>war drama </strong>in this setting, along the veins of <em><strong>Final Fantasy Tactics, Hero, House of Flying Daggers, Malazan, Tigana, Game of Thrones, </strong></em><strong>and </strong><em><strong>Tactics Ogre!</strong></em> Manipulate others with Debt, persuade others with your Honor, or generate the power to defy fate by holding fast to your <strong>Passions,</strong> <strong>letting</strong> <strong>it</strong> entangle you even further, and deny death its reward by wielding your <strong>Conviction.</strong> The baked <strong>Honor </strong>system interplays with <strong>Debt,</strong> which is how you get others to do things for you. NPCs can accrue this same debt, forcing you to follow them. If your Honor ever falls below 0, you must play a new Kadungganan!</p>
<p>Describing or doing dangerous and cool things is encouraged, as that is how you get Thunderbolts, which let you dictate your fate. Thunderbolts are there to encourage Kadungganan to be as dramatic and over-the-top as possible.</p>
<p>It is <strong>war drama</strong> because it is is not about delving into dungeons, getting loot and money and getting stronger. It’s about being forced to kill your closest loved one in the middle of a blood-stained sea in the midst of a storm because they serve a datu that blasphemed your sultan. As a war-drama roleplaying game, expect an emphasis on relationships with other warriors, with your loved one, and intense interpolity politics with the other polities. Gubat Banwa expects you to play into these melodramas: sons having to kill their fathers for defecting to an enemy polity, daughters having to flee to the mountains to escape misogynistic responsibilities, loved ones killing each other slowly due to orders and/or emotions, best friends dueling at the top of a mountain in the midst of a storm as their ideologies bring them to a head.</p>
<p>This game is made for <strong>war stories</strong>, the likes one would see in Final Fantasy Tactics and Tactics Ogre. It’s about being forced to kill your closest loved one in the middle of a blood-stained sea in the midst of a storm because they serve a Datu that blasphemed your Sultan. War Drama narrative mechanics make sure that violence happens inevitably, in one way or another, all threads intertwining and coming to a head with tactical combat.</p>
<h1>Gambits</h1>
<p><strong>An enemy system that allows for Solo and GM-less play! </strong></p>
<p>Roll an Enemy's Gambit Dice to find out what actions they do when they Riff. Adapt and act accordingly, make for dynamic fights! You can play an entire game of Gubat Banwa on your own, and planned modules are being written with solo play in mind.</p>
<p>Event tables and generators for every possible thing you might encounter while journeying in the Sword Isles, to fully immerse and play in its cultures! As well as <strong>Lore</strong> to fulfill any questions you might have.</p>
