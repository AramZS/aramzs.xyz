<p>Many publishers execs say — despite the hype — agentic media buying remains a test-and-learn proposition, rather than a proven source of returns.</p>
<p>That caution was clear in Digiday’s conversations with publishers at Advertising Week New York last week, where on-stage discussions dusted across the topic. Sessions on agentic media, tools and marketing were peppered throughout the programming of the four-day event.</p>
