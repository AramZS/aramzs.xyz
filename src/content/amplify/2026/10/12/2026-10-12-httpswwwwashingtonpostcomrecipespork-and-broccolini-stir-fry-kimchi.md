<html><body><figure><img src="https://cloudfront-us-east-1.images.arcpublishing.com/wapo/ZQNWBIVF2MGCFKDQUWIWVFGXRY.jpg"/></figure><div><p>This quick stir-fry relies on kimchi to add complex umami flavor to pork and broccolini. </p><p>Serve with cooked brown rice.</p></div><p>Refrigerate for up to 3 days.</p><p>Kimchi can be found at well-stocked supermarkets and Asian markets.</p><ol><li>Whisk together the broth, cornstarch and soy sauce in a liquid measuring cup, until well combined.</li><li>Cut the pork crosswise into 1/4-inch-thick medallions, then cut each medallion in half. Sprinkle the pork with the salt. Set a large plate near your workspace.</li><li>Heat 1 tablespoon of the oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add half of the meat and cook, stirring once or twice until browned, about 2 minutes. Transfer the meat to the plate and repeat with another 1 tablespoon of the oil and the remaining pork.</li><li>Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of oil to the skillet or wok. Then add the onion and broccolini and stir-fry until slightly softened and charred in spots, about 3 minutes. Stir in the garlic and ginger and stir-fry until aromatic, 30 seconds more.</li><li>Re-whisk the broth mixture, and add it to the pan. Increase the heat to high and stir-fry until the mixture becomes a slightly thickened sauce. Return the pork with any accumulated juices to the pan, then add the kimchi and the gochujang or sriracha and stir-fry until just warmed through and the pork and broccolini are evenly coated in the sauce, about 2 minutes. Taste, and add more gochujang or sriracha, as needed.</li><li>Stir in the scallions and remove from the heat. Divide among shallow bowls, top with more scallions and serve hot, with rice.</li></ol><p>Pork tenderloin &gt;&gt; boneless, skinless, thinly cut chicken, or beef sirloin.
Vegan? &gt;&gt; Use firm or extra-firm cubed tofu and vegan kimchi.
