<p>I do not claim any rights on this collection. The games here are in the public domain.</p>
<p>(I collected them from many sources, mostly from the net. <a href="http://webdocs.cs.ualberta.ca/~mmueller/index.html">Martin Müller</a>'s <a href="http://webdocs.cs.ualberta.ca/~mmueller/go/games.html">collection</a> of games was a good start, and <a href="http://rongen17.home.xs4all.nl/">Jan van Rongen</a> had a few more recent games. Also ftp.nuri.net/Go had a lot of stuff, unfortunately mostly of low quality. Gerald Westhoff contributed lots of games from 1989-1990. Many other references contribute a handful of games. Many games have several sources, and where there were contradictions, I have attempted to decide which source was most reliable. Many minor details were corrected. The format has been made a bit more uniform. In particular, all dates are given in ISO standard form.)</p>
