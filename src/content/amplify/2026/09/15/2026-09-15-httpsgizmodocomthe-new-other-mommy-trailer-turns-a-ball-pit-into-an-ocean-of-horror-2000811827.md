<p>The <a href="https://gizmodo.com/other-mommy-trailer-jessica-chastain-2000788493">first trailer for <em>Other Mommy</em></a> was totally unnerving, giving us a peek at <a href="https://gizmodo.com/director-rob-savage-on-how-other-mommy-is-like-obsession-2000804486">Jessica Chastain’s dual role</a> as a regular human mother—and a creature who exactly resembles a regular human mother, except for her tendency toward very creepy behavior. This new trailer, which leans into the film’s behind-the-scenes connections to <a href="https://gizmodo.com/the-success-of-backrooms-and-obsession-might-be-a-historic-moment-in-film-history-2000765819"><em>Obsession</em> and <em>Backrooms</em></a>, doubles down on the parental freak show.</p>
<p>Two words: bubble bath. Two more words: ball pit. Watch, and be glad you’re not a little kid realizing for the first time that adults aren’t always who they say they are.</p>
<figure><img alt="" src="https://i.ytimg.com/vi/avXdGfYmRhM/hqdefault.jpg"/></figure>
<figure><img src="https://gizmodo.com/app/plugins/wp-rocket/assets/img/youtube.png"/></figure>
<p>Unlike 2026 horror sensations <em>Obsession</em> and <em>Backrooms</em>, the James Wan-produced <em>Other Mommy</em> has literary origins; it’s based on the book <em>Incidents Around the House</em> by Josh Malerman, who also wrote <em>Bird Box</em>. Rob Savage, whose previous horror credits include <em>Host</em> and <em>The Boogeyman</em>, directs the Oscar-winning Chastain, with Arabella Olivia Clark <em>(The Housemaid)</em> playing the girl who realizes her mom’s doppelganger isn’t so “nice” after all. In fact, she has deeply malevolent intentions, and she’s not afraid to crash a kiddie play zone to try and get what she wants.</p>
<p>The rest of the cast includes Jay Duplass, Dichen Lachman (<i>Severance</i>), Arian Moayed, and Karen Allen (the <i>Indiana Jones</i> films).</p>
<p><em>Other Mommy</em> arrives in theaters October 9. Will you be checking out this tale of smotherly love?</p>
