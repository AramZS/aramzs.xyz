<p>In 2020, Mariana Mazzucato cochaired a Renewal Commission, which gave residents of the London Borough of Camden an opportunity to shape priorities for local government. One institutional innovation that came out of it was a £30 million ($40.4 million US) Camden Community Wealth Fund, which offers loans, equity, and support to local enterprises, with residents helping to decide where the money should go. It’s the kind of institution her new book is written to celebrate: democratically governed public wealth used to reshape the local economy toward more inclusive social outcomes.</p>
