<p>(WJW) — The U.S. Department of Agriculture has announced a <a href="https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/star-meat-delivery-inc--recalls-raw-pork-beef-and-goat-products-produced-without">massive meat recall</a> on more than 167,000 pounds of of raw pork, beef, and goat products that officials said were produced without the benefit of federal inspection.</p>
<p>According to the <a href="https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/star-meat-delivery-inc--recalls-raw-pork-beef-and-goat-products-produced-without">nationwide recall alert</a>, Star Meat Delivery Inc., located in Lucama, N.C., is recalling the meat, which officials said is labeled with a false USDA mark of inspection.</p>
<p>“Food produced without inspection may contain undeclared allergens, harmful bacteria, or other contaminants that put consumer health and safety at risk,” warned USDA’s Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS.) “FSIS is concerned that some product may be in the refrigerators or freezers of consumers, restaurants, or retailers.”</p>
<p>Consumers who have purchased the recalled products are urged not to eat them. The products should be thrown away or returned to the place of purchase.</p>
<p>Here’s what to check for, according to the USDA:</p>
<ul>
<li>Any product bearing the false establishment number “EST. 1363” should be considered misbranded and unsafe to eat.</li>
<li>The raw pork, beef, and goat items were produced on various dates prior to September 22, 2026.</li>
<li>The products were vacuum-sealed and packed into cardboard boxes for shipment; individual consumer packages may not bear this labeling, as retail stores may have applied their own stickers with pricing and safe handling instructions.</li>
</ul>
<p>The FSIS said the problem was discovered during “surveillance activities.”</p>
<p>FSIS notes that as more information becomes available, the product list may be updated to include more items.</p>
<p>Currently, consumers can <a href="https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/star-meat-delivery-inc--recalls-raw-pork-beef-and-goat-products-produced-without">visit the FSIS website</a> for a complete list of recalled products, and <a href="https://www.fsis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/food_label_pdf/2026-09/Recall-022-2026-Labels.pdf">to view</a> available labels of the impacted products.</p>
<p>So far, there have been no confirmed reports of illness. Anyone concerned about an illness is encouraged to contact a healthcare provider.</p>
