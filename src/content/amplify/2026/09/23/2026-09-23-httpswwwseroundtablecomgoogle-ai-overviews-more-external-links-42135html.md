<p>New data shows that Google is showing far more external links in its AI Overviews than before. A jump from close to 0% of AI Overviews containing external links to over 25% of AI Overviews containing external links.</p>
<p>Malte Landwehr posted on <a href="https://x.com/MalteLandwehr/status/2101973324507062621">X</a> data from his company, Peec AI, and wrote, "Google massively increased the number of external links within AI Overview answers."</p>
<p>Here is his chart that shows it went from almost 0% of AI Overviews containing external links to over 25% containing external links:</p>
<figure><a href="https://images.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-external-links-QJG9HrcS.jpg"><img alt="Google Ai Overviews External Links" src="https://images.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-external-links-QJG9HrcS.jpg"/></a><figcaption><a href="https://images.seroundtable.com/google-ai-overviews-external-links-QJG9HrcS.jpg">Google Ai Overviews External Links</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>This doesn't "feel" right to me but I think I should share this data point, because all I do now is share how Google is less likely to link out these days and this shows the opposite.</p>
