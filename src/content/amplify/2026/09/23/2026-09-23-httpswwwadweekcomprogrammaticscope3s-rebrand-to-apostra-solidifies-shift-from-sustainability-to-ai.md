<p>ADWEEK House: Advertising HQ brings the people shaping advertising, commerce, and media together for four days of unscripted conversations and after-hours connections.<a href="https://event.adweek.com/advertising-hq-2026?ref=articlebox"><strong> Meet us in Midtown, Oct. 5–8.</strong></a></p>
<p>Scope3, which launched in 2022 to measure carbon emissions across the ad supply chain, is rebranding today as Apostra to reflect its AI focus.</p>
<p>The name shares a Greek root with “apostrophe”—apo (“away”) and strephein (“to turn”). It encapsulates CEO Brian O’Kelley’s focus: ushering his business—and the advertising industry—toward a more efficient future ruled by AI.</p>
<p>“Adtech spent 20 years, 25 years, making things really complicated,” he said, “and simplifying it would have a significant impact.”</p>
<p>Already, Apostra’s agentic platform is handling automated media buys in the U.S., the U.K., Brazil, France, and some other markets for some “very large” brands totaling around $1.2</p>
