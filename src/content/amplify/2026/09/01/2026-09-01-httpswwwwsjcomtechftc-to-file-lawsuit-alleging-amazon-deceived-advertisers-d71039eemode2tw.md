<h1>FTC Sues Amazon, Alleging It Deceived Advertisers and Drove Up Prices</h1>
<h2>Commission and more than 20 states allege e-commerce giant reaped billions by manipulating ad prices on its retail platform</h2>
<p>By</p>
<p><a href="https://www.wsj.com/news/author/dave-michaels">Dave Michaels</a>, <a href="https://www.wsj.com/news/author/isabella-simonetti">Isabella Simonetti</a> and <a href="https://www.wsj.com/news/author/suzanne-vranica">Suzanne Vranica</a></p>
<figure>
<img alt="Amazon facility in Queens, New York, with cars parked along the street on Prime Day." src="https://images.wsj.net/im-70203101?width=700&amp;height=466"/>
<figcaption>Amazon operates the third-largest digital-ad platform in the world.Klaus Galiano/Bloomberg News</figcaption>
</figure>
<p>WASHINGTON—The Federal Trade Commission on Monday filed a lawsuit against <a href="https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/AMZN">Amazon.com</a> alleging that the <a href="https://www.wsj.com/topics/industry/e-commerce">e-commerce</a> giant manipulated prices businesses paid to advertise on its retail platform, reaping tens of billions of dollars for itself over seven years.</p>
<p>The lawsuit, joined by a bipartisan group of more than 20 state attorneys general, alleged that Amazon deceived advertisers by secretly raising the minimum price advertisers had to pay to place ads promoting their products.</p>
