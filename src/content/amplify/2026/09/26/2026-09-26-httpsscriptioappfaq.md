<h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
<details><summary>Is Scriptio free to use?</summary><p>Scriptio is free to use for most of its features. No project limitation, no forced watermark on PDF generation. Only advanced features such as real-time collaboration and report generation are part of our Pro plan, which is available for a reasonable monthly fee.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I use Scriptio offline?</summary><p>Absolutely. The desktop app works fully offline. When you sign in and reconnect, your changes sync automatically to the cloud.</p></details>
<details><summary>What export formats are supported?</summary><p>You can export your screenplays as PDF, FDX (Final Draft), and Fountain. You can also import from these formats.</p></details>
<details><summary>How does real-time collaboration work?</summary><p>You can invite up to 5 collaborators to a project. Everyone edits the same screenplay simultaneously with changes appearing in real time.</p></details>
<details><summary>Is my data safe?</summary><p>Your data is hosted entirely on European servers, passwords are securely hashed, and all communications are encrypted with TLS. We never sell or share your data. Read more in our <a href="https://scriptio.app/privacy">privacy policy</a>.</p></details>
