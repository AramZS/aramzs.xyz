<figure><img alt="Macklemore’s “Free Palestine” tour sells out immediately" src="https://www.pastemagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/30/GettyImages-2153049908_cropped.jpg"/></figure>
<p>Macklemore is getting the last laugh in the Ed Sheeran debacle, as his <a href="https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/macklemore/macklemore-announces-a-free-palestine-tour-in-the-uk-and-eu">“Free Palestine” tour</a> sold out, while the “Shape of You” crooner struggles to sell tickets.</p>
<p>In an Instagram post shared today (September 30), the rapper celebrated the news. “All three shows sold out immediately,” he wrote. “I’m honestly blown away. We put these shows together fast and in community, believing that musicians can stand in solidarity with Palestine, use the stage to raise funds and show what collective action can look like. When those in power try to silence our calls for liberation, art will always be a form of resistance.”</p>
<p>He then thanked his fans in Dublin, Paris, and London for “showing up the way you did.” He added, “The response to these shows is another reminder of how many people across the world are standing together for a free Palestine.”</p>

<p>Macklemore also teased that the lineup will be shared soon. While none of the supporting names have been confirmed yet, fans have suggested the inclusion of Sheeran’s other openers, who <a href="https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/ed-sheeran/support-acts-and-backing-band-pull-out-of-ed-sheerans-tour-following-macklemores-removal">quit the Loop tour in solidarity with Macklemore.</a> The three shows are being held next month, hitting Dublin on October 26th, Paris on the 29th, and London on the 30th. All proceeds will benefit organizations supporting Palestine and the Palestinian movement.</p>
<p>Ironically, after Sheeran decided to boot Macklemore off his tour following an ultimatum from Robert Kraft due to the “Thrift Shop” artist’s speech about Palestine at MetLife Stadium, the crooner’s <a href="https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/ed-sheeran/ed-sheerans-gillette-stadium-shows-cancelled">performance at the Kraft-owned Gillette Stadium was canceled because of severe weather</a>.</p>
<p>Stadium and tour management, along with public safety officials, have been closely monitoring the weather forecast over the past week. Due to the severe weather forecast throughout the weekend, the tour has made the difficult decision to cancel the Ed Sheeran concerts at Gillette Stadium,” read a statement shared on the official Instagram account for Sheeran’s tour promoter, Messina Touring. Sheeran has also seen a dip in sales following the controversy, with thousands of seats still available.</p>
