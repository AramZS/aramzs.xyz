<h2>Your newsletter's archive links can now share as rich, branded cards on Bluesky.</h2>
<figure><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="https://buttondown.com/next-assets/img/blog/bluesky-enhanced-cards/featuredImage-dark.png"/><img alt="Enhanced link cards on Bluesky" src="https://buttondown.com/next-assets/img/blog/bluesky-enhanced-cards/featuredImage.png"/></picture></figure>
<p>When you share a Buttondown archive link on Bluesky, it can now render as an enhanced link card carrying your publication's name, avatar, and a themed cover, instead of the plain title-and-snippet Bluesky shows by default.</p>
<p>This is powered by <a href="https://standard.site">standard.site</a>, a community-built standard for describing publications on the AT Protocol (the network underneath Bluesky). Buttondown publishes a small record to your own Bluesky account describing your publication, plus one for each publicly-archived email — and Bluesky uses those to render the nicer card.</p>
<p>To turn it on, head to <strong>Settings → Integrations</strong>, open the <a href="https://docs.buttondown.com/bsky">Bluesky integration</a>, and click <strong>Create publication</strong> under "Enhanced link cards." From then on, both the links Buttondown <a href="https://docs.buttondown.com/bsky#sharing-your-newsletters-to-bluesky">auto-posts to Bluesky</a> and the ones you (or your readers) paste in by hand will render as enhanced cards.</p>
<p>A couple of things worth knowing:</p>
<ul><li>You'll need your Bluesky account connected and your archive set to public — enhanced cards describe your public archive, so they only apply to emails that are publicly archived.</li><li>Bluesky pins a card's contents when it's posted, so this applies to <em>future</em> posts and shares. Links you've already shared keep the card they had.</li><li>The records live in your own Bluesky account and carry only enough to identify each article and link back to it — never your email's content. Disconnect any time and the cards you've already posted keep working.</li></ul>
<p>Check out the <a href="https://docs.buttondown.com/bsky#enhanced-link-cards">full documentation</a> for the details. Happy posting!</p>
