title: 'Trump Says He Has Banned CNN, MS NOW and Politico From White House'
---
<p>President Trump said on Friday that he would ban journalists from CNN, MS NOW and Politico from reporting at the White House.</p><p>Mr. Trump published his abrupt proclamation, which he said was “effective immediately,” in <a href="https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/posts/117293599348325006">a post</a> on Truth Social, the social media platform he owns.</p><p>The president wrote that his move was “a result of their constant ‘reporting’ FAKE NEWS!,” repeating one of his most common complaints about independent news outlets.</p><p>It was not clear how such a ban might be enforced, or whether it could pass muster in the event of a legal challenge. </p><p>The White House did not immediately respond to a request for comment about Mr. Trump’s post. Representatives of the three news organizations mentioned by Mr. Trump also did not immediately respond to a request for comment.</p><p>Mr. Trump has tried to bar some news organizations from parts of the White House in the past. During his first term, he revoked a White House pass for Jim Acosta, the former CNN journalist. CNN succeeded in getting that ban overturned in court.</p><p><em>This is a developing story.
