<p>Most electoral campaigns in the US show up every few years asking for your vote, your money, and your trust. Then they disappear.</p>
<p>Kat Abughazaleh tried something different. <a href="https://www.katforillinois.com/">During her congressional campaign in IL-09</a>, she invested in the community by using resources to feed people, host accessible events, and turn the campaign office into a space that actually served the people it hoped to represent.</p>
<p><strong>Now we’re building on that idea. KAPOW scales a model where campaigns don’t just take from communities, </strong><strong>they invest in them.</strong></p>
