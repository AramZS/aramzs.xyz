<h3> Twan Van Loon </h3>
<p>For this week's vlog I sat down with DJ / producer Mark Sherry, to talk with him about the track "Operation Blade (Bass In The Place...)"; a hard trance / acid classic Mark did back in 2000 under the project name "Public Domain". You'll hear how the track was born, what happened after the release, some crazy stuff that happened on tour and much more!</p>
<p>And we spoke as well about Mark's debut rtist album "Confirm Humanity" will be out soon via his label Outburst Records. And you will hear more about Gentech as well, which is a project from Mark together with Frank Zenker a.k.a. Scot Project!</p>
<p>Enjoy and thanks for watching!</p>
