<p>Once upon a time in the early 2000s, when I was very young and more impressionable than I realized at the time, every single fucking teen movie was about An Ugly (Glasses-Wearing) Girl Who Is Finally Made Pretty (Glasses Removed) and Then Noticed By That Guy. <em>She’s All That</em>, <em>Never Been Kissed</em>, <em>Ever After,</em> <em>13 Going on 30, The Princess Diaries: </em>for a long while there, every rom-com-esque film aimed at teens had to contain a makeover scene, where the ugly (or rather, near-sighted) girl went to the mall, was hairsprayed to the gods, and eventually, after many plot twists and turns and at least one red flag large enough to be seen from afar without squinting even with the spectacles off, found true love.</p>
<p>And then, into my own dreary (glasses-wearing) teenage years entered one single film, my own Prince Charming of a teen movie: 1999’s <em>Ten Things I Hate About You, </em>a sparky, bizarre, hilarious retelling of <em>the Taming of the Shrew. </em></p>
