<p>What does the real climate footprint of the biggest company on the planet look like? It’s a good question, but here’s a better one: why don’t we already know the answer?</p>
<p>In a new report, we find NVIDIA, a beneficiary of the AI boom, has seen its supply chain emissions grow 725% since 2020. But we also find that the emissions from the use of its products isn’t disclosed; despite the company supplying potential carbon bomb sites (like the biggest proposed gas plant on the planet). With that in mind, we produced our own conservative estimate, finding that the climate impact from 2025’s use of NVIDIA’S AI products is on par with the state-owned Russian Coal company.</p>
<p>Despite these eye-watering estimates, NVIDIA claims there’s a “consensus” that AI will be a net benefit for climate, and that energy efficiency and demand flexibility can deal with impacts and help the energy transition. Continuing the group’s work on AI greenwashing narratives, we explore and contest all these claims, which we contend are not supported by strong evidence.</p>
<p>In this new investigation by a consortium of eight climate, corporate accountability and digital campaigining groups, we build on previous work and publish a deep dive into the climate claims and climate impacts of the company that sits smack in the middle of one of the biggest new threats to fossil phase-out this decade.</p>
<p><strong><em>NVIDIA was contacted for comment but did not provide a response before deadline.</em></strong></p>
