<p>Allo.<a href="https://allo.joshpuckett.me/#download">Get the font↓</a></p>
<p>Allo is your friendly neighborhood typeface.</p>
<p>It’s variable in weight and roundness, with proportions and curves designed to feel warm without being too cute and clear without being too cold. It’s a perfect companion out of the box, and three variable axes and plenty of alternates let you tune its personality to exactly what you need.</p>
<p>Allo is optimized for screens and interfaces, with an optical size that adjusts automatically between text and display. And it’s packed with big little details: tabular figures, fractions, punctuation that lifts beside capitals, arrows that appear when you type them, a host of alternates and symbols, as well as a true oblique in every weight.</p>

<p>A friendly, free, and open source font family.</p>

<p>Allo began from <a href="https://rsms.me/inter/">Inter</a> 4.1, which provided an incredible base to work from. Every one of Allo’s glyphs has been redrawn, with extensive work on spacing, kerning, features and axes, all tuned for the friendly character that makes Allo its own. I owe a huge debt of gratitude to my friend <a href="https://rsms.me">Rasmus Andersson</a> for creating Inter and releasing it under an open license; without it, Allo would not exist.</p>
<p>Allo also pays homage to Honk Sans by <a href="https://sebmclauchlan.com/">Seb McLauchlan</a> and aims to bring some of that same warmth and fun to everyone.</p>
