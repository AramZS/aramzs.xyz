<p>Tech Policy Press fellow <strong>Lam Le</strong>, who's based in Hanoi, recently attended a conference in Bangkok called the Second Regional Networking Visit for Platform Workers in Asia, which was organized by the Just Economy and Labor Institute (JELI) and Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung (FES) Thailand. At the conference, she had the opportunity to meet workers and organizers and observe their discussions.</p>
<p>In this episode, she speaks with <strong>Justin Hendrix</strong> about what she learned, and shares recordings of delivery riders, remote outsourcing workers, artists, trade unionists and researchers from India, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand, who describe how they are organizing to win recognition and labor rights for platform workers.</p>
