<p>A couple of weeks ago, Google announced <a href="https://www.seroundtable.com/google-expands-ads-ai-mode-42412.html">Multiturn ads</a>, ads show up on responses to follow-up user queries. Well, those ads seem live. I was testing AI Mode on Friday afternoon and here are screenshots and videos of Multiturn ads in AI Mode.</p>
<p>As a reminder, Google told me that "Multiturn ads show up on responses to follow-up user queries as users engage in back-and-forth conversations with AI Mode." Google also said, "Ads will be clearly labeled as 'Sponsored.' We’ll continue testing new formats to ensure ads remain helpful and relevant."</p>
<p>So here is a full-size screenshot of ads not just in the first query but in the follow-up query. You'll see at the bottom of the results, there are ads labeled "Sponsored," twice.</p>
