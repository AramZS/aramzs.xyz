<p>The Trump administration’s taxpayer-funded <a href="https://www.ms.now/news/news-analysis/state-department-grants-human-rights-communism-sarah-rogers-trump">sponsorship of far-right foreign groups hostile to multiracial democracy</a> is getting some congressional scrutiny following Democrats’ blue wave of midterm wins. And Democrats aren’t waiting until January, when they take control of Congress, to get the ball rolling.</p>
<p>Maine Rep. Chellie Pingree, the top Democrat on the House Appropriations Subcommittee on Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies, is demanding answers from the National Endowment for the Humanities — an organization Trump has eviscerated, laying off <a href="https://www.artnews.com/art-news/news/neh-staff-layoffs-trump-administration-1234745069/">two-thirds of its staff</a> — about its role in hosting a secretive conference in Rome. The inquiry follows <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/16/arts/national-endowment-humanities-rome-conference.html">a New York Times report</a> that the NEH organized a postmidterm confab featuring a bunch of illiberal critics of democracy from around the world.</p>
<p>Pingree <a href="https://pingree.house.gov/uploadedfiles/letter_to_neh_on_rome_conference_final.pdf">sent a letter</a> Tuesday to NEH Chair Michael McDonald, demanding documents and other details on the conference.</p>
<p>From the letter:</p>
<blockquote> <p>According to The New York Times, the two-day conference featured nearly a dozen Anglophone academics, most of them conservative critics of universities, modern secular liberal democracy, or both. Speakers also included the president of a Budapest think tank closely aligned with former Hungarian President Viktor Orbán. Recordings of the panels were posted on an Italian government website, yet NEH chose not to publicize the conference in advance or while it was happening.</p> </blockquote>
<p>“The fact that NEH has not highlighted this conference itself raises even more questions about whether you would prefer this event was kept under the radar,” Pingree wrote. “But such a seemingly exorbitant trip requires transparency and accountability to the American taxpayer and justification for its purpose.”</p>
<p>The letter cited <a href="https://www.cbsnews.com/news/supreme-court-trump-dei-research-grants/">axed research grants</a> and <a href="https://www.artnews.com/art-news/news/neh-staff-layoffs-trump-administration-1234745069/">draconian staff cuts</a> imposed on the agency under the guise of “efficiency,” with purported cost-saving measures that seem at odds with shelling out tax dollars for a rendezvous at a Roman palazzo.</p>
<p>The NEH, the nation’s largest funder of humanities projects, didn’t respond to the Times’ request for comment on the gathering, reportedly titled “Classical Foundations and Moral Authority.”</p>
<p>The Times reported that McDonald and the U.S. ambassador to the Holy See both suggested the West was under attack following Democrats’ recent midterm victories. Among those who reportedly attended were writer R.R. Reno, who has <a href="https://firstthings.com/ferguson-missouri/">argued</a> that police are right to focus their attention on young Black men; Patrick Deneen, a <a href="https://www.ms.now/opinion/dhs-trump-vance-catholicism-immigration">conservative writer who authored a book titled “Why Liberalism Failed”</a> and has defended <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2022/05/13/podcasts/transcript-ezra-klein-interviews-patrick-deneen.html">making it harder for women to leave toxic marriages</a>; and John O’Sullivan, the British president of the Danube Institute, a far-right think tank previously propped up by former Hungarian strongman Viktor Orban. The institute is <a href="https://www.thedriftmag.com/the-last-days-of-the-danube-institute/">in retreat</a> as part of Hungary’s latest anti-corruption efforts, but officials have<a href="https://globalextremism.org/post/orbans-danube-institute-launches-a-nonprofit-arm-in-the-us/"> launched a nonprofit arm of the group in the U.S.</a></p>
<p>These are not people our government should be spending American tax dollars to meet with and to platform. And Pingree’s probe is an important step toward tamping down the Trump administration’s push to spread its antidemocratic ideals worldwide.</p>
