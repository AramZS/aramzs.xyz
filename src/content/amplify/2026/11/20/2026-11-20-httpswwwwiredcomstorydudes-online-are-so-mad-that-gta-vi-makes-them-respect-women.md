Courtesy of Rockstar Games</figcaption></figure><div class="body__inner-container"><p>The <em>Grand Theft Auto</em> <a class="text link" href="https://www.wired.com/story/13-years-of-anticipation-deserves-a-day-off-the-gta-vi-work-blackout-is-here/">games</a> never actually taught someone how to knock over a bank or survive a shootout with police, but the latest storyline in the <a class="text link" href="https://www.wired.com/story/i-will-never-stop-playing-grand-theft-auto/">franchise</a> serves up some valuable lessons about what it means to be in a loving and committed relationship.</p><p class="paywall">This isn’t sitting well with a histrionic minority of right-wing gamers who have been warning for months that <em>GTA VI</em> would be intolerably “woke.” While their attempt at a <a class="text link" href="https://www.wired.com/story/the-anti-woke-right-cant-figure-out-how-to-stop-grand-theft-auto-vi/">manufactured backlash</a> is going even more poorly than the one that greeted Christopher Nolan’s film <em>The Odyssey</em> this summer (the day of its official release, the game is already closing in on 20 million copies sold and <a class="external-link text link" href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/conormurray/2026/11/19/grand-theft-auto-vi-passes-18-billion-in-revenue-on-release-day-eclipses-the-odysseys-box-office-total/?streamIndex=0">$2 billion in revenue</a>), these streamers and commentators are throwing tantrums about gender politics anyway.</p><div></div><div></div><p class="paywall">“RIP GTA,” one X user somberly <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/trackthedecline/status/2123190035306680611">concluded</a>. “Killed by women.”</p><p class="paywall">In case you’re among the handful of skeptics who didn’t launch their single-player campaign in <em>GTA VI</em> at midnight last night after 13 years of desperate anticipation: This latest installment in the satirical crime-caper series is led by dual protagonists, Jason and Lucia, a couple whose meet-cute involves them both trying to jack the same car. Living together in a fictionalized version of South Florida, they look to get by on the wrong side of the law—but the player must also negotiate their emotional bond through domestic chores and acts of affection.</p><div class="Container-ioqmpI jzsvml"></div><p class="paywall">“This game is such woke slop,” <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/ExoGhost/status/2123042449006182538">wrote</a> one blue-check user on X who has posted almost nothing besides negative reactions to the game for the past week. The post included a screenshot of a fully optional <em>GTA VI</em> mission that describes how the Lucia character will appreciate a flower delivery and indicates with a heart icon that the action will strengthen feelings between her and Jason.</p><p class="paywall">Other choices you can make as Jason to get closer to Lucia (sex is very much on the table) include picking up groceries, getting a haircut or beard trim, gifting her a piece of jewelry, and <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/scubaryan_/status/2123107492762620281">repainting walls</a> in the house.</p><p class="paywall">The concept of role-playing as a nice and non-performatively masculine boyfriend has come as a rude shock to those more interested in explosions and mayhem. The right-wing streamer Zack Hoyt, known online as Asmongold, was dumbfounded to find himself unclogging a sink in a scene where Jason is trying to collect back rent from a tenant who refuses the money until the plumbing is fixed. “I actually have to fucking fix this bitch’s sink?” Hoyt <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/hddndoors/status/2123146581071593629?s=20">asked</a> on stream. “Why can’t she do this?” Elsewhere, when running errands for Lucia as Jason, Hoyt <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/AutisticClip/status/2123171739416883616">referred</a> to <em>GTA VI</em> as a “cuck simulator.” (That Hoyt thinks caring for a romantic partner makes someone a cuckold is perhaps an unfortunate glimpse into his dating history.)</p></div><div class="body__inner-container"><p class="paywall">More frustrated responses to the massively popular <em>GTA VI</em> came from looksmaxxing influencer Braden Peters, aka Clavicular, (the game required too much “simping for girls,” the streamer, currently facing <a class="external-link text link" href="https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/looksmaxxing-influencer-clavicular-faces-rape-charges-in-massachusetts">multiple rape charges</a>, <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/clavicular/status/2123269957974962526">complained</a> on X), anti-woke gamer activist Mark Kern, aka Grummz (he <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/Grummz/status/2123200131634016450">called</a> the housework “mundane crap”), and misinformation superspreader Dom Lucre, who falsely <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/dom_lucre/status/2123158366994514303">claimed</a> that Rockstar had specifically made it impossible to open fire in a gay nightclub. (Washington Post critic Gene Park <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/GenePark/status/2123182587606884608">pointed out</a> that the club in question was not LGBTQ-specific and that the mechanics of the game don’t allow you to shoot anyone in any kind of club.) These comments came alongside the <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/NikkiMcR/status/2123500128212226105">predictable gripes</a> over supposedly “overweight” female characters with insufficiently large breasts.</p><p class="paywall">And Andrew Tate himself denounced the game in a Thursday email blast to his newsletter subscribers. “The establishment does not understand the male spirit,” he wrote from jail in Miami, where he and his brother Tristan Tate are fighting extradition to the UK on charges of rape, human trafficking, and assault. “No man wants to be a slave who breaks his back and gets nothing back.” Promoting a link to classes for building “AI systems,” he exhorted his readers: “Put down the game controller.”</p><p class="paywall">If the Tates, incels, and other far-right reactionaries of the world are so bent out of shape, it’s probably because whereas <em>GTA</em> once punched down at marginalized groups like <a class="external-link text link" href="https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-prostitutes-hookers-strippers-where-are-npcs-rockstar/">sex workers</a> and <a class="external-link text link" href="https://www.gamesindustry.biz/rockstar-appears-to-have-edited-transphobic-elements-in-gta-v-re-release">transgender people</a>, bigots and self-styled alpha males are now the butt of the joke. The Vice City of <em>GTA VI</em> is bristling with parodies of toxic manosphere content and crypto scams. Random characters on the street will spout MAGA-coded conspiracy theories and call you a “libtard” or “commie socialist” if you walk into them. These touches seem to have made the game into “a bug zapper for chuds,” as one Bluesky user <a class="external-link text link" href="https://bsky.app/profile/nopartyhats.bsky.social/post/3n2msbk2udk27">put it</a>. (That hasn’t stopped <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/HouseGOP/status/2123413147021197574">Republicans</a> and <a class="external-link text link" href="https://x.com/KatieMiller/status/2123454038977269791">Trump allies</a> from trying and failing to score political points with the game.)</p><p class="paywall">Yet the provocation of asking guys to consider acts of service for a virtual girlfriend could prove the most revolutionary element of <em>GTA VI</em>.</p><p class="paywall">It’s not that games like <em>The Sims</em> haven’t explored this territory before, and Rockstar’s own <em>Red Dead Redemption</em> franchise paid similar attention to quotidian detail. In those frontier-set narratives, your cowboy character had to make coffee over campfires and even clean up manure in barns. By addressing characters’ most basic needs—you can even manage personal hygiene by showering—<em>GTA VI</em> recalls some of the slower and quieter aspects of that franchise.</p><p class="paywall">What’s truly remarkable, though, is that adult men who grew up indulging their most selfish and sociopathic instincts in the older <em>GTA</em> games have had their nostalgia for those escapades cleverly complicated with real-life problems. They can still steal cars, sell drugs, and rise through the ranks of a seedy underworld. That will always be the appeal of the series, which is now asking eternally aggrieved culture warriors to consider a vital question: What if you didn’t have to do it alone?</p></div></div>
