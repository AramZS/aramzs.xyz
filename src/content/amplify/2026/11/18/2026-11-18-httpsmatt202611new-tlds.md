<p>For the first time since 2012, there’s a new round of TLDs being created by ICANN, and we just had <a href="https://www.icann.org/en/announcements/details/icann-confirms-2026-round-applications-input-and-objections-period-begins-updated-17-11-2026-en">string confirmation day</a>!</p>
<p>I’m happy to share that Automattic is uncontested for .jazz, .php, .soul, .wordpress, .tumblr, .woo, and I personally got .matt.</p>
<p>We have really enjoyed <a href="https://my.blog/">shepherding .blog</a> which has turned into a nice option with a pretty clean namespace (that’s why we have premium prices on renewal, not just registration) and it’s had some high profile adoption, for example <a href="https://github.blog/">github.blog</a>, <a href="https://stackoverflow.blog/">stackoverflow.blog</a>, <a href="https://seths.blog/">seths.blog</a> (Seth Godin), <a href="https://tim.blog/">tim.blog</a> (Tim Ferriss), <a href="https://www.noahpinion.blog/">noahpinion.blog</a> (Noah Smith), <a href="https://steve.blog/">steve.blog</a> (Steve Jurvetson), and <a href="https://mgs.blog/">mgs.blog</a> (M.G. Siegler) and ~ 395,000 more. 🤠</p>
<p><a href="https://newgtldprogram-aps.icann.org/applications">You can see all the still contested TLDs here</a>.</p>
