<p><a data-tag="david-ellison" href="https://variety.com/t/david-ellison/">David Ellison</a>, chairman and CEO of <a data-tag="skydance" href="https://variety.com/t/skydance/">Skydance</a>, confirmed that tech mogul <a data-tag="elon-musk" href="https://variety.com/t/elon-musk/">Elon Musk</a> has invested in the company, formed through the merger of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery.</p>
<p>Musk, the world’s richest person, invested about $1 billion in Skydance post-merger, <a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/22/elon-musk-skydance-investment.html">CNBC</a> and Semafor reported last month. Ellison, speaking Wednesday at the New York Times’ 2026 DealBook Summit at Manhattan’s Jazz at Lincoln Center, acknowledged that Musk is a part-owner of Skydance but he did not confirm the investment amount.</p>
<p><a href="https://variety.com/2026/film/news/nicole-kidman-honorary-cesar-french-film-academy-1236945969/"><figure><img alt="Nicole Kidman at Chanel &amp; Charles Finch Pre-Oscars Dinner held at Polo Lounge on March 14, 2026 in Beverly Hills, California." data-lazy-loaded="1" src="https://variety.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/GF2_3291.jpg?w=250&amp;h=167&amp;crop=1"/><figcaption><a href="https://variety.com/2026/film/news/nicole-kidman-honorary-cesar-french-film-academy-1236945969/">Nicole Kidman at Chanel &amp; Charles Finch Pre-Oscars Dinner held at Polo Lounge on March 14, 2026 in Beverly Hills, California.</a></figcaption></figure></a></p>
<p>The New York Times’ Andrew Ross Sorkin asked Ellison about the reports of Musk investing a billion dollars into Skydance, “and I think some people wanted to know whether this is the beginning of something bigger with Starlink long-term, or is a one-off as a given his relationship with your father,” Larry Ellison.</p>
<p>Ellison responded, “So look, I have so much admiration and respect for Elon. I mean, I don’t know if you saw recently, but literally watching, you know, [SpaceX’s] Starship, it’s a marvel…. I mean, he is both the [Nikola] Tesla and [Thomas] Edison… He is, from my perspective, the greatest entrepreneur of our time, and he doesn’t invest in a lot of companies.”</p>
<p>Ellison continued, “So to have his vote of confidence and to have him as a shareholder is an honor and a privilege. And if there was any opportunity to be able to collaborate with him in the future… [which] is the appropriate opportunity in the future, we’d be thrilled to do so. But there’s nothing [right now].”</p>
<p>Sorkin asked Ellison whether he is still planning to put Alex Gibney’s documentary “Musk” — which prompted a <a href="https://variety.com/2026/film/spotlight/alex-gibney-elon-musk-elizabeth-holmes-1236943837/">legal threat from the tech titan against the filmmaker</a> — on HBO. (“Musk” has been pegged to begin streaming on HBO Max in the first quarter of 2027.) Ellison responded, “Of course.”</p>
<p>Ellison’s Paramount Skydance closed its $111 billion megamerger with <a href="https://variety.com/2026/film/news/paramount-warner-bros-merger-officially-closes-skydance-david-ellison-1236900047/">Warner Bros. Discovery on Oct. 6</a>, with major backing from his father, Oracle billionaire Larry Ellison. The company, one of the biggest media and entertainment conglomerates in the industry, has around <a href="https://variety.com/2026/biz/news/skydance-debt-cash-flow-ellison-sp-moodys-creditsights-1236899331/">$80 billion in net debt</a> and is expected to lay off thousands of employees as part of achieving $6 billion in annualized savings by 2029.</p>
