<p>Video showed demonstrators from a pro-Palestinian protest throwing projectiles at former President Joseph R. Biden Jr. after he attended an event at Trinity College Dublin on Tuesday.</p><figure><img alt="Former President Joseph R. Biden Jr,, wearing a navy pinstripe suit and a yellow-and-blue striped tie, looks toward another person. The second person, in a dark suit, is partially turned and appears to smile." src="https://static01.nyt.com/images/2026/11/11/multimedia/11xp-biden-topart-01-qkbf/11xp-biden-topart-01-qkbf-articleLarge.jpg"/><figcaption>Former President Joseph R. Biden Jr. in Dublin on Tuesday.  Niall Carson/PA, via Associated Press</figcaption></figure><p>Former President Joseph R. Biden Jr. was met by protesters, some of whom who threw projectiles, after he attended an event at Trinity College Dublin on Tuesday, as the police scuffled with demonstrators attending a pro-Palestinian rally, according to video of the incident.</p><p>An Garda Síochána, the Irish national police force, said three men were arrested on Tuesday in relation to a public order incident. Two of the men were later released, and one was charged, the police said in a statement on Wednesday.</p><p>Mr. Biden had been attending a ceremony held by at the College Historical Society, a debate society informally known as the Hist, in honor of his son, Hunter Biden. Hunter Biden and Jill Biden, the former first lady, also attended the event, according to Ireland’s national broadcaster, RTÉ.</p><p>“A protest took place in Trinity College Dublin’s Front Square on Tuesday, 10 November during a ceremony in the Exam Hall,” said Catherine O’Mahony, a spokeswoman for the college. “The protest escalated while the Bidens were leaving” the event, she said.</p><p>Videos show groups of people gathered in the square, some waving Palestinian flags and chanting “blood on your hands.” As Mr. Biden was leaving the area, footage shows objects and white liquid, which appeared to be milk, being thrown at him. Mr. Biden was <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/joe-biden/joe-biden-pelted-objects-ireland-dublin-palestinian-protests-hunter-rcna608713">shielded from the projectiles by black umbrellas</a> held by members of his entourage.</p><figure><img alt="A chaotic scene with uniformed officers and people in suits near black cars. On stone steps, an officer holds a person who points forward." src="https://static01.nyt.com/images/2026/11/11/multimedia/11xp-biden-body-01-hqwf/11xp-biden-body-01-hqwf-articleLarge.jpg"/><figcaption>A person is detained outside Trinity College during the visit by Hunter and Joe Biden.  Niall Carson/PA, via Associated Press</figcaption></figure><p>The protesters dispersed not long after the Bidens departed, Ms. O’Mahony said.</p><p>A representative for the Bidens declined to comment.</p><p>Of the three men arrested in connection with the incident, one was in his 50s, one in his 30s, and another in his late teens, the police said. The man in his 50s was charged and was expected to appear in court, the police said, declining to give further details.</p><p>The Hist awarded Hunter Biden its Gold Medal for Outstanding Contribution to Public Discourse. The accolade is “awarded to guests who have dedicated their lives to dialogue — peacemakers, politicians, personalities,” according to the organization’s website. Past <a href="https://www.tcd.ie/news_events/articles/president-of-ireland-receives-gold-medal-from-the-hist/">recipients</a> include the former Irish president Michael D. Higgins.</p><p>“To a society like the Hist, Biden’s recent advocacy on the state of American politics and media as well as for matters of addiction and recovery make him a commendable figure,” the <a href="https://universitytimes.ie/2026/11/hunter-biden-to-visit-trinity-next-week/">organization said</a> in a post on social media last week announcing that it would host Hunter Biden for the event on Tuesday.</p><p>The former president also met with Micheál Martin, Ireland’s prime minister, who posted a photograph of himself with Mr. Biden and the former first lady on social media on Tuesday.</p><p>Mr. Biden, who has Irish ancestry, has long been vocal about his fondness for Ireland, and he has previously enjoyed a warm welcome in the country. But support for the former president has chilled since 2023, when Israel began its devastating military offensive in Gaza in response to the <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2023/12/22/world/europe/beeri-massacre.html">Oct 7. Hamas-led attacks</a>.</p><p>Support for Palestinians is <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2023/12/02/world/europe/ireland-palestinians-support.html">widespread</a> in Ireland. As leader of the United States, Israel’s closest ally and by far its <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2024/04/06/us/politics/israel-us-weapons.html">biggest supplier of weapons</a>, Mr. Biden became a focus of Irish ire.</p><p>Mr. Biden, 83, was diagnosed last year with <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/18/us/politics/biden-prostate-cancer.html">an aggressive form of prostate cancer</a>. He <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/08/us/politics/biden-cancer-health-memoir.html">said Sunday</a> that his health would prevent him from making as many public appearances to promote his forthcoming memoir as he had hoped.</p>
