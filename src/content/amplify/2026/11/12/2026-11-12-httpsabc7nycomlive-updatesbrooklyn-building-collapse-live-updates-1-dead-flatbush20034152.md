<p>FLATBUSH, Brooklyn (WABC) -- Eight people are dead and 17 others are hurt, two critically, after part of a three-story building collapsed into a construction site at the corner of Flatbush and Bedford avenues in Flatbush, Brooklyn, just after 4 p.m. Wednesday, according to the FDNY.</p>
<p>Six bodies were recovered from the rubble, and two people died at the hospital. <a data-testid="prism-linkbase" href="https://abc7ny.com/post/flatbush-brooklyn-building-collapse-leaves-2-dead-17-injured-search-continues/20035354/">Four of the victims found Thursday</a> are believed to be construction workers. Seven children are among those treated.</p>
<p>The building at 1321 Flatbush Ave., which had a store at street level and apartments upstairs, fell into an open excavation at 2625 Bedford Ave.</p>
<p>The Department of Buildings says the developer had an active permit for excavation and foundation work and has issued a full stop-work order while investigators determine the cause.</p>
<p>Officials say others may still be missing.</p>
<p><strong>Stay with Eyewitness News for continuing updates.</strong></p>

<p>Mayor Zohran Mamdani posted on X that he's "heartbroken" that eight New Yorkers have lost their lives and at least 17 have been injured, including 3 firefighters.</p>
<p>"My heart is with the families and loved ones of those we have lost, those who are injured and everyone grieving today," he said in the post.</p>
<p>He urged New Yorkers to call 311 if they are looking for someone who may have been involved in the collapse.</p>
