<p>The revelation follows two other extramarital ⁠affairs that led to his <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/live/nvidia-live-board-meets-fate-ceo-jensen-huang-after-affair-reports-2026-11-12/">resignation</a> ​Thursday.
