<p>High-profile former DOJ official Harmeet Dhillon has a bone to pick with the Republican Party and she claims <a href="https://www.newsnationnow.com/politics/harmeet-dhillon-gop-religious-intolerance/">she now feels “free” to speak her mind</a> after her abrupt resignation.</p>
<p>Speaking with NewsNation host Katie Pavlich, the former U.S. assistant attorney general for the Civil Rights Division unloaded on what she saw as i<a href="https://www.rawstory.com/harmeet-dhillon-2678322598/">nherent “bigotry” within her own party</a>.</p>
<p>Weeks out of government, Dhillon used her Wednesday appearance on <a href="https://www.newsnationnow.com/politics/harmeet-dhillon-gop-religious-intolerance/">“Katie Pavlich Tonight”</a> to sound a warning about where the party is heading.</p>
<p>“I think it is very important for the future of this country that we have a vibrant Republican Party, and that means that this type of drift into bigotry and antisemitism needs to be called out and stopped,” the Donald <a href="https://www.rawstory.com/trump-news/">Trump</a> appointee warned.</p>
<p>Dhillon was born in <a href="https://www.rawstory.com/tag/india">India</a> and practices Sikhism. During her interview, she claimed she has endured a steady stream of abuse from the online right ever since she led a Sikh prayer from the stage of the 2024 Republican National Convention. Detractors have dismissed her as a diversity pick, and some have gone as far as <a href="https://www.rawstory.com/trump-devil-worshippers/">branding her a demon worshipper</a>.</p>
<p>“It’s unfortunate because a political party is not a religion, and you can only win <a href="https://www.rawstory.com/tag/elections">elections</a> through addition, not subtraction,” she told Pavlich. “My religion doesn’t proselytize. The reason I did this prayer is the same reason why Jews and people of other faiths do prayers at a national convention, which is to show the breadth of acceptance of other people in the party.”</p>
<p>Dhillon signaled the gloves are now off.</p>
<p>“I’m not the only target of this hate, but I do have a big voice,” she said. “I kind of got tired of not being able to defend myself.”</p>
<p>That new combativeness was already on display on X earlier this month, when she <a href="https://x.com/HarmeetKDhillon/status/2119087438526046266">challenged faceless trolls</a> to reveal who they were.</p>
<p>Dhillon <a href="https://federalnewsnetwork.com/people/2026/11/harmeet-dhillon-exits-after-radical-overhaul-of-justice-departments-civil-rights-division/">announced her exit Nov. 5</a>, shortly after <a href="https://www.rawstory.com/harmneet-dhillon-quitting/">Republicans were crushed at the ballot box</a>. Her departure <a href="https://www.rawstory.com/harmeet-dhillon-2678322598/">unleashed another round of racist attacks from MAGA corners</a>. On her watch, the bulk of the division’s lawyers headed for the doors, a staff collapse she has openly embraced.</p>
