<p>Denmark’s oldest-ever person has been outed as a former local leader of her country’s Nazi party.</p>
<p>Kirsten Schwalbe was widely celebrated last month when she became the oldest Dane ever, at 112 years and 218 days, after already being part of the “world’s oldest siblings” with her 104-year-old younger brother Bertel.</p>
<p>She initially denied ever having Nazi sympathies despite records showing she joined Denmark’s branch, the Danish National Socialist Workers Party, in August 1941, during World War II.</p>

<p>“I barely knew what it was, because I’ve never been interested in politics,” she said in an <a href="https://www.tvmidtvest.dk/struer/kirsten-schwalbe-var-medlem-af-nazistparti-dengang-satte-konerne-sig-ikke-ind-i-sadan-noget-f19d9">interview with Danish news outlet TV Midtvest</a> two days after reaching her landmark age.</p>
<p>But newly uncovered documents in the Danish National Archives allegedly show Schwalbe was the acting leader of the party in her local town of Holstebro between August 1943 and July 1944, according to local reports. She also led the local women’s group.</p>
<p>Her late husband also reportedly held a leadership position in the Danish Nazi Party, which mimicked its German counterpart with the use of the swastika, the Hitler salute, and antisemitism, and actively collaborated with the Third Reich during the occupation of Denmark during the war.</p>
<p>“She was more than an ordinary member of the rank and file,” said Danish historian Claus Bundgaard Christensen, who examined the documents, describing Schwalbe as one of the “prominent Nazis” in her area at the time.</p>
<p>Schwalbe’s story came to light after the recent publication of a biography of her life, titled “We Need Something to Laugh About,” which made no mention of her Nazi past.</p>
<p>The Nazi revelations then emerged later last month in Danish newspaper <a href="https://www.kristeligt-dagblad.dk/kultur/danmarks-aeldste-livstykkerekordlangt-liv-temmelig-tynd-bogbogen-om-kirsten-schwalbe-er">Kristeligt Dagblad</a>.</p>
