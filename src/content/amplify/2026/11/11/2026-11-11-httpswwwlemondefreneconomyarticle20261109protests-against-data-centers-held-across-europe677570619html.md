<p>A wave of opposition to data centers is sweeping across Europe. On Friday and Saturday, demonstrations against these facilities, which are essential for the rollout of artificial intelligence, took place in about 15 European countries. The protests were organized by Stop Dirty Data Centres, a coalition of environmental and digital rights advocacy groups. "The explosion of AI data centres across our towns and cities is taking a toll that we can no longer afford," the movement states <a href="https://stopdirtydatacentres.eu/">on its website</a>. "We're calling for an immediate moratorium on large data centres in Europe!"</p>
<p>In Poland, the Netherlands and Spain, small groups gathered locally. Most often, only a few dozen people attended, though sometimes numbers rose to several thousand, depending on the form of protest. The movement recorded a total of 90 events in the countries involved. Actions in France included a citizens' march against a mega data center project in northern Brebières and Corbehem and a rally in southeastern Les Pennes-Mirabeau outside another center's construction site. In France, about 15 events, organized by local groups already active on the issue, were scheduled over the two days to demand "a moratorium on large data center projects" and the launch of a "democratic and binding consultation" on digital development in the country.</p>
