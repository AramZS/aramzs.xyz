I buy Bramley apple sauce</li> <li><strong>Golden syrup</strong> - for that toffee-like flavour, but if you can't get hold of it, you can use maple syrup</li> <li><strong>Plain flour</strong> - all-purpose flour</li> <li><strong>Brown sugar</strong> - or demerara sugar</li> </ul>

<h2>🥕 Flavour Swaps and Added Extras</h2>
<p>The basic recipe works well, but you can tweak it.</p>
<ol> <li>Swap the grated apple for grated carrot and add <strong>mixed spice </strong>or pumpkin spice and ginger for a carrot cake vibe.</li> <li>Add raisins, dried cranberries or chopped dates for little pockets of chewy sweetness.</li> <li>Throw in a few chopped pecans or walnuts for crunch.</li> <li>At Halloween, add some pumpkin pie spice (which is similar to our mixed spice here in Scotland) for a seasonal autumn (fall) twist.</li> <li>Or drizzle melted dark chocolate or dairy-free white chocolate over the flapjacks instead of the cinnamon icing drizzle.</li> </ol>

<p><strong>These apple and cinnamon flapjacks are always soft and chewy. Here's why:</strong></p>
<ul> <li><strong>Golden syrup</strong> - Keeps the moisture locked in and creates that classic Scottish flapjack chewy texture with the distinctive caramel flavour. Only use maple syrup instead if you can't get golden syrup anywhere.</li> <li><strong>Apple sauce and fresh apple</strong> - Adds moisture and prevents the oats from drying out. They also bring sweetness.</li> <li><strong>Mixing the mixture thoroughly</strong> - So the oats have a chance to soak in all the flavours and no dry patches.</li> <li><strong>Well-pressed mixture</strong> - It's important to press the mixture down firmly, so that it is well compacted. Loose oats can turn your flapjacks into a crumbly mess, so take time to compress the mixture with the back of a spoon thoroughly before baking.</li> </ul>
<p>The flapjacks will be soft when they come out of the microwave, but will firm up more as they cool.</p>

<ol start="3"> <li><strong>Step 3: </strong>In a mixing bowl, add the flour, oats, and brown sugar, then add the cinnamon and salt and mix.</li> </ol>

<ol start="4"> <li><strong>Step 4:</strong> Add the grated apple and mix it into the dry flapjack mixture well with a spoon.</li> </ol>

<ol start="5"> <li><strong>Step 5: </strong>Pour the melted butter mixture into the dried flapjack mixture (with the grated apple) and mix well to combine.</li> </ol>

<ol start="8"> <li><strong>Step 8:</strong> Cut the flapjacks into bars (in the dish whilst still warm). Leave to cool completely, then drizzle with the icing, if you are adding it.</li> </ol>
<h2>💭 How to Store Flapjacks</h2>
<p>Once cool, you can store the cut oat bars in an airtight container.</p>
<p>Then keep them in the fridge for 4-5 days.</p>
<p>Although they never last that long in my house.</p>

<h2>Microwave Apple &amp; Cinnamon Flapjacks - Quick &amp; Easy</h2>
<p>Forget the oven. Make these chewy Scottish-style apple &amp; cinnamon flapjacks in the microwave in a few minutes. Ridiculously good oat bars!</p>
<h3>Ingredients  </h3>
<ul><li data-uid="0">250 g porridge oats (rolled oats)</li><li data-uid="4">100 g plain flour (all purpose flour)</li><li data-uid="5">120 g brown sugar</li><li data-uid="1">150 g vegan butter</li><li data-uid="2">50 g apple sauce</li><li data-uid="3">25 g golden syrup (or maple syrup)</li><li data-uid="6">2 ½ teaspoons cinnamon</li><li data-uid="7">1  pinch  salt</li><li data-uid="12">1 small apple (grated, skin on, discard the core)</li></ul>
<h4>cinnamon icing (optional)</h4>
<ul><li data-uid="9">2 tablespoons icing sugar (confectioner's sugar)</li><li data-uid="10">½ teaspoon cinnamon</li><li data-uid="11">1 teaspoon water</li></ul>
<h3>Instructions </h3>
<ul><li>Butter your microwave dish (mine is a ceramic dish which is 13 ½ cm x 18 cm (5 ½ inches x 7 ¼ inches) approximately. Try to find one of a similar size, glass, ceramic or plastic. A bit bigger or smaller is ok.</li><li>Add the butter, golden syrup and apple sauce to a microwave friendly bowl and melt for about 1 minute. Set aside.</li><li>Add the oats, flour, brown sugar, grated apple, cinnamon and salt to a mixing bowl and mix well.</li><li>Pour in the wet ingredients and mix well to allow all the oats to be well coated.</li><li>Spoon into your buttered microwave dish and press down really well with the back of a spoon. You want the surface as even as possible and the mixture well compacted to prevent the bars being crumbly.</li><li>Microwave for 4 minutes, sit for 2 minutes, then microwave for 3 ½ minutes. See notes below, your microwave may be more or less powerful and you will have to adjust the time.</li><li>Cut the bars in the dish and leave to cool in the dish. it will firm slightly as it cools.</li><li>Enjoy!</li></ul>
<h3>Notes</h3>
<ul> <li>My top tip is to compact the mixture really well, if you don't they will be crumbly.</li> <li> My microwave is 800 watts. If yours is a higher wattage, you may want to take the flapjacks out a minute or so early. If your microwave is less powerful, the flapjacks may need an extra minute or two. </li> <li>The bars can be cut into 16 squares to half the calories, and skipping the icing will also reduce the calories.</li> </ul>
<h3>Nutrition</h3>
<p>Serving: 1 barProtein: 6gSodium: 133mgSugar: 22gCalcium: 39mg</p>
