<p>The best detail from Russell Brand's trial on charges of rape and sexual assault? That the <a href="https://www.independent.co.uk/news/uk/home-news/russell-brand-trial-verdict-inside-southwark-crown-court-wife-b3081471.html">only person willing</a> to testify on his behalf was his wife, Laura Brand (née Gallacher). The desperation of this is even more obvious upon learning that <a href="https://www.yahoo.com/entertainment/celebrity/articles/inside-russell-brands-relationship-beloved-113844854.html">Mrs. Brand was a 19-year-old</a> student when she met the then-30-year-old comedian, who was already quite famous in 2007. Not helpful when the prosecution was arguing that Russell Brand had a pattern of preying on younger, less powerful women.</p>
<p>It was a genuine relief to see Brand be convicted and <a href="https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/russell-brand-convicted-of-rape-and-sexual-assault">sentenced to over</a> 10 years for one count of rape and three of sexual assault in a British court. I've always felt the backlash to #MeToo was overhyped, but still, it was nice to have another piece of evidence that the public is ready to take these allegations more seriously than they might have before.</p>

<p>What was fascinating to me was Brand's showy conversion to MAGA Christianity, an offshoot of evangelical Christianity where all your praying is done in public, ideally on camera. Going MAGA is a well-trod path for celebrities who are facing career collapse, or even just decline, and are hoping that the rubes of the right will be so eager for celebrities of their own that they'll spend money. The <a href="https://www.salon.com/2026/10/18/ben-shapiro-is-building-a-comeback-machine-for-canceled-stars/">Daily Wire has staked</a> its hopes of Hollywood relevance on scooping up actors who have personal scandals — including gendered violence — that make them toxic to hire in more professional productions. Even Nicki Minaj and Kanye West have made this play.</p>
<p>Brand could have done that, without all the Christian bullshit. Merely facing credible rape accusations was enough to make Brand a hero to MAGA leaders. RFK Jr. and Dr. Oz weren't interested in Brand's blabbering about Jesus when they <a href="https://www.the-independent.com/news/world/americas/us-politics/rfk-jr-russell-brand-mehmet-oz-picture-b2699593.html">posted a shirtless photo</a> with him.</p>
<p>Perhaps Brand was sincere in his conversion. Likelier, though, he just saw this as an audience of people who are naive or stupid enough to buy anything, and so thought he could milk them for both money and hopefully enough political power to keep him out of prison.</p>
<p>Maybe that worked on the money front, as evidenced by his hawking of <a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/callumbooth/2024/10/16/the-internet-is-angry-about-russell-brands-magical-amulet-heres-why/">magical amulets</a> and <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/KnowledgeFight/comments/1p89h4j/a_week_or_so_after_mocking_alex_to_his_face_for/">Christian-branded supplements</a>. But, as his guilty verdict showed (and I <a href="https://youtube.com/shorts/2ezTqkuu7-Q?si=A1rnQYYxLTAiG3Ty">mocked in short video </a>form), it was utterly useless as a defense against rape allegations. That's certainly true in Great Britain, where evangelicalism isn't nearly as big a cultural deal as it is in the U.S. But honestly, even if you sat a jury in Alabama, I don't think the Bible-hugging would have saved him. Because Christian conservatives do not care about Russell Brand.</p>
<p>The edgelord-to-saved folks are mostly tokens in the evangelical world. Christian conservatives put them on display as circus freaks, but ultimately the church crowd will never fully embrace the former wild children as theirs.</p>
<p>The example of famous tattoo artist Kat Von D is helpful. After <a href="https://www.thelist.com/602247/the-truth-about-kat-von-d-and-rafael-reyes-anti-semitism-controversy/">a series of scandals</a> involving anti-vaccination statements and affairs with men who are into Nazi imagery, <a href="https://www.businessinsider.com/how-kat-von-d-makeup-will-change-2020-1">she left the makeup company</a> she founded and they rebranded with a different name. A couple of years later, she <a href="https://cbn.com/news/entertainment/kat-von-d-remains-transparent-about-christian-journey-shares-new-step-orthodoxy">claimed she found Jesus </a>and did the rounds on Christian podcasts. This was fun for them, because they got to titilliate their audiences by putting a heavily tattooed woman on screen and performing love and acceptance of her, but it never really amounted to much. She's never really become a Christian celebrity, because they will never take her seriously.</p>
<p>Brand got a little more play, giving speeches at TPUSA and being a guest on Tucker Carlson's show. In the end, however, none of his famous Christian or MAGA buddies seemed interested in standing up for him in court. All <a href="https://www.facebook.com/reel/1027846513691215">he got was a few loons</a> waving signs outside the courtroom. Which is more than he deserved, to be clear, but still. He spent years bellyaching about Christianity online and, in the end, the community he tried to join spat him out the second he became too inconvenient.</p>
