<p>Federal workers in a government tech office are once again being mandated to send in updates about what they do, giving some flashbacks to the <a href="https://www.washingtonsun.com/congress/elon-musk-5-things?redirected=notus_org&amp;_gl=1*16cag2w*_gcl_au*MjAxMzMxNjU5OC4xNzg4MTk4MDU5LjI1NDE5OTQxOS4xNzg5NDc4ODk0LjE3ODk0Nzg4OTQuMTMxODI3NzM1OS4xNzg5Mzk1NzI5LjE3ODk0Nzg4OTQ.*_ga*MTQ4OTA5NDgwNi4xNzg4MTk4MDU5*_ga_2B7KD8RZRH*czE3OTQyNTYzOTMkbzE4OCRnMSR0MTc5NDI1NzMxMiRqNTgkbDAkaDA."><u>infamous</u></a> “five things” emails Elon Musk required at the height of DOGE.</p>
<p>Sam Corcos, a former member of DOGE currently leading the Technology Transformation Services office in the General Services Administration, is asking employees to send daily emails recapping their work, or risk “adverse action.”</p>
<p>“The purpose of these updates is to help us better understand what everyone is working on, give TTS Front Office a clearer picture of progress across the organization, and identify any blockers,” Corcos wrote to over 200 employees last Friday. “Failure to comply may result in adverse action.”</p>
<p>Employees are to send in a summary of the work they did that day, what they plan to do tomorrow and any issues they need help with by 6 p.m (in their respective time zones). The requirement began Monday.</p>

<p>Justin Adelman, who, according to his LinkedIn, graduated college in 2023 and was hired over the summer through Trump’s early-career tech program, the U.S. Tech Force, will be charged with helping “unblock” any problems identified in the emails, Corcos wrote.</p>
<p>One employee within the GSA told The Washington Sun that the email reflects Corcos’ “basic distrust in and underestimation of Federal workers.”</p>
<p>Another GSA employee said they don’t trust that the email is genuinely meant to help the agency surface problems.</p>
<p>“It feels like they’re trying to mess with our heads again,” they said.</p>
<p>Last year, Musk directed all federal workers to submit weekly emails noting five things they accomplished. The White House formally <a href="https://www.washingtonsun.com/trump-white-house/elon-musk-five-things-email-federal-employees-trump-white-house"><u>ended the requirement</u></a> in August.</p>
<p>Corcos, who is also the Treasury Department’s chief information officer, called Adelman an “experienced engineer whose work has spanned major public-sector technology initiatives, including the IRS mobile app and super intelligence efforts across Treasury, as well as prior startup experience.”</p>
<p>Adelman is set to receive a masters from the Georgia Institute of Technology next month, according to his LinkedIn profile, where he lists himself as a co-founder of several startups and has also done stints working for government contractors, one of those for two and a half years.</p>
<p>Adelman, per Corcos, is leading the GSA’s work on the Trump administration’s <a href="http://america.gov"><u>America.gov</u></a> website. That <a href="https://www.washingtonsun.com/whitehouse/trump-government-ai-bot-2027-america-gov"><u>project</u></a> is through the White House’s National Design Studio, which is led by another former DOGE associate and Airbnb co-founder, Joe Gebbia.</p>
<p>The GSA did not respond to a request for comment.</p>
