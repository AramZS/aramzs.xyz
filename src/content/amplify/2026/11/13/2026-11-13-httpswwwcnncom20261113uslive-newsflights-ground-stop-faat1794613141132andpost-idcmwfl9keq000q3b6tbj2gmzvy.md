<h2 data-editable="headline">What's happening now</h2>
<p>• <strong>Ground stop:</strong> All flights in the United States are <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/faa-nationwide-ground-stop">grounded until further notice</a>, “due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety,” the Federal Aviation Administration said. The issue stems from a <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794608428143">telecommunications outage</a>, but an airline source told CNN <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794609418375&amp;post-id=cmwfkqcuz00003b6unjugmwc6">the root cause is still unclear</a>.</p>
<p>• <strong>No known threat:</strong> Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said the ground stop <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?post-id=cmwfj1dzh00003b6tujtqmbd1">did not stem from a threat to the US</a> and was <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794608428143&amp;post-id=cmwfix7jp00003b6tz2tqguzw">not linked</a> to this week’s disruptive Microsoft outage. Preliminary reports indicate <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?post-id=cmwfhx4br00003b6tcfhe070g">this was not a cyberattack</a>, law enforcement sources told CNN. President Donald Trump <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?post-id=cmwfj7pfy00003b6t908oqoct">has been briefed</a>.</p>
<p>• <strong>No timetable for resolution: </strong>Mullin <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794608428143&amp;post-id=cmwfjdvt100073b6tmsonkpl6">warned that a resolution will take time</a>. News of the <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794608428143&amp;post-id=cmwfhna9600003b6uh443445t">rare nationwide ground stop</a> is trickling in to passengers at <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794608909584&amp;post-id=cmwfj480w000q3b6uelcg9hmd">major</a> <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794608909584&amp;post-id=cmwfim0mg00053b6uo8qnbuaw">airports</a> across the country as airlines start to share updates about delays and cancellations.</p>
<p>The Federal Aviation Administration announced this afternoon “extraordinary circumstances” and “reasons of safety” prompted a ground stop for all flights in the United States.</p>
<p><em>Here’s what we know so far and outstanding questions left to be answered:</em></p>
<ul data-editable="items"> <li data-editable="items.0.text"> <strong>What is a ground stop?: </strong>A ground stop is <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfipobz00003b6tvr2dxdxv">an order</a> issued by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), which requires aircraft to remain on the ground and bars them from taking off. Planes already in flight are allowed to remain in the air. </li> <li data-editable="items.1.text"> <strong>Unclear cause: </strong>The reason for the ground stop <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfh0w1o001527p95l6adijn">was not immediately clear</a>, but an FAA spokesperson said there is a communications problem affecting Air Route Traffic Control Centers, which handle flights going across the country. According to Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy, “FAA technicians <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfhosfg000m3b6tow40iaex">are investigating the issue</a>.” But <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfhx4br00003b6tcfhe070g">preliminary reports</a> indicate this is not a cyberattack, according to two law enforcement officials. The FAA “doesn’t know yet what is going on; it is affecting different facilities in different ways,” an airline source told CNN. “Ten minutes ago, they were on a crisis call and there was still no clear answer as to a cause.” </li> <li data-editable="items.2.text"> <strong>DHS weighs in:</strong> Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin says there is currently <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfj1dzh00003b6tujtqmbd1">no reason</a> to suspect a national security threat is behind the outage that grounded all US flights. He did say the department is <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfj8h4c00083b6t4jk2ynuy">looking into</a> whether a new AI-supported air traffic control system contributed to massive communications problems, though. Mullin also said yesterday’s massive global Microsoft outage and today’s problem causing the ground stop <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfix7jp00003b6tz2tqguzw">are not connected</a>. He added officials have not yet discussed a time frame for resolving the outage as the administration focuses on passenger safety. </li> <li data-editable="items.3.text"> <strong>Briefing: </strong>President Donald Trump <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfj7pfy00003b6t908oqoct">has been briefed</a> by officials on the FAA ground stop, Mullin said, adding he has spoken on the phone with Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy. </li> <li data-editable="items.4.text"> <strong>Ground stop history: </strong>The last time there was a <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfhna9600003b6uh443445t">nationwide ground stop</a> was in January 2023, when a pilot notification system went down. Prior to that, the last nationwide ground stop was issued after the September 11, 2001, attacks. </li> <li data-editable="items.5.text"> <strong>What people are saying: </strong>Airlines for America, the major trade association representing US airlines, said it’s <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfjkhfd00003b6trvn7b3z8">in close communication</a> with the Department of Transportation and the FAA. Major airlines, including <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?post-id=cmwfjacaq00093b6tiiwa2x0j">American</a>, <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?post-id=cmwfjjqp6000h3b6tuojhbd7x">United</a> and <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?post-id=cmwfk530f000n3b6t417q2p52">Southwest</a>, have notified passengers of the ground stop. And Sen. Tammy Duckworth today <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610237854&amp;post-id=cmwfjppqi00003b6tp3hbaz1l">called for</a> a “thorough and complete” investigation into the matter. <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/13/us/live-news/flights-ground-stop-faa?t=1794610851173&amp;post-id=cmwfkdl1700073b6tvefith3u">See how travelers and pilots are reacting to the ground stop here.</a> </li> </ul>

<p>There is no known radar or communications outage at the National Weather Service, NOAA communications director Chris Vaccaro told CNN.</p>
<p>An outdated webpage run by the Anchorage, Alaska, office of the National Weather Service indicated there was a radar outage. Once CNN alerted that office to the page, they updated it to say there is no outage.</p>
<p>Democratic Sen. Maria Cantwell, the ranking member of the Senate Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation, said the committee will be reviewing technology issues related to communication.</p>
<aside data-article-gutter="true" data-component-name="pull-quote" data-uri="cms.cnn.com/_components/pull-quote/instances/cmwfm549w00003b6toeg6n72r@published"> <p data-editable="text"> “Technology issues related to how planes communicate through primary frequencies, backup frequencies, and other issues will be something the Commerce Committee will be reviewing,” Cantwell said in a statement. </p> </aside>
<p>Cantwell said her team is in contact with the FAA and is monitoring the situation.</p>

<p>“I don’t know what to do,” a student pilot told air traffic controllers during the nationwide ground stop on Friday.</p>
<p>Controllers at the Fort Worth Meacham International Airport tower advised the pilot he needed to land the aircraft and asked where he would like to land.</p>
<p>The plane landed a few minutes at Hicks Airfield, about 15 miles northwest of Fort Worth, after the pilot’s conversation with the controller, according to FlightAware.</p>

<p>Planes across the country are still barred from taking off roughly two hours after a nationwide ground stop was issued, according to an advisory from the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA).</p>
<p>As facilities begin to recover, flights will be allowed to depart once they coordinate with the FAA’s command center located in Virginia, according to the update.</p>
<p>Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin and Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth were both in and out of the West Wing Friday as officials scrambled to respond to a nationwide ground stop of flights.</p>
<p>Hegseth was in the Situation Room, a person familiar with the matter told CNN, as Mullin briefly stepped outside to answer reporters’ questions.</p>
<p>Mullin said President Donald Trump had been briefed by officials on the FAA ground stop for all flights and had spoken on the phone with Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy.</p>
<p>That urgency wasn’t apparent across government social media accounts, namely the president’s.</p>
<p>The president posted on Truth Social about an effort to provide tax incentives to help revitalize the US entertainment industry, and about a Coast Guard rescue from a crash between two ships in Louisiana.</p>
<p>JetBlue customers scheduled to fly this evening should expect delays and cancellations, the airline said in a statement.</p>
<p>JetBlue has temporarily suspended all departures, the airline said, adding flights already in the air are continuing to destinations.</p>
<p>“Safety remains our top priority, and we are working closely with the FAA as we monitor the situation,” JetBlue said. The airline said customers should check flight status at <a href="http://jetblue.com">jetblue.com</a> or the JetBlue app for the latest information.</p>

<p>A mother and her 21-month-old daughter have been stranded at Chicago O’Hare International Airport for about 9 hours, she told CNN.</p>
<p>“All done,” her exhausted toddler repeated to her mom, Melissa Ogden.</p>
<p>“She’s very tired of this day,” Ogden said.</p>
<p>Ogden said she had been at the airport since 8 a.m. for her 10:30 a.m. flight to San Francisco, where she was traveling for work.</p>
<p>“Then we went through security and did all of the normal stuff, and then got on the plane, and then yeah, it took forever,” she said.</p>
<p>She was told their bags would be transported there without them.</p>
<p>Ogden said she hasn’t yet rebooked the flight for a later date.</p>
<p>“We just wait and see, because I mean there’s no way that if they get all this stuff figured out that a flight is going to go anytime soon,” she said. “There’s going to be a cascading effect.”</p>
<p>For now, she’s just grateful her family is safe.</p>
<p>“We’re just going to go home and just hope that everything gets better and it’s not a big deal,” she said.</p>

<p>No flights have taken off from Chicago O’Hare International Airport in over two hours, according to flight-tracking service Flightradar24, amid the Federal Aviation Administration’s nationwide ground stop.</p>
<p>Several other major US airports have also not seen any planes leave since before 4 p.m. ET, <a href="https://x.com/flightradar24/status/2121368841087574368">according</a> to a Flightradar24 post at 5:25 p.m.</p>
<p>The last recorded takeoffs at both New York’s John F. Kennedy International Airport and Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport were at 3:35 p.m., according to the tracker.</p>
