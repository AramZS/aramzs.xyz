<h3><a href="https://www.nytimes.com/live/2026/11/13/us/ground-stop-nationwide-flights-faa#nationwide-ground-stop-flights-faa">Here’s the latest.</a></h3>
<p>The Federal Aviation Administration announced a rare nationwide ground stop on Friday afternoon, in what appeared to be a major outage affecting its air traffic control system.</p>
<p>Sean Duffy, the secretary of transportation, confirmed the outage in a social media post, citing “a communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers.”</p>
<p>“FAA technicians are investigating the issue,” he added. “We will provide immediate updates as we have them."</p>
<p>The agency posted an advisory stating that “all airports are ground stopped,” citing “extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety” as the reasons.</p>
<p>“All departing traffic should remain ground stopped until further notice,” the official advisory read, adding that the landings of inbound international flights would be managed on a case-by-case basis.</p>
<p>Shortly after the advisory was posted, an F.A.A. website that lists air traffic control system notices appeared to have gone down, erasing dozens of notices that previously had been listed.</p>
