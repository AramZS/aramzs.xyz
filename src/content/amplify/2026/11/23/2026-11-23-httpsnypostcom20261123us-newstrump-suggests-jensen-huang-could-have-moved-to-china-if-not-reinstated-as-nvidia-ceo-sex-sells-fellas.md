<p>WASHINGTON — President Trump claimed Monday that<a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/23/business/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo-weeks-after-shocking-exit-over-chinese-spy-affair-scandals/"> Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang</a> could have become a leader in China’s artificial intelligence industry if he hadn’t been reinstated to the US firm — telling CNBC in a phone interview that the 63-year-old has “got a little blood there” despite being born in Taiwan.</p>
<p>“If we didn’t do this, I could see China coming in and saying to Jensen, ’Hey, how would you like to come to China?’ — He’s got a little [Chinese] blood in him. He’s got a little blood there — ‘How would you like to come to China and head up Huawei?’” Trump told CNBC in a phone interview, during which he called Huang, 63, a “national treasure” despite his affair with an alleged Chinese spy.</p>

<p>“If Jensen was angry enough, if he was treated badly, maybe he could do that.”</p>
<p><a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/23/us-news/trump-hails-jensen-huangs-reinstatement-as-nvidia-ceo/">Trump pressured Nvidia’s board</a> to <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/20/us-news/trump-backs-jensen-huangs-return-as-nvidia-ceo-says-he-discussed-with-xi/">restore the AI pioneer</a> <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/12/business/jensen-huang-ousted-as-nvidia-ceo-in-wake-of-chinese-spy-scandal-and-second-alleged-affair/">11 days after he resigned</a> — saying he gave the directors assurances that Huang wouldn’t face legal issues over his relationship with Alice Huang (no relation).</p>
<p>If Alice Huang or her Singapore-based Megaspeed firm violated US export laws by sending advanced Nvidia chips to China, “that wouldn’t be Jensen’s fault,” Trump argued.</p>

<p>The president, who returned late Thursday from a <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/17/us-news/trump-philippines-visit-live-updates/">trip to China</a> that included a <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/19/us-news/trump-knocks-old-school-diplomacy-as-he-urges-china-and-russia-to-reduce-nukes-during-summit/">trilateral summit</a> with his counterpart Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin, reiterated that “Jensen could take the next plane to China, and I guarantee you they would give him whatever he wanted.</p>
<p>“And then they’d automatically have you know all of the things. He never even suggested that. But I know how good China is, how professional they are, how cunning they are.”</p>
<p>Trump said during his nearly hour-long interview that he didn’t ask Xi if Alice Huang was a spy — a claim Beijing has denied though <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/13/business/howard-lutnick-to-testify-before-senate-on-nvidia-megaspeed-fiasco/">Megaspeed was under investigation</a> by Singapore’s government<a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/17/us-news/commerce-department-reveals-megaspeed-chip-smuggling-probe-was-launched-in-2024/"> and the US Commerce Department </a>for allegedly <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/16/business/more-than-20-of-nvidias-revenue-was-driven-by-china-last-year-report/">flouting US export restrictions</a> on AI chip shipping to China.</p>

<p>“You know, he’s in a class by himself. We would have lost him,” Trump said of Huang, who was sent to the US by his parents at age 9, cofounded Nvidia in 1993 and built it into one of the world’s <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/11/10/business/nvidia-on-track-for-470b-two-day-wipeout-as-ceo-jensen-huangs-affair-sparks-panic/">most valuable companies</a>.</p>
<p>“If I were President Xi, I’d say, ‘Hey, let’s get him over here real fast. He can have whatever he wants. We’ll give him Beijing. Okay, we’ll give him all of Beijing.’”</p>
<p>“Crazy things like that happen,” Trump said.</p>
<p>“I think he’s going to start off at an almost higher level. He’s apologized for indiscretion, which was unfortunate…. That’s what brought it to such a level because sex sells, fellas.”</p>
