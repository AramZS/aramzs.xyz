<p>NEW YORK (AP) — Customers reported difficulty accessing the online accounts at some major banks Monday.</p>
<p>Bank of America, Chase, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo and Zelle were listed by users as down or experiencing issues, according to the website downdetector.com.</p>
<p>Chase Bank’s services are experiencing a technical bug issue, not a cyber issue, a spokesperson said Monday.</p>
<p>“Due to a technical issue, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete but customers still have access to their accounts and funds,” a Chase spokesperson said in a statement. “We are working to resolve this as quickly as possible.”</p>
<p>The reports follow a <a href="https://apnews.com/article/microsoft-outage-outlook-email-365-recover-ca395c0f1e51af784b6d70afb6554f6a">major outage by Microsoft</a> that began Friday and lasted for hours which impacted services including Outlook, Teams and AI assistant Copilot.</p>
<p>Some Chase customers received a message that said “Some services aren’t available. We’re working on them. Please try back later.”</p>
