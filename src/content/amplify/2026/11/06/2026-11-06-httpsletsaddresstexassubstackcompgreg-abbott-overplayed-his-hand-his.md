<h2><strong>The tea</strong></h2>
<p>In the 2023 session, the Republican-controlled Legislature quietly passed <a href="https://www.texastribune.org/2024/06/11/texas-appeals-court-15th-abbott-appointees/">Senate Bill 1045</a>, creating a brand new, specialized appellate court called the 15th Court of Appeals. This created a court with three judges with statewide jurisdiction. It was packaged alongside a constitutional amendment, Proposition 13 of 2023, that was marketed to Texans as “specialized business courts for efficiency.” And yes, the ballot language was deliberately misleading. It talked about commercial disputes and efficient handling of corporate cases, blah blah, all fake marketing terminology. I mean, who who vote against efficient commercial litigation? Exactly. That’s why Texans waved it through.</p>
<p>Here’s the fine print. The 15th Court of Appeals doesn’t just handle corporate disputes, it handles every appeal in Texas that involves the state of Texas or any of its agencies. That means every challenge to an agency. That means every lawsuit against Greg Abbott personally, every lawsuit against Ken Paxton’s office, every challenge to a Dan Patrick culture-war bill. Do you see where I’m going with this?</p>
<p>Abbott and the Republican supermajority invented a <em>single statewide appellate court</em> that EVERY lawsuit against them and their biggest donors would have to pass through. It’s designed so that if the court ruled against them, they had a kill point they controlled the trigger on. On June 11, 2024, Abbott went and personally appointed <a href="https://www.ksat.com/news/texas/2024/06/11/abbott-appoints-first-judges-to-new-appeals-court-for-cases-involving-state-government-businesses/">Scott Brister as Chief Justice, Scott Field, and April Farris</a> to fill all three seats. Yes, they’re all his goons.</p>
<p>One advocacy group’s general counsel called the court <a href="https://texasstandard.org/stories/texas-legislature-passes-appeals-court-lawsuits-against-state/">“profoundly anti-democratic”</a> and warned it was a rescue vehicle for Republicans and mega-donors to get any lawsuit dismissed before it could reach the Texas Supreme Court. But of COURSE they dgaf because that’s exactly why they created it.</p>
<h2><strong>The thing he forgot</strong></h2>
<p>But here’s the plot twist. In Texas, appellate judges are elected by the people. Abbott’s three appointees got to serve on the brand new court, but ONLY until the next statewide election, which was Tuesday. That’s when the voters got to decide whether to confirm his picks or to send up somebody else for a full six-year term.</p>
<p>And thankfully, Texans voted out all three of them.</p>
<p>Brister, Field, Farris are donezo. The 15th Court of Appeals is now going to be run by three Democratic judges, elected to full six-year terms, who CAN’T be appointed, CAN’T be vacated, and CAN’T be replaced before 2032. They are locked in by the Texas Constitution. *insert evil MUAHAHAHAHAHAHA laugh here*</p>
