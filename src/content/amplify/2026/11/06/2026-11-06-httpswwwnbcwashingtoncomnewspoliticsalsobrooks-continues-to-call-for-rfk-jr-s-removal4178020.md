<p>U.S. Sen. Angela Alsobrooks sent a Facebook post Wednesday calling for the <a href="https://www.facebook.com/SenatorAlsobrooks/posts/pfbid0GCY3TTokbMx8FLdKVYPq4VbsAEV1vT9VRXdzuMVSzsqhFRxstghEVdcaFrfj1AWNl">resignation or removal of Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr</a>.</p>
<p>The Maryland Democrat's Facebook post reads, “The time is now. Kennedy must resign or be fired. End of discussion.”</p>
<p>Alsobrooks, a fierce critic of Kennedy, has called for the health secretary’s resignation for over a year.</p>

<p>In September 2025, a <a href="https://www.nbcwashington.com/news/local/democratic-senators-call-for-rfk-jr-to-be-removed-from-hhs/3984105/">group of more than 20 senators, including Alsobrooks, called for Kennedy's removal at Health and Human Services</a>, saying his actions undermined public health in the United States.</p>
<p>Alsobrooks filed a resolution of disapproval that September shortly after Kennedy testified during a lengthy hearing over upheaval at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and changes to COVID-19 vaccine guidance.</p>
<p>Alsobrooks has continued to call for Kennedy’s removal since she filed that resolution. In February, Alsobrooks and Sen. Ron Wyden, D-Oregon, released a <a href="https://www.finance.senate.gov/ranking-members-news/wyden-and-alsobrooks-update-report-chronicling-harmful-actions-rfk-jr-has-taken-in-last-365-days/">report chronicling his first year as secretary</a> and have continued to update the report with Kennedy’s actions.</p>

<p>During the September 2025 hearing, Kennedy said he didn’t know how many people had died from COVID-19.</p>
<p>Virginia Sen. Mark Warner asked Kennedy at the hearing if he accepted that a million Americans have died from COVID-19, to which Kennedy responded, "I don't know how many died."</p>
<p>"You're the secretary of Health and Human Services. You don't have any idea how many Americans died from COVID?" Warner replied.</p>
<p>"I don’t think anybody knows that, because there was so much data chaos coming out of the CDC and there were so many perverse incentives,” Kennedy said.</p>
<p>At the time, approximately 1.2 million Americans had died from the virus, according to both the <a href="https://covid.cdc.gov/covid-data-tracker/#trends_totaldeaths_select_00">U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention</a>, and the <a href="https://data.who.int/dashboards/covid19/deaths">World Health Organization</a>.</p>
<p>Before the hearing, Alsobrooks called for Kennedy's resignation during a rally on Capitol Hill.</p>
<p>"RFK claims that he wants to make America healthy again, but from day one he has systematically dismantled HHS through his anti-science, quack agenda," Alsobrooks said at the rally.</p>
<p>Alsobrooks also accused Kennedy of spreading anti-vaccine disinformation.</p>
<p>"He has undermined public trust in the systems, institutions, research and public health officials that protect our safety and well-being," she said.</p>
