<p>A writer who worked on <a href="https://thetab.com/tag/gta">GTA 6</a> has deleted their social media accounts following a wave of hate. Alexa D’Agostino was targeted by people who were against the inclusion of Lucia.</p>
<p>Alexa D’Agostino had been vocal about some of the inspirations behind Lucia, <a href="https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/grand-theft-auto-6-writer-leaves-social-media-following-online-harassment/">as per VideoGamesChronicle</a>, and had been active sharing videos of gameplay. However, now her account appears to have been taken down, and the handle is being used by a crypto enthusiast.</p>
<p>The publication has said wrongly informed GTA players had resurfaced some of her blog posts, and claimed Alexa ran a blog that encouraged women to “manipulate and take advantage of men”. However, she in fact ran a blog named The Year of Alexa, between 2015 and 2018, that advised women on being themselves around their male partner and his friends.</p>
<p>Off the back of this, more users began to blame what they saw as faults in GTA 6 on Alexa. They are said to have criticised the inclusion of <a href="https://thetab.com/2026/11/20/when-lucia-becomes-completely-playable-in-gta-6-and-you-swap-between-her-and-jason">Lucia as a playable character</a>, and having her as a strong protagonist.</p>

<p>“When it came to writing Lucia, I thought of the women I know,” Alexa told <a href="https://lovemagazine.net/articles/gta-vi-the-american-dream">Love Magazine</a> ahead of the game launching. “My mother was an immigrant who grew up in Newark, New Jersey, close to Lucia’s hometown of Liberty City. I thought about the way she raised me: Rowdy, kind, but not to take any sh*t. I related a lot to Lucia, and writing her came very naturally to me.</p>
<p>“When you are focusing on writing a fully realised person with their own wants, you’re going to get something perfectly imperfect-with clichés, but also undeniably real. That’s how you get a woman that can stand tall next to the likes of Arthur Morgan and Niko Bellic.”</p>
<p>Alexa D’Agostino also has credits on GTA 5, GTA Online and Red Dead Online.</p>
