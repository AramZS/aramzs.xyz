<p>The organisers of a Christmas market have cancelled all bookings and promised to refund stallholders following a backlash to AI-generated promotional materials.</p>
<p>David Rowland, who produced the images for Kernow Christmas Market in Pool, Cornwall, said claims they did not match reality were a "lesson learned".</p>
<p>Images showed rows of stalls beneath Christmas decorations, fairy lights and festive displays for the event last weekend. But photographs taken by visitors showed a mostly empty hall with only a handful of traders, little Christmas decoration and buckets on the floor collecting water from a leaking roof.</p>
<p>Rowlands Attractions said it was "gutted" things "didn't go as planned".</p>
<p>It said: "The criticism we have faced and the words that have been seen on social media and across the news make us look at the world slightly differently.</p>
<p>"Kindness and compassion goes out of the window so easily to knock something that people don't care to understand the full story of."</p>

<p>The company added: "As a result of this, we are going to cancel and refund all current and future bookings and Kernow Christmas Market will take no further bookings for 2026.</p>
<p>"Any current bookings will be fully refunded and if any traders wish to continue to trade they must book direct with Pool Market."</p>
<p>Prior to the cancellation announcement, one stallholder, Ceilidh Hurrell, said she felt "let down" after paying £180 to trade at the event.</p>
<p>Hurrell, who runs Nails, Hair and More in Falmouth, paid for a pitch at the Christmas market, which formed part of the wider Winter Wonderland attraction with more stalls, Santa's grotto, an ice rink and fun fair.</p>
<p>She said she had expected a decorated venue to "entice people in for Christmas".</p>
<p>Instead, she said traders arrived to find no advertisement for the stallholders and no decorations.</p>
<p>"It was just an empty space. There was nothing to suggest that there's a market going on," Hurrell said.</p>

<p>Sammie Retallick, who attended with her partner on Saturday expecting a festive shopping experience, said they found "only about five stalls", as well as no decorations, Christmas music or lights.</p>
<p>Retallick said she felt sorry for traders who had invested money and time in the event.</p>
<p>"It's heartbreaking for them," she said.</p>
<p>Another trader claimed there were no signs directing visitors to the market area.</p>
<p>They added: "People turning up to the Winter Wonderland wouldn't have been aware we were even there.</p>
<p>"They've told us we're going to be part of this massive Winter Wonderland, 50-plus stalls, visitors of 2,500 people plus a day, then we get shoved in a dark corner of a warehouse."</p>

<p>Prior to the cancellation announcement, Rowland, who organises Winter Wonderland, said he took on the promotion of the market to help the Pool Market location.</p>
<p>"I thought it would be nice to help them," he said.</p>
<p>He said the AI images were "so easy to do" and gave a "rough idea" of what people could expect.</p>
<p>"You are never going to make it look exactly like the picture," he said.</p>
<p>"It's a lesson learned."</p>
<p>Pool Market has been approached for comment.</p>
<p>Kernow Christmas Market said on Facebook the event was organised and operated by Pool Market, running alongside Cornwall Winter Wonderland.</p>
<p>It said: "We are a small, family run business that started many years ago here in Cornwall.</p>
<p>"This year, as an add on from Cornwalls Winter Wonderland we thought we would try and do a good deed and bring a little festive magic into Pool Market and for those who live locally in Cornwall.</p>
<p>"The agreement was always with Pool Market to do their bit and we do ours. We advertise it, book the stalls and they do the rest- such as signage, decorating and greeting the traders and helping them with queries.</p>
<p>"We are gutted, just as much as you all that things didn't go as planned."</p>
<p>It said Cornwall Winter Wonderland will remain open until 3 January and Rowlands' focus will remain "always remain on providing the very best experience for those who visit".</p>
<p>It added: "Entry is free, kindness is free, and we will always be grateful for those who continue to recognise the efforts, cost and dedication that goes on behind the scenes."</p>
