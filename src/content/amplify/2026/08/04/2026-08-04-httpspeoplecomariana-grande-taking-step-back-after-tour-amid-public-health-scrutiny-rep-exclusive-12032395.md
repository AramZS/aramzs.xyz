<p><a data-component="link" data-ordinal="1" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" href="https://people.com/tag/ariana-grande/">Ariana Grande</a> is taking a break.</p>
<p>After her <a data-component="link" data-ordinal="1" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" href="https://people.com/ariana-grande-announces-eternal-sunshine-tour-2026-11799854">Eternal Sunshine tour</a> wraps in London on Sept. 1, the singer, 33, will be stepping away from the public eye, as scrutiny surrounding her health and body intensifies.</p>
<p>A representative for Grande tells PEOPLE exclusively, “Ariana will be taking a step back from visibility after she completes the Eternal Sunshine Tour.”</p>
<p>“She looks forward to finishing the tour and ending it on a high note, both healthily and happily, and then taking a much-deserved break from public-facing work and appearances, which has led to endless, ongoing public scrutiny,” the rep continues. “This tour has been a beautiful experience for her. She loves her fans and has loved every minute of this tour so much.”</p>
<p>A source close to Grande tells PEOPLE of the singer, “She performs a very physical show, and there is a lot of athleticism involved. She performs healthily and successfully at a very high level night after night.”</p>

<p>Grande will also no longer be appearing in the <a data-component="link" data-ordinal="1" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" href="https://people.com/ariana-grande-and-jonathan-bailey-sunday-in-the-park-with-george-revival-11885115">West End revival of Stephen Sondheim’s <em>Sunday in the Park with George</em></a>, PEOPLE confirms.</p>
<p>It was scheduled to open at London’s Barbican Centre next summer, with Grande’s <em>Wicked</em> costar <a data-component="link" data-ordinal="1" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" href="https://people.com/jonathan-bailey-8742153">Jonathan Bailey</a> starring opposite her.</p>
<p>The source close to Grande tells PEOPLE she “cannot wait to support the brilliant team bringing this project to life.”</p>
<p>Grande’s physical appearance and overall health have been a topic of public discussion in recent years.</p>
<p>Most recently, after the <a data-component="link" data-ordinal="1" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="externalLink" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=afSgBNwmZrQ">music video</a> for her latest single, <a data-component="link" data-ordinal="2" data-source="inlineLink" data-type="internalLink" href="https://people.com/ariana-grande-wields-chainsaw-at-critics-while-taking-on-complex-relationship-with-fame-in-bloody-petal-video-12031985">“Petal”</a> — off her recently released album of the same name on July 31— was released, the public discourse ramped up.</p>

<p>The Grammy Award winner has hit back at commentary surrounding her body on various occasions.</p>
<p>In her song “Yes, And?” off her 2024 album <em>Eternal Sunshine</em>, Grande sings at one point, “My face is sitting, I don’t need no disguise / Don’t comment on my body, do not reply / Your business is yours, and mine is mine.”</p>

<p>As for her new album, <em>Petal</em>, the insider says, “This album is a fight song, an anthem about the love-hate relationship with the public and their toxicity.”</p>
<p>“This album is not about any romantic partner or breakup in that way,” the source adds.</p>
<p>Grande’s Eternal Sunshine tour kicked off in June in Oakland, Calif. It has since made stops across the country, in cities including Los Angeles, Atlanta, New York City and more.</p>
<p>Grande will wrap her U.S. dates this week in Chicago, before performing a set of shows in London.</p>
