<p>When I created my list of the <a href="https://www.purewow.com/entertainment/best-shows-july-2026">best shows of July 2026</a>, I included must-watch options like <em><a href="https://www.purewow.com/entertainment/five-star-weekend-renewed-season-2">The Five Star Weekend</a></em> and the <em>Legally Blonde</em> prequel, <em><a href="https://www.purewow.com/entertainment/elle-prime-video-review-legally-blonde-prequel">Elle</a></em>. But one incredible new show dropped in the final days of the month, and I'm sad to say I missed it—until now.</p>
<p><em><a href="https://go.skimresources.com/?id=27667X859343&amp;url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.hulu.com%2Fseries%2Ffurious-5d4924b7-1cde-47c3-8852-f30bdd7fe979&amp;xcust=furious-hulu-review">Furious</a></em>, starring <em>Shameless</em> actress Emmy Rossum, is a new, under-the-radar show on <a href="https://go.skimresources.com/?id=27667X859343&amp;url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.hulu.com%2Fwelcome&amp;xcust=furious-hulu-review">Hulu</a>, and I highly recommend a watch. In fact, it's my favorite series on streaming at the moment.</p>
<figure><img alt="furious hulu review 1600x1067" src="https://assets.purewow.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/furious-hulu-review-1600x1067.jpg"/><figcaption>furious hulu review 1600x1067</figcaption></figure>
<p><em>Furious</em> follows an NYPD-officer-turned-FBI-agent (Rossum) who makes the career switch after experiencing some horrific personal trauma. In a pencil-pushing position at the FBI, she's drawn into a case involving a female serial killer (Lola Petticrew) who seems to be targeting men connected to sex crimes and sex trafficking.</p>
<p>Rossum is phenomenal as our flawed heroine, and Petticrew is truly terrifying as our wig-donning murderess. It's clear that each of these characters has a chaotic and complicated past, making them intensely interesting in this cat-and-mouse story. The show feels like <em>Killing Eve</em> (minus the romantic relationship between detective and killer). And with plenty of dark twists, I'm fully invested in the season.</p>
<p>I'm not alone in my love of the series. <em>Furious</em> is currently the <a href="https://go.skimresources.com/?id=27667X859343&amp;url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.hulu.com%2Fhub%2Ftv%2Fcollections%2F9979&amp;xcust=furious-hulu-review">number one show on Hulu</a> (far ahead of Ryan Murphy's <em>The Shards</em>, which was a giant miss for me). It also has a whopping 98 percent fresh rating on <em><a href="https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/furious">Rotten Tomatoes</a></em> from critics, and a nearly-as-impressive 87 percent rating from viewers. Let's just hope Hulu realizes the hit it has on its hands and gives it a <a href="https://www.purewow.com/entertainment/renewed-shows-five-star-weekend-american-idol-wonder-man-canceled">season two renewal</a>.</p>
<p>The first five episodes of <em>Furious</em> are out now, with new episodes dropping each Monday until the August 31 finale.</p>
