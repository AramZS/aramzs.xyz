(Eric Lee/Reuters)</figcaption></figure><p>As a defense hawk, I’ve long been in favor of more military spending — up to and including when the defense budget hit <a href="https://breakingdefense.com/2025/04/a-1-trillion-defense-budget-trump-hegseth-say-its-happening/" rel="">$1 trillion</a> this year. In inflation-adjusted terms, that 50 percent increase would result in the <a href="https://www.csis.org/analysis/unpacking-15-trillion-fy-2027-defense-budget-topline" rel="">largest defense budget ever</a> — exceeding even the World War II peak.</p></body></html>
