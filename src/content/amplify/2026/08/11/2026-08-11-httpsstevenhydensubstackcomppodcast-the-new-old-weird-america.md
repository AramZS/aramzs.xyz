<p>Twisted Teens is one of my favorite bands of 2026. The New Orleans garage-rock duo has already put out two very worthy albums this year — <em>Blame The Clown </em>in the late winter and the even better <em>Florida Water Blues </em>this summer. And it sounds like they have a third LP — a break-up album the band likens to <em>Blood On The Tracks — </em>this fall. I figured it was worth reaching out to singer-songwriter Caspian Hollywell over Instagram to see if he would want to talk, and thankfully he agreed. Once we hopped on Zoom, I was pleased to find that Caspian is just as lively in conversation as he is on record. We talked about his long, winding journey in life and music, including his time riding the rails (literally), his experience playing and learning about old time traditional music, and how he’s now centered on writing “bangers” while subtly aping the visual aesthetic of Cheap Trick. Great band, great conversation!</p>
