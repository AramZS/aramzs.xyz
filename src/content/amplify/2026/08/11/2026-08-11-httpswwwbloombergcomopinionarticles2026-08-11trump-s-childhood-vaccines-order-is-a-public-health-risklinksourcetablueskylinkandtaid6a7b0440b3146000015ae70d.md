<p>President Donald Trump’s <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-10/trump-plans-to-sign-order-on-splitting-up-combined-measles-shot">proposed sweeping changes</a> to the childhood vaccine schedule, whether ever actually implemented or not, are yet another affront to public health in the US.</p>
<p>No new evidence is being offered to justify the proposed overhaul, which <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-08-10/trump-wants-to-split-up-the-mmr-vaccine-video">arrived via executive order</a> on Monday and calls for cutting back the number of vaccines children receive and instructing states to review their immunization laws — probably because there is no new data to support it. Instead, the change is being pushed forward in the name of protecting parental authority, while likely making it harder for parents to protect their kids from preventable diseases.</p>
