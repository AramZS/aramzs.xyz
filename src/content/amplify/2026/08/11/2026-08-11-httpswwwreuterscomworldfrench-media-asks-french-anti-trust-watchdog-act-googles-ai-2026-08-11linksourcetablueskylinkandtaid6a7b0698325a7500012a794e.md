<p>PARIS, Aug 11 (Reuters) - French media have asked the ​country's anti-trust watchdog to ‌act on Google's <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/GOOGL.O">(GOOGL.O)</a> artificial intelligence-generated article summaries ​which they say ​deprive them of traffic, ⁠a press trade association ​body said on ​Tuesday.</p>
<p>The media have called on the regulator to make ​a decision similar ​to <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/french-antitrust-watchdog-orders-meta-resume-talks-with-media-groups-over-2026-07-08/">the one in July</a> that ‌ordered ⁠Meta <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/META.O">(META.O)</a> to put forward a payment plan and resume talks with ​traditional ​media ⁠that are seeking fees for the ​use of their ​content ⁠by platforms' AI tools.</p>
<p>There was no immediate ⁠comment ​from Google ​regarding the situation.</p>
