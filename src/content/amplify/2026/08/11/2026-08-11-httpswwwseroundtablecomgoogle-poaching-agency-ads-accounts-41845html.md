<p>I feel like a broken record and that Google simply does not care but again, I am seeing numerous reports from the PPC community about Google representatives attempting to poach their client accounts from them.</p>
<p>We all get the emails and to be fair to Google, you can now <a href="https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-expert-book-call-emails-unsubscribe-41617.html">unsubscribe from those emails</a>, but maybe there should be a way for Google to know an agency is managing the account and not send these emails in the first place?</p>
<p>We've covered these types of complaints many many times, over <a href="https://www.seroundtable.com/google-adwords-stealing-clients-17735.html">a dozen years ago</a> and Google even <a href="https://www.seroundtable.com/google-ads-will-address-third-party-concerns-33573.html">promised to make changes</a> back in 2022. But yet, we still see this happening and PPCers complaining that it is happening.</p>
<p>Here are two recent complaints...</p>
<p>Bia Camargo posted on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bia-camargo_googleads-ppc-digitalmarketing-share-7492366650306920448-AZFs/">LinkedIn</a> the other day and wrote, "What the actual f&amp;$@ is happening? One of my clients, an account that is actively managed by an agency, received this email directly from Google. Not through me. Not through our Google agency representative. Directly to the client."</p>
