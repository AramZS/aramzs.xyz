<p>Halo Studios recently released <a href="https://insider-gaming.com/tag/halo-campaign-evolved/">Halo: Campaign Evolved</a>. It’s the first game in the long-running Xbox franchise to be released on a Sony PlayStation platform. Unfortunately, following the game’s release, the studio appears to be conducting layoffs.</p>

<h2>Halo Studios Staff Share Layoff Announcements on LinkedIn</h2>
<figure><img alt="master chief holding onto vehicle in halo campaign evolved" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" src="https://insider-gaming.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-Long-Is-Halo-Campaign-Evolved-Game-Length-Total-Hours-Explained-guide.jpg" srcset="https://insider-gaming.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-Long-Is-Halo-Campaign-Evolved-Game-Length-Total-Hours-Explained-guide-1024x576.jpg 1024w, https://insider-gaming.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-Long-Is-Halo-Campaign-Evolved-Game-Length-Total-Hours-Explained-guide-300x169.jpg 300w, https://insider-gaming.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-Long-Is-Halo-Campaign-Evolved-Game-Length-Total-Hours-Explained-guide-770x433.jpg 770w, https://insider-gaming.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-Long-Is-Halo-Campaign-Evolved-Game-Length-Total-Hours-Explained-guide-1536x864.jpg 1536w, https://insider-gaming.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/How-Long-Is-Halo-Campaign-Evolved-Game-Length-Total-Hours-Explained-guide.jpg 1920w"/><figcaption>Credit to Halo Studios</figcaption></figure>
<p>Several LinkedIn posts reflect this, with multiple people stating that their time at the studio has ended. <a href="https://lnkd.in/p/efwzxGW9">Nick Treitman</a>, a producer at the studio, has noted that he was recently affected by a round of layoffs.</p>
<blockquote> <p>After 6.5 years working on Halo and dodging just as many rounds of layoffs – it has finally caught up to me. I have nothing but love for Halo and the folks at the studio, this is just a tough reality in the gaming industry right now.</p> <p>I am open to new roles as a Producer/Project Manager working remotely or hybrid in the Seattle area.</p> <p>During my time, I have had the honor of supporting multiple amazing teams ranging from Art, Networking, Localization, Narrative, and being the Product Owner of Halo Infinites social multiplayer pillar.</p> </blockquote>
<figure><div class="rw-embed-wrapper"><embed src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/3t3kF5rlrOM?feature=oembed" type="video/mp4"/></div></figure>
<p><a href="https://lnkd.in/p/e5NFDzvv">Paul Morris</a> was listed as a scrum master, and his time at the studio has also come to an end. However, it is noted that he was a contractor.</p>
<blockquote> <p>Today, I learned my time at Halo Studios will be coming to an end following the successful launch of our latest game, Halo: Campaign Evolved.</p> <p>I know this is one of the most challenging job markets our industry has seen, and I know the road ahead won’t be easy.</p> <p>But I’m not done, and my purpose has not changed.</p> </blockquote>
<p><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/caleblawrence-dev/">Caleb Lawrence, </a>a Software Engineer at the studio, recently listed himself as available for work as well.</p>
