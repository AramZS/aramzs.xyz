<p>Aug 10 (Reuters) - Taylor Farms said on Sunday it was recalling prepared food items containing jalapenos from ‌retailers, including Walmart, Kroger and Whole Foods in some states, over concerns about potential salmonella contamination.</p>
<p>The California-based supplier said it had voluntarily recalled the products, including salsas and guacamole, after being notified that <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/cdc-says-345-sickened-salmonella-outbreak-tied-jalapeno-peppers-2026-08-05/">Coast Citrus Distributors</a> was recalling the fresh peppers.</p>
<p>Taylor Farms products were also recalled from Target, ​Trader Joe's and Scarborough, Maine-based supermarket chain Hannaford across about 20 states.</p>
<p>Taylor Farms said in a statement it ​was no longer sourcing from a farmer in Sinaloa, Mexico, that was identified as the ⁠potential source of the salmonella outbreak.</p>
<p>The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) and the FDA <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/business/healthcare-pharmaceuticals/cdc-says-345-sickened-salmonella-outbreak-tied-jalapeno-peppers-2026-08-05/">said</a> earlier this week they were ​investigating the salmonella outbreak, which had sickened 345 people across 27 states and led to 36 hospitalizations.</p>
<p>The U.S. investigation into ​the salmonella outbreak identified restaurants including Chipotle Mexican Grill <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/CMG.N">(CMG.N)</a> and Qdoba, a Mexican-style restaurant chain, as having received jalapeños imported by Coast Citrus Distributors.</p>
<p>Both restaurants stopped using them, while Coast Citrus recalled the remaining product and notified customers.</p>
<p>Salmonella infections can cause diarrhea, fever and stomach cramps and ​can be particularly severe in young children, older adults and people with weakened immune systems.</p>
<p>Taylor Farms said it was unaware ​of any reported illnesses linked to the voluntarily recalled items.</p>
<p>Walmart said it had removed the impacted products from its stores and implemented ‌a sales ⁠block at its registers as well as online. Kroger, Target, Whole Foods, Trader Joe's and Hannaford-parent Ahold Delhaize did not immediately respond to a Reuters request for comment.</p>
<p>Taylor was already facing heightened scrutiny over an outbreak of <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/michigan-salad-warnings-ease-cyclospora-keeps-shoppers-grocers-edge-2026-08-08/">cyclosporiasis</a>, which causes severe diarrhea.</p>
<p>The U.S. FDA said it had linked the cyclosporiasis to Taylor Farms products from central Mexico, although the company said the ​regulator had not confirmed positive ​sample tests for cyclospora on ⁠its lettuce as of August 5.</p>
<p>Taylor Farms said it had voluntarily suspended all iceberg lettuce sourcing and production from central Mexico and commissioned a safety review.</p>
<p>The current cyclosporiasis outbreak is ​among the largest caused by <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/us-restaurant-chains-hit-by-foodborne-illness-outbreaks-2026-08-06/">foodborne illness</a> in recent U.S. history, and health authorities continue to ​look for sources ⁠of the illness beyond iceberg lettuce.</p>
<p>According to NielsenIQ data, U.S. fresh iceberg lettuce unit sales in the four weeks ended July 25 fell 15.3% from a year earlier, following an 11.1% decline in the four weeks ended June 27.</p>
<p>The cyclosporiasis outbreak, which ⁠led to ​recalls at Taco Bell, broadly hit restaurant traffic as consumers turned wary ​of dining out and chains such as <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/business/chipotle-mexican-grill-pulls-jalapenos-linked-minnesota-salmonella-cases-2026-08-04/">Chipotle </a>and <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/salad-chain-sweetgreen-shares-slide-cyclosporiasis-fears-prompt-forecast-cut-2026-08-07/">Sweetgreen </a>have flagged a hit to sales.</p>
