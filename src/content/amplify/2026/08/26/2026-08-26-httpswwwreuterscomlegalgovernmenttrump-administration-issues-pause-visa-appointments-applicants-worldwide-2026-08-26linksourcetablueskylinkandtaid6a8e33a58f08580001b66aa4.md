<p>WASHINGTON, Aug 25 (Reuters) - President <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/us/donald-trump/">Donald Trump's</a> administration has ​issued a pause on visa appointments for applicants around the world during ‌an ongoing immigration crackdown by the U.S. government in the Republican leader's second term in the White House.</p>
<p>A U.S. State Department spokesperson said on Tuesday that it launched a global training initiative at ​all U.S. embassies and consulates worldwide and that appointments for visa services ​will be adjusted to accommodate the training.</p>
<p>Trump has pursued an aggressive deportation ⁠drive and immigration crackdown that includes revocations of visas and green cards and rejection ​of applications over a. range of reasons like <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-screen-social-media-immigrants-rights-advocates-raise-concerns-2025-04-09/">political opinions</a> and <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/pro-palestinian-foreigners-us-arrested-by-trump-administration-ordered-be-2025-06-20/">pro-Palestinian protests</a> against U.S. ally <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/israel-hamas/">Israel's ​assault on Gaza</a>.</p>
<p>He says the crackdown aims to improve domestic security.</p>
<p>The crackdown has faced <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-judge-strikes-down-policy-suspending-immigrant-visa-processing-75-nations-2026-08-22/">some legal setbacks</a>. A U.S. judge on Friday struck down a Trump administration policy <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/us/us-suspend-visa-processing-75-nations-next-week-fox-news-reports-2026-01-14/">suspending the issuance</a> of immigrant visas to applicants ​from 75 countries, saying that the policy exceeded Secretary of State Marco Rubio's statutory ​authority.</p>
<p>The State Department did not specify details on the training and its timeline, beyond saying the ‌training aimed ⁠to help consular officers screen out applicants deemed likely to become dependent on U.S. public benefits and to ensure evaluation of visa applicants "comprehensively and consistently."</p>
<p>The pause on visa appointments was reported earlier by the Financial Times, which said immigrant visa applicants with scheduled ​interviews at U.S. embassies ​and consulates have ⁠received emails that their appointments were being rescheduled and that they would receive future notice of a new date.</p>
<p>Trump's immigration crackdown has ​been widely condemned by human rights groups as being discriminatory and ​in violation ⁠of free speech and due process rights. Rights groups also say the crackdown has created an unsafe environment in the U.S., especially for ethnic minorities who have raised concerns about <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-crackdown-protests-immigration-led-islamophobia-muslim-group-says-2026-03-10/">racial ⁠profiling</a>.</p>
<p>While ​Trump campaigned in 2024 on a platform of stopping ​illegal immigration, his administration has also made legal immigration more difficult - for example, by <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/world/us/trump-set-expand-immigration-crackdown-2026-despite-brewing-backlash-2025-12-21/">imposing new and expensive fees</a> ​for applicants of certain work visas.</p>
