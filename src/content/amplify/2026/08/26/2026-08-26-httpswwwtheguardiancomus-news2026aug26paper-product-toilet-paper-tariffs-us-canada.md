<h2 data-gu-name="standfirst">Paper products among hardest-hit sectors after trade negotiations broke down, with 25-50% tariffs estimated </h2>
<p>Using the bathroom or having a cry is about to become more expensive for North Americans as the <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news">US</a> and <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/canada">Canada</a> enter a full-fledged trade war that threatens to flush away decades of peaceful trading between the two nations.</p>
<p>After trade negotiations <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/23/canada-us-collapsed-trade-talks-negotiation-breakdown-analysis">broke down</a> between the two countries <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/23/donald-trump-lashes-out-after-us-canada-talks-devolve-into-trade-war">last weekend</a>, Mark Carney, the Canadian prime minister, <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/22/canada-tariffs-trump-trade-deal-talks-fail">vowed</a> to match US tariffs “dollar for dollar” and <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/aug/25/canada-retaliatory-tariffs-on-range-of-us-goods">unveiled</a> a <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/international-trade-finance-policy/canadas-response-us-tariffs/complete-list-us-products-subject-to-counter-tariffs.html">list</a> of nearly 900 American goods that will face 25% to 50% tariffs starting on 8 September.</p>
<p>Paper products are among the hardest-hit sectors, with <a data-component="auto-linked-tag" data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/canada">Canada</a> threatening to put tariffs of between 25% and 50% on “toilet paper or face tissue stock” from 8 September in retaliation for a 50% hike from Washington DC.</p>
<p>Though American toilet paper and tissues are often made domestically, they <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/2019/mar/01/canada-boreal-forest-toilet-paper-us-climate-change-impact-report">heavily rely</a> on lumber-rich Canada for raw materials. Procter &amp; Gamble, the owner of Charmin toilet paper, <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.cnn.com/2025/07/29/business/tide-charmin-price-increases">said</a> last year that it would have to increase prices amid tariffs that were in place at the time.</p>
<p>The US imported $328m worth of toilet paper from Canada in 2024, according to the <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/USA/year/2024/tradeflow/Imports/partner/ALL/product/481810">World Bank</a>, making it by far the largest exporter of the product to the US. Retailers <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.housedigest.com/1623349/where-who-makes-kirkland-toilet-paper-costco/">including Costco</a> source much of their paper products from the country.</p>
<p>The US accounts for more than 20% of global tissue consumption despite having only 4% of the world’s population. The average American <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.statista.com/chart/15676/cmo-toilet-paper-consumption/">uses 141 rolls</a> of toilet paper per year, making them No 1 for No 2s globally, just ahead of Germans, each using an average of 134 rolls annually.</p>
<p>It’s not just paper products that could rise in price. The <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/commentisfree/2026/aug/25/canadians-trade-war-bullied-donald-trump-alliance-rupture">trade war</a> highlights the deep economic ties shared between the two countries as consumers continue to worry about inflation on both sides of the border. <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/trump-tariffs">US tariffs</a> are specifically affecting Canadian liquor, including popular whiskey brands Crown Royal and Canadian Club, which are currently under a 50% tariff. While Canada has not introduced a tariff on American liquor, most Canadian provinces have introduced their own bans on American alcohol, in retaliation for earlier tariffs on Canadian products. <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/donaldtrump">Donald Trump</a> used these province-wide bans on American alcohol as part of his <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/07/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-imposes-additional-tariffs-on-canada/">legal justification</a> for his new tariffs against Canada.</p>


<p>Carney has asked province leaders to consider putting American liquor back on the shelves, though as the Nova Scotia premier Tim Houston <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/canada-us-trade-talks-tariffs-9.7312405">told</a> CBC News: “Whether Nova Scotians or Canadians will actually buy it when it’s back on the shelves, that’s a whole other discussion.”</p>

<p>Trump also said that his new tariffs are in retaliation for Canadian levies against the <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/business/2021/may/27/us-canada-trade-tariffs-dairy-imports">American dairy industry</a>. He <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jul/20/trump-canada-tariffs">introduced</a> a 50% tariff on nearly all Canadian dairy products, with the exception of Canadian cheese.</p>
<p>In response, Canada has a 50% tariff on American dairy products, along with a 25% tariff on American cheese.</p>
<p>Canada also took a swing at the US fishing industry, with a 25% tariff on American fish and seafood, including frozen lobster. The move prompted Republican senator Susan Collins, facing a tough re-election battle in lobster-capital Maine, to call Trump’s latest move <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6047210-collins-trump-canada-tariffs/">“a mistake”</a>.</p>
<p>A 25% <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/24/canada-tariff-increase-trump-auto">tariff on Canadian cars and auto parts</a> is set to affect the American auto industry, which relies heavily on Canadian parts for manufacturing. Trump has threatened to <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/24/canada-tariff-increase-trump-auto">double the tariff</a> to 50% if a deal isn’t made by 1 January 2027.</p>
<p>With negotiations down the drain, it’s unclear if or when the tariffs will be lifted. But the Trump administration is adamant that <a data-link-name="in body link" href="https://www.mediaite.com/politics/trump-official-insists-theres-no-possible-way-trade-war-with-canada-will-impact-us/">“there’s no possible way”</a> the trade dispute with Canada will affect US consumers, according to Jamieson Greer, the US trade representative.</p>
<p>“The fundamentals are good,” he said. “I don’t think this is going to affect anything.”</p>
