<p>Did the American Historical Association ask a corporate lobbying firm for advice on how to silence its members?</p>
<p>If you happened to be in Chicago this past January for the Annual Meeting of the American Historical Association (AHA), an unusual session might have caught your eye, scheduled for conference prime time — Saturday, just after lunch. The AHA’s Professional Division had invited “Jess Venable, Thorn Run Partners,” to chair and be the only speaker at an event called “The Historian as Advocate: A Strategic Seminar on Influencing Policy.”</p>
