<p><a href="https://www.tmz.com/people/tim-curry/"><strong>Tim Curry</strong></a> -- the legendary film and TV actor best known for playing Dr. Frank-N-Furter in the cult classic "The Rocky Horror Picture Show" -- has died ... TMZ has learned.</p>
<p>Sources tell TMZ the actor -- who had been plagued with serious health issues for more than a decade -- passed away late Tuesday night at his Los Angeles home.</p>
<p>Law enforcement sources tell TMZ ... police responded to his house at 11:23 PM Tuesday night. We're told there is no foul play suspected in the case.</p>
<p><a data-container-ref="tmz:article:975ad1ca-651e-4133-8b53-2bbf54b3d56c" data-context='{"section":"permalink","name":"image"}' data-node-ref="tmz:image-asset:image_jpg_20260826_bce5b16fb9c24e9abfb2ac468c8a66dd"><figure><img alt="tim curry in Rocky Horror Picture Show getty 1" src="https://imagez.tmz.com/image/bc/4by3/2026/08/26/bce5b16fb9c24e9abfb2ac468c8a66dd_md.jpg"/><figcaption>tim curry in Rocky Horror Picture Show getty 1</figcaption></figure></a></p>
<p>Tim got his start in Hollywood in the late 1960s ... before catching his big break in "The Rocky Horror Picture Show."</p>
<p>While initially ignored by critics, the film developed a second life as a cultural event in the "midnight movie" space ... much like "The Room" would decades later.</p>
<p><a data-container-ref="tmz:article:975ad1ca-651e-4133-8b53-2bbf54b3d56c" data-context='{"section":"permalink","name":"image"}' data-node-ref="tmz:image-asset:image_jpg_20260826_772b4d79714b4ed5bd100693b519d503"><figure><img alt="tim curry in it shutterstock 1" src="https://imagez.tmz.com/image/77/4by3/2026/08/26/772b4d79714b4ed5bd100693b519d503_md.jpg"/><figcaption>tim curry in it shutterstock 1</figcaption></figure></a></p>
<p>Fans may also recognize Tim as the terrifying clown Pennywise from the 1990 adaptation of Stephen King's "It" and his role as Mr. Hector in "Home Alone 2: Lost in New York."</p>
<p>Tim's other credits include "Annie," "The Hunt for Red October," "Muppet Treasure Island," "Addams Family Reunion," and "Scary Movie 2."</p>
<figure><div class="rw-embed-wrapper"><embed src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/?enablejsapi=1&amp;origin=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tmz.com&amp;widgetid=1&amp;forigin=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.tmz.com%2F2026%2F08%2F26%2Ftim-curry-dead%2F&amp;aoriginsup=1&amp;vf=1" type="video/mp4"/></div></figure>
<p>While always an active performer -- both in his film roles and onstage as a theater actor -- health issues slowed Tim down in recent years. He suffered a massive stroke in 2012, which required brain surgery.</p>
<p>Last year -- around the time he released his memoir "Vagabond" -- Tim stated he was still unable to walk.</p>
<figure><img alt="082626_tim_curry_last_time_kal" src="https://imagez.tmz.com/image/c0/16by9/2026/08/26/c02e1ea7f0244e6daed6180392fa159a_md.png"/></figure>
<p>We last caught up with Tim in 2017 ... when we asked him what he thought of the <strong>Bill Skarsgård</strong>-led "It" film.</p>
<p>Tim was 80 years old.</p>
