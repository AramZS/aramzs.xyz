<p>Okay, so what will Kit Harington say when he is inevitably asked by a reasonable journalist about aligning himself with a transphobic nut? He’ll have to figure it out soon because the <a href="https://www.vulture.com/tv/game-of-thrones/"><em>Game of Thrones</em></a> and <a href="https://www.vulture.com/article/industry-season-4-hbo-premiere-review.html"><em>Industry</em></a> star is joining the cast of HBO’s upcoming <em>Harry Potter</em> series, per <a href="https://deadline.com/2026/08/kit-harington-harry-potter-gilderoy-lockhart-nicholas-hoult-1237048935/">Deadline</a>. He’ll play Gilderoy Lockhart, the pompous wizard who teaches the kids Defense Against the Dark Arts in their second year. Harington is familiar with the role — he played the same part in the audiobook adaptation <em>Harry Potter: The Full-Cast Audio Editions,</em> which was released earlier this year. “I read it and, like so many generations, I was entranced and then I was a huge fan all through my teenage years,” he said while promoting that project.</p>
<p>Less than two weeks ago, Nicholas Hoult was announced as Gilderoy. Deadline claimed there had been “scheduling issues” with the actor, though how the scheduling was worked out, then immediately lost, is confusing. Hoult did <a href="https://www.them.us/entertainment/tv-and-movies/nicholas-hoult-harry-potter">come under fire</a> for choosing to work on a project that benefits Rowling, an avowed transphobe who regularly donates to anti-trans causes. Now, he’s no longer involved and gets to shunt those questions off to Harington instead. Harington, meanwhile, will be able to uphold his title as the king of HBO.</p>
