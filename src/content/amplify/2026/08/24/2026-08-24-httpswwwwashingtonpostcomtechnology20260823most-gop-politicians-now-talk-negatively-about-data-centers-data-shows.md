<p>In the past six months, Republicans’ public statements about data centers have turned sharply negative, according to a Washington Post analysis.</p>
<p>Sen. Jon Husted once promoted data centers as all upside for his state of Ohio. “Data centers are essential to our digital lives,” the Republican said in 2024, praising a planned Google project that he claimed would establish “central Ohio as an important tech hub in America.”</p>
