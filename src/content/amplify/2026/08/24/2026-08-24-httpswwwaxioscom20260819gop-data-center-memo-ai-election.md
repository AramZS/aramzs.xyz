<h1>Exclusive: GOP issues stark warning to AI companies</h1>

<p><img alt="Animated gif of the Capitol Building with a data center bank in place of the columns" src="https://images.axios.com/gZsARbm115UzgQw3J3-uV2pHkRQ=/0x0:1920x1080/1920x1080/2026/08/18/1787092722776.gif?w=3840"/></p>
<p>Illustration: Sarah Grillo/Axios</p>
<p>The Senate GOP campaign arm, in a private memo to top AI companies, warns that toxic views of U.S. <a href="https://www.axios.com/2026/08/17/openai-nvidia-ohio-data-center-sb-energy">data centers</a> are killing the party's chances of holding a vital seat in Ohio.</p>
<ul>
<li>In the memo, obtained by Axios, the National Republican Senatorial Committee says Democrats have made data centers a "centerpiece" of their campaign to defeat Sen. Jon Husted (R-Ohio) — and that it's working.</li>
<li>"If he loses and data centers get the blame, politicians across the country will take notice — and they will not go near the next one," the memo says. "This has become a sleeper issue for the entire election cycle."</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> Republicans want the AI companies to do whatever it takes to stem the public backlash.</p>
<p><strong>The big picture:</strong> AI CEOs and top Republicans share in the rising panic. They tell us internal polls show data centers are as popular as spent nuclear waste, and more broadly have become a proxy for feelings about AI.</p>
<ul>
<li>Tech giants are scrambling to pull together media campaigns to try to shift opinion before it hardens locally and nationally.</li>
<li>But Democrats aren't dummies. They see the same polls and are moving fast.</li>
<li>Even politicians who previously embraced data centers, including Pennsylvania Gov. Josh Shapiro (D), are <a href="https://x.com/PAGovOffice/status/2089773358749470913">racing to crack down</a> on them this election year. On Tuesday, Shapiro, ahead of a possible 2028 presidential run, signed an <a href="https://www.pa.gov/governor/newsroom/2026-press-releases/governor-shapiro-signs-executive-order-on-data-center-developmen">executive order</a> imposing "strict guardrails" for data-center development.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>There are now</strong> more than 4,000 data centers online across the U.S., with another 3,000-plus centers proposed or in progress.</p>
<ul>
<li>They're designed to power the AI that's transforming the American economy, but have drawn <a href="https://www.axios.com/2026/08/14/data-center-backlash-fossil-fuel-protests">protests</a> from communities nationwide because they require massive amounts of electricity, can drive up utility bills, use huge amounts of water, involve enormous warehouse-like structures and provide few jobs.</li>
<li>They also have become local targets for residents' anxiety about how AI is eliminating jobs.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Zoom in:</strong> Husted's opponent in Ohio, former Sen. Sherrod Brown (D), has spent millions of dollars on a summer <a href="https://x.com/AdImpact_Pol/status/2074493471247728721">ad campaign</a> calling Husted "the face of data centers in Ohio."</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Republicans say the issue is especially potent in heavily blue-collar Ohio because the state has several big data center projects in the works. <a href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-guarantees-sb-energy-s-ports-pike-technology-campus-in-ohio-to-exclusively-host-nvidia-ai-compute">Nvidia</a> and <a href="https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project/">OpenAI</a> have announced a project in the last few days. <a href="https://datacenters.atmeta.com/ohio-new-albany/">Meta</a>, <a href="https://vantage-dc.com/data-center-locations/north-america/new-albany-ohio">Vantage</a> and <a href="https://rgp.org/city-of-van-wert-and-qts-announce-data-center-campus-in-northwest-ohio/#:~:text=The%20City%20of%20Van%20Wert%20and%20QTS,valuable%20partner%20to%20the%20Van%20Wert%20community.">QTS</a> also have projects there.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The NRSC memo says Brown has made data centers his "de facto opponent."</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>By the numbers:</strong> A <a href="https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fox-news-poll-economic-anxiety-candidate-concerns-define-ohio-senate-race">Fox News poll</a> last week showed Brown leading Husted by 8 points.</p>
<p><strong>State of play:</strong> Republicans say in the memo, headlined "Ohio Data Center Risk," that it's up to AI companies to improve perceptions of data centers by explaining "who benefits, who pays, and why a community should want one."</p>
<ul>
<li>"Until that happens, the issue will continue to dominate this race," the memo says.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>The bottom line:</strong> The memo argues that a Husted loss would cause long-lasting damage to AI companies in their push to build data centers nationwide to power future generations of AI.</p>
<ul>
<li>"If voters' perceptions of data centers are not fixed quickly, the campaign against them will expand far beyond Ohio," the party warns.</li>
</ul>
