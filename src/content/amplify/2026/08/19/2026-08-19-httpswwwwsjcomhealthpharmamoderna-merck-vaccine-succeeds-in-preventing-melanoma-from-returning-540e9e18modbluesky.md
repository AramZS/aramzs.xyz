<h1>Moderna Shares More Than Double on Success of mRNA Cancer Vaccine</h1>
<h2>Results from a Merck-Moderna partnership validate years of research into a new approach to cancer treatment</h2>
<p>By <a href="https://www.wsj.com/news/author/xavier-martinez">Xavier Martinez</a> and <a href="https://www.wsj.com/news/author/alex-janin">Alex Janin</a></p>
<p>Updated Aug. 19, 2026 3:49 pm ET</p>
<figure>
<img alt="The Moderna Inc. facility in Laval, Quebec, Canada." src="https://images.wsj.net/im-44522727?width=700&amp;height=488"/>
<figcaption>Moderna hopes to have the drug on the market in 2027.Graham Hughes/Bloomberg News</figcaption>
</figure>
<p>An experimental mRNA-based vaccine succeeded in preventing <a href="https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/cancer">cancer</a> from coming back or spreading in a study of high-risk melanoma patients, <a href="https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/MRNA">Moderna</a> and its partner <a href="https://www.wsj.com/market-data/quotes/MRK">Merck</a> said Wednesday, paving the way for a potentially new life-extending treatment for the thousands of people diagnosed with the deadly skin <a href="https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/cancer">cancer</a> each year.</p>
<p>Moderna shares more than doubled Wednesday, while Merck’s stock hit an all-time high.</p>
