<p>Sometimes when you look at the ads within OpenAI's ChatGPT, you might notice an ad from one advertiser first loads and then it quickly blinks and loads a different advertiser. I suspect this is a bit of a deficiency with the ad server, but I am not certain.</p>
<p>This was spotted by Brodie Clark on SERP Alerts who posted this video of it in action on <a href="https://x.com/SERPalerts/status/2089176957397184875">X</a>. He wrote, "State of ChatGPT ads today... an ad loads for one site, then it quickly switches to show an ad for a different site. Isn't this a cost-per-impression model?"</p>
