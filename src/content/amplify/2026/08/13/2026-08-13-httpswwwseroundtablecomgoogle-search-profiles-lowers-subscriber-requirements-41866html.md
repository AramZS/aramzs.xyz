<figure><img alt="Google Social Search Profiles" src="https://images.seroundtable.com/google-social-search-profiles-ustD673b.jpg"/><figcaption>Google Social Search Profiles</figcaption></figure>
<p>Google has dropped the minimum number of followers needed to be approved for a <a href="https://www.seroundtable.com/google-search-profiles-publisher-41447.html">Search Profile</a> on Google. Previously, it was 100,000 subscribers across YouTube, Instagram, X and Tiktok. Now it is 35,000 subscribers across YouTube, Instagram, and X but still 100,000 on TikTok.</p>
<p>Google updated a couple of its help documents with this new information including <a href="https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16904498?hl=en">this document</a> that now says:</p>
<p>You must have a minimum number of subscribers or followers on at least one content platform:</p>
<ul><li>YouTube: 35,000</li> <li>Instagram: 35,000</li> <li>X: 35,000</li> <li>TikTok: 100,000</li></ul>
<p>The <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20260808191147/https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/16904498?hl=en">old version</a> said:</p>
<p>You must have a minimum number of subscribers or followers on at least one content platform:</p>
<ul><li>YouTube: 100,000</li> <li>Instagram: 100,000</li> <li>X: 100,000</li> <li>TikTok: 100,000</li></ul>
<p>So more of you can now claim your Google Search Profile.</p>
<p>"Search profiles are a dedicated, shareable space to highlight content across platforms, and help audiences find accurate, up-to-date information about sources on Search," Google explained. You can follow your favorite publishers from the profile page, see a large header image, find the publisher's other social profiles, the latest articles and more.</p>
<p>Robby Stein from Google also announced on <a href="https://x.com/rmstein/status/2087590311828783443">X</a>, "We're lowering the follower thresholds so more creators and publishers can set them up," and added that Google is "also expanding them to more countries soon."</p>
