<p><a href="https://mcdm.gg/conventures"><strong><em>The Road to Broadhurst</em></strong></a> written by Carlos Cisco is available for free. Internally, we called this “the Conventure.” It’s designed for <em>experienced </em>Directors who already know the rules, and want to run <em>Draw Steel</em> for new players at their local convention or game store. It doesn’t <em>explain</em> how to play, that’s what the director is for!</p>
<p>Because RPG events at a convention can last anywhere from 45 minutes to 4 hours, <em>The Road to Broadhurst</em> has advice on how to skip sections you don’t have time for without affecting the flow of play.</p>
