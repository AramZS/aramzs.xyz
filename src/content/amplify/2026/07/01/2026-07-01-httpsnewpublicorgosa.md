<p><em>About the jury members:</em></p>
<ul> <li><b>Audrey Tang</b> — Taiwan’s first Minister of Digital Affairs, Tang pioneered prosocial digital tools to combat polarization and rebuild civic trust, and is a leading global voice for technology that deepens democracy.</li> <li><b>Mike Masnick</b> — Founder of tech policy blog Techdirt, his 2019 essay “Protocols, Not Platforms” proposed open protocols as the solution to social media’s moderation crisis, directly inspiring the creation of Bluesky, where he now sits on the board.</li> <li><b>Laurens Hof</b> — A Dutch analyst and writer who has spent over three years closely tracking the open social web, building deep expertise in the fediverse and AT Protocol through his widely-read weekly Fediverse Report.</li> <li><b>Johannes Ernst</b> — Co-founder of FediForum and CEO of Dazzle Labs, which advises organizations navigating decentralized platforms; he also co-founded the OpenID Foundation and helped create identity protocols foundational to the fediverse.</li> <li><b>Melanie Bartos</b> — A science communicator at the University of Innsbruck who leads its Mastodon and fediverse efforts under the banner of Open Science Communication, and speaks across Europe on the case for public-interest digital infrastructure.</li> <li><b>Robin Berjon</b> — A technologist specializing in digital governance, formerly VP of Data Governance at The New York Times and Vice-Chair of the W3C board, and board director of the Modal Foundation.</li> </ul>
<p><b>About New_ Public:</b><b><br/>
</b><a href="http://newpublic.org">New_ Public</a> is made up of researchers, engineers, designers, and community leaders. We want more healthy, flourishing spaces on the internet. We have two main strategies to achieve this: <a href="http://newpublic.org/local">helping local communities across America</a> create more flourishing digital spaces and developing prototypes for digital conversation with <a href="http://newpublic.org/psi">public service media organizations</a>. We work by partnering and co-designing with community stewards and public institutions. We’re in this to serve, remove obstacles to power, and make life significantly better for people, especially folks from underestimated communities. We think this work is essential for democracy.</p>
<p><b>About PublicSpaces:</b><b><br/>
</b><a href="https://publicspaces.net/">PublicSpaces</a> is a unique coalition of various public and civic society organisations, such as public broadcasters, municipalities, educational, healthcare and heritage institutions, libraries and museums. They are united in their desire to reduce the reliance on Big Tech and the accompanying privacy violations, algorithmic manipulation and misuse of public data. Under the PublicSpaces umbrella, these organisations work together to boost an alternative digital ecosystem that serves the common good, in order to establish a ‘Digital Public Space’, which can host a healthy public debate and where public values are protected.</p>
<p><b>About Waag Futurelab:</b><br/>
<a href="https://waag.org/nl/">Waag Futurelab</a> researches and designs technology for a better future. Waag examines new technologies and existing systems, and works with artists, citizens, designers and scientists to develop alternatives. Through public research, events and educational programmes, Waag empowers as many people as possible to contribute to the design of technology that puts people and society first.</p>
