<p>Officials with the U.S. Navy's elite Blue Angels flight demonstration squadron said they're conducting a review after social media footage showed a jet flying low over a crowd of beachgoers in Pensacola Beach, Florida, on Wednesday morning.</p>
<p>"During an arrival maneuver, an aircraft flew lower than standard profiles, resulting in a disturbance on the beach that affected civilian chairs and umbrellas," the statement said, calling it a "low-altitude pass."</p>
<p>North Florida ABC affiliate <a data-testid="prism-linkbase" href="https://weartv.com/news/local/watch-low-blue-angels-flyover-at-pensacola-beach-sends-items-flying-shocks-beachgoers"><u>WEAR</u></a> reported the flyover happened during a "Breakfast with the Blues" event.</p>
