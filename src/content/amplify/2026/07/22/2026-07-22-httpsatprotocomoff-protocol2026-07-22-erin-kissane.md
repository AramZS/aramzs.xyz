<h2>Show notes</h2>
<p>There’s no shortage of diagnosis of what went wrong with the social web, most of it <em>on</em> the social web. It’s a familiar space for writer, speaker, and researcher Erin Kissane, who’s spent a career with a sharp eye and an indefatigable resolve doing what she fully admits isn’t really anyone’s job.</p>
<p>Sorting out the <em>why</em> we’re building new apps, networks, and protocols is just as important as the how, and Erin is a steady guide. In this wonderfully wide-ranging conversation, she and Jim work through familiar arcs of technology with some important takeaways for a new generation of network builders.</p>
