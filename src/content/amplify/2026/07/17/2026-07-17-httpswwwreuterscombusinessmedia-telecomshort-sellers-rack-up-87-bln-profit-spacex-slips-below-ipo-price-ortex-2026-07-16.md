<p>NEW YORK, July 16 (Reuters) - Short sellers targeting SpaceX <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/markets/companies/SPCX.O">(SPCX.O)</a> shares are sitting on an estimated $8.7 billion in paper ​profit since the rockets-to-AI firm's initial public offering last month, as ‌its stock slipped below the IPO price, according to data and analytics firm Ortex Technologies.</p>
<p>Short sellers, who borrow shares to sell them and later buy them back at ​a lower price for a profit, have pressed their bearish bets ​on SpaceX as the company's shares slipped toward its IPO price ⁠of $135 from a post-IPO high of $225.64.</p>
<p><a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/spacexs-slide-risks-turning-blockbuster-ipo-into-confidence-test-2026-07-15/">SpaceX shares have been volatile</a>, experiencing brief ​bouts of strength before slipping back. On Wednesday, the stock dropped <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/spacex-shares-slide-below-ipo-price-blistering-rally-unravels-2026-07-15/">below its initial ​public offering price</a> for the first time before recovering to close just above that level.</p>
<p>"SpaceX has been a rollercoaster for the short sellers, and it has ended up firmly ​in their favor," Ortex co-founder Peter Hillerberg said. "Rather than take profits, the ​bears kept adding the whole way down."</p>
<p>Almost half of SpaceX's tradable shares, about 49% ‌of ⁠the free float, are now out on loan, according to Ortex.</p>

<p>"We believe most of that is short selling," Hillerberg said.</p>
<p>SpaceX did not immediately respond to a request for comment.</p>
<p>SpaceX's lofty valuation makes it a target for ​short sellers skeptical of ​its rich price ⁠tag, but strong retail and institutional interest as well as CEO <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/business/elon-musk/">Elon Musk's</a> history of public battles against short sellers ​make bearish bets against the company <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/short-sellers-tread-carefully-musks-spacex-debuts-2026-06-09/">a risky proposition</a>.</p>
<p>The weakness ​in SpaceX ⁠shares reflects in part investor concern over debt-funded <a data-testid="Link" href="https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/">AI</a> spending.</p>
<p>The stock's sizable short position could inject further volatility into the shares, with every dollar SpaceX shares ⁠move ​worth more than $300 million to the short side, ​Ortex estimates. That means the stock could swing hard in either direction.</p>
<p>SpaceX shares were up ​about 1% to $136.28 on Thursday.</p>
