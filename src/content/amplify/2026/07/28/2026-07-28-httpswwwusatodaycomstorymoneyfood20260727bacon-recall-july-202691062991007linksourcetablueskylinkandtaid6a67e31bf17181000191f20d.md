<p>Maple Leaf Foods has <a data-t-l=":b|e|k|⚑u" href="https://www.usatoday.com/story/money/food/2026/07/23/egg-recall-products-states-list/91020489007/">recalled</a> over 12,000 pounds of bacon imported to the United States from Canada due to an import violation, according to a notice from the Department of Agriculture's Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS).</p>
<p>The products were "imported from Canada without the benefit of import reinspection into the United States," the FSIS stated in its <a data-t-l=":b|z|k|⚑u" href="https://www.fsis.usda.gov/recalls-alerts/maple-leaf-foods-inc--recalls-not-ready-eat-nrte-bacon-product-imported-without">July 24 notice</a>.</p>
<p>The recall affects not ready-to-eat smoked bacon products distributed to retailers in Idaho, Oregon and Washington state, according to the FSIS.</p>
<p>No illnesses have been reported related to the product, per the recall notice.</p>
<p>The recall also comes about a week after President <a data-autotag="26f031d1-9924-4f10-b4e6-019d076113d5" data-t-l=":b|e|k|⚑u" href="https://www.usatoday.com/news/politics/donald-trump/">Donald Trump</a>'s administration announced <a data-t-l=":b|e|k|⚑u" href="https://www.usatoday.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/20/trump-canada-tariffs-wildfire/90987616007/">50% tariffs on many Canadian imports</a>. The tariffs, expected to impact goods imported into the United States from Canada’s auto, alcohol and dairy industries, are scheduled to take effect on Aug. 19. However, Canadian Prime Minister <a data-t-l=":b|e|k|⚑u" href="https://www.usatoday.com/news/world/mark-carney/">Mark Carney</a> <a data-t-l=":b|z|k|⚑u" href="https://x.com/MarkJCarney/status/2079353135126941864/photo/1">has signaled</a> he is open to negotiations, so the final rate and scope may still change.</p>
<p>Here's what to know about the bacon recall.</p>
<h2>What is being recalled?</h2>
<p>The following Maple Leaf Foods products are included in the recall:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Royale Natural Applewood Smoked ALL NATURAL Uncured Bacon Product of Canada</strong> – 12-ounce vacuum package, with the following sell-by dates printed on the side of the package:<ul><li>Sept. 1, 2026</li><li>Sept. 7 2026</li></ul></li><li><strong>TOP VALU Uncured Hardwood Smoked Bacon Product of Canada</strong> – 12-ounce vacuum package with the following sell-by dates printed on the side of the package:<ul><li>Sept. 1, 2026</li><li>Sept. 2, 2026</li><li>Sept. 4, 2026</li><li>Sept. 5, 2026</li><li>Sept. 7, 2026</li></ul></li></ul>
<p>The recalled products were produced on the following dates:</p>
<ul><li>June 9</li><li>June 10</li><li>June 12</li><li>June 13</li><li>June 15</li></ul>
<p>"EST. 1" is also printed on the side of the package.</p>
<h2>Where were the products sold?</h2>
<p>The products were sent to Grocery Outlet distributors and retailers in the following states, according to FSIS:</p>
<ul><li>Idaho</li><li>Oregon</li><li>Washington</li></ul>
<p>A full list of where the product was distributed will be posted to the FSIS website once it is available.</p>
<h2>What should you do if you purchased the recalled products?</h2>
<p>The agency said the recalled products may still be in consumers' fridges and freezers. It urged people not to consume the recalled products.</p>
<p>Consumers who purchased the recalled bacon should throw it away or return it to the place of purchase, according to the FSIS.</p>
