title: Russia hacks doorbell cameras to spy on Nato bases
---
<p>Russia has been spying on Nato military bases through civilian cameras connected to the internet, Dutch intelligence services said.</p>
<p>Kremlin-based hackers accessed the devices to monitor the transfer of military equipment to <a data-yga='{"yLinkElement":"link","yLinkElementType":"article_link"}' data-ylk="slk:Ukraine;elm:context_link;itc:0;sec:content-canvas;" href="https://www.telegraph.co.uk/ukraine/">Ukraine</a>, the AIVD domestic security and MIVD military intelligence agencies said.</p>
<p>Their joint investigation found the Russian operation had targeted cameras pointing towards military transport routes in the hope of identifying what weapons were being sent to Kyiv.</p>
<p>"Organisations with IP [internet protocol] cameras on these routes have now been warned so that they could take action," the agencies warned after exposing what they described as a "large-scale Russian operation".</p>
<p>They said it had targeted "European <a data-yga='{"yLinkElement":"link","yLinkElementType":"article_link"}' data-ylk="slk:Nato;elm:context_link;itc:0;sec:content-canvas;" href="https://www.telegraph.co.uk/nato/">Nato</a> member states, including the Netherlands, and Ukraine".</p>
<p>Internet protocol cameras, which are cameras with internet access, have risen to prominence in recent years because of cheap Chinese imports and high-speed broadband connections.</p>
<p>While the intelligence agencies did not specify the type of cameras hacked, the doorbell systems are frequently used by people to monitor their property from mobile phones. Hackers then use readily available apps to scan for devices that might be accessible.</p>

<p>The Dutch investigation found that many of the cameras were unsecured, and "often have standard passwords, outdated firmware and standard configurations".</p>
<p>They said: "When the IP camera is identified, the malicious party can attempt to access the IP camera via the internet. This is often relatively easy, because many IP cameras connected to the internet are insufficiently secure."</p>
<p>Hacking cameras has become commonplace in modern warfare in recent years. Almost any camera installed on a city street, outside a home or business can be used to gather intelligence.</p>
<p>Ukrainian hackers hijacked Russian surveillance cameras to monitor Russian troop movements and direct long-range strikes.</p>
<p>The technique was used by Kyiv during the <a data-yga='{"yLinkElement":"link","yLinkElementType":"article_link"}' data-ylk="slk:first use of an undersea drone strike against a Russian submarine;elm:context_link;itc:0;sec:content-canvas;" href="https://www.telegraph.co.uk/world-news/2025/12/24/ukraine-drone-attack-on-russian-surveillance-aircraft/">first use of an undersea drone strike against a Russian submarine</a> docked in the port of Novorossiysk.</p>
<p>Israeli security officials warned that Iran had used private security cameras to find targets for drone and missile barrages against Israel.</p>
<p>Research released earlier this year found hundreds of devices had been accessed as part of the Islamic Republic's long-range campaign after the first US-Israeli attacks in late February.</p>
<p>But most notably, Israeli spies and the CIA managed to force their way into a network of security and traffic cameras across Tehran, locating <a data-yga='{"yLinkElement":"link","yLinkElementType":"article_link"}' data-ylk="slk:Ali Khamenei;elm:context_link;itc:0;sec:content-canvas;" href="https://www.telegraph.co.uk/world-news/2026/02/28/irans-supreme-leader-khamenei-found-dead-in-rubble/">Ali Khamenei</a>, the former supreme leader, before launching the air strikes that killed him on the first day of Iran war.</p>
<p>The practice is now considered easier and cheaper than using drones and satellites to gather intelligence.</p>
<p>It also aids operational surprise because most camera owners are blissfully unaware their devices have been penetrated by hackers.</p>
<p>Ground-based cameras offer a unique perspective on the terrain, which isn't the case with conventional aerial-based spy kit.</p>
