<h2>NTS is a music discovery platform,<br/><br/> built by music lovers<br/> for music lovers.</h2>
<figure><img src="https://www.nts.live/img/NTS_Booth_Nabihah%20Iqbal_2-min.jpg"/></figure>
<p>Home to two live radio channels broadcasting 24/7 and hundreds of thousands of hours of human-curated shows, NTS is one of the world’s most revered music archives.</p>
<h2>Since 2011, NTS has celebrated music from every corner of the world,<br/><br/> from hip-hop to highlife and jungle to j-pop.</h2>
<h2>from hip-hop to highlife and jungle to j-pop.</h2>
<figure><img src="https://www.nts.live/img/about-page-2.png"/></figure>
<p>This ever-expanding resource is made possible by a network of artists and DJs <a href="https://www.nts.live/explore/location">who broadcast from over 80 countries</a> – all without on-air advertising.</p>
<p>Beyond online radio, we help our listeners fall in love with music through <a href="https://www.nts.live/series">special broadcasts</a>, <a href="https://www.nts.live/events">international events</a>, and <a href="https://www.nts.live/videos">original video content</a>.</p>
<h2>NTS always operates in the best interests of music culture.</h2>
<figure><img src="https://www.nts.live/img/nts-festival.jpg"/></figure>
<p>We elevate artists on the peripheries through our programming. We always offer fair fees for events, and our resident hosts are paid through NTS Supporter subscriptions. We have radio licenses with collection agencies across the world and <a href="https://ntslive.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/77000586376-licensing-and-royalty-fees">pay royalties</a> to artists and rightsholders for all the music aired on the platform.</p>
<p>We have and always will prioritise human curation over algorithmic recommendations. We also believe in freedom of expression, and will never tell radio hosts what they can play or say on NTS.</p>
