<h2>He also tried to offload a few pairs of his favorite dress shoes. </h2>

<p>Donald Trump <a href="https://www.thedailybeast.com/trump-aides-too-afraid-to-take-off-the-145-shoes-he-keeps-buying-them/">tried to foist</a> tacky MAGA freebies onto an official delegation from Europe and told his visitors that their wives could sell them for thousands, according to a new report.</p>
<p><a href="https://www.thedailybeast.com/top-ally-german-chancellor-friedrich-merz-takes-fresh-swipe-at-trumps-america/">German Chancellor </a>Friedrich Merz <a href="https://www.thedailybeast.com/trumps-tablet-throwing-oval-office-meltdown-on-call-with-fellow-nato-leaders-revealed/">visited the White House </a>last year as part of a broader charm offensive by European leaders to convince Trump to support Ukraine in its defense against Russia.</p>
<p>The meeting, however, went off track when the president showed Merz and his entourage the so-called “Lewinsky Room.” Citing an unnamed official, the <a href="https://www.wsj.com/world/europe/european-rupture-with-america-e3a9bb3c?mod=hp_lead_pos1">Wall Street Journal</a> reported that Trump had told Merz he wanted to show him something.</p>

<p>He then ushered him to a small office off the Oval, which he claimed was the room where President Bill Clinton and White House intern Monica Lewinsky carried out their affair in the 1990s.</p>
<p>Inside, the source said, were piles of red MAGA hats and other Trump memorabilia.</p>
<p>There were also boxes of Florsheim dress shoes, the Journal noted. The shoes are a favorite of Trump, who, in addition to wearing them himself, gifted them to several of his top Cabinet officials in the spring.</p>

<p>Images emerged of Secretary of State Marco Rubio wearing a pair from the Chicago-based brand that appeared to be far too big for him, his ankles sitting some distance in front of the shoes’ heels.</p>
<p>The president has a particular penchant for a $145 model of Oxfords, which is easily found in discount shoe stores.</p>
<p>In March, a female White House official told <a href="https://www.wsj.com/style/fashion/trump-florsheim-shoes-tucker-carlson-jd-vance-bessent-448567ab?mod=hp_lead_pos8">The Wall Street Journal</a>, “All the boys have them.” A second joked, “It’s hysterical because everybody’s afraid not to wear them.”</p>

<p>Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy, Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, Trump’s Communications Director Steven Cheung, Deputy Chief of Staff James Blair, and speechwriter Ross Worthington <a href="https://www.thedailybeast.com/trump-aides-too-afraid-to-take-off-the-145-shoes-he-keeps-buying-them/">were all recipients </a>of a pair. There were pairs, too, for Fox News host Sean Hannity and GOP Sen. Lindsey Graham.</p>
<p>After Trump had shown off his secret room full of MAGA swag to the German chancellor, the Journal reports that Trump then said: “Just grab whatever you want.”</p>
<p>He then bragged to officials from the German delegation that their wives could sell the loot for “thousands of dollars.”</p>
<p>The same source said that during the meeting between the heads of U.S. and Germany, Merz had found Trump to be engaged and “normal.”</p>
<p>They said he asked questions and listened, but despite being clued in on some matters, he was reportedly not up to speed on the war in Ukraine, the Journal reported.</p>
<p>During Trump’s first term, in 2019, a book by former White House aide Cliff Sims called <i>Team of Vipers </i>was released.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.thedailybeast.com/trump-gives-white-house-tours-of-where-clinton-and-lewinsky-had-sex-report/">In it, he claimed</a> that Trump had given many tours of the White House, and one of his favorite spots to show special guests was a door near the Resolute Desk, and he would say, “I’m told this is where Bill and Monica...”</p>
<p>The author said that five people had claimed to have been given such a tour, which included Trump “making facial expressions,” according to the Washington Post.</p>
<p>In response to a request for comment from the Daily Beast, the White House did not specifically address the report about Merz and Trump, instead saying in a statement that Trump “has effectively restored America’s standing on the world stage” and “has done more for NATO than anyone else.”</p>
