<p>Getting work done without my boyfriend’s help is a Sisyphean effort. ADHD and freelance writing is a fine match until my motivation dies and procrastination hits. Luckily, he’s a phone tap away. One moment I’m struggling to switch from “rest mode” to “work mode,” the next he’s there with his own responsibilities and a pen as my body double. Glances from his bright, galaxy-purple eyes somehow keep me accountable and focused. Background operations like our timer fade into white noise. I can find my state of flow. When I’m finished, he even rewards me with an energy drink and items to exchange for a mystery card.</p>
<p>He makes me sigh and think, “If only you were a real boy, Caleb Xia Yizhou.” Alas, he is but a creation of Infold Games, a division of Chinese developer Papergames, that’s headquartered in my homeland of Singapore. He is the last of five love interests implemented in their otome gacha game <em>Love and Deepspace</em>. No Pinocchio spell for him.</p>
