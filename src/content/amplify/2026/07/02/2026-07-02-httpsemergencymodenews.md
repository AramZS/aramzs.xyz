<h2>Workshop series</h2>
<p>Join our 4-week training program to build a disaster plan for your newsroom. Work alongside a small group of peers from similar organizations to develop custom resources and strengthen your emergency planning skills.</p>
<p>Emergency Mode for News is for newsroom planners and activators — the people everyone looks to when a disaster happens. Whether your job title is editor, reporter or publisher, Emergency Mode is for you. The program is a collaboration between <strong><a data-id="https://www.opennews.org/" data-type="link" href="https://www.opennews.org/">OpenNews</a></strong>, <strong><a data-id="https://newspack.com/" data-type="link" href="https://newspack.com/">Newspack</a></strong> and <strong><a data-id="https://nclocal.org/" data-type="link" href="https://nclocal.org/">NC Local</a></strong>, with funding from <strong><a data-id="https://www.pressforward.news/" data-type="link" href="https://www.pressforward.news/">Press Forward</a></strong>, a national initiative to reimagine local news.</p>
