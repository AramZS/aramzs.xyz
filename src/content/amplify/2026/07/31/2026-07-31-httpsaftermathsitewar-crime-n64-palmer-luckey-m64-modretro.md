<p>First let’s just talk about the practical: There’s no shortage of ways to play the N64. You can get original hardware and a CRT or an upscaler like the <a href="https://www.retrotink.com/?ref=aftermath.site">Retrotink</a>, a piece of hardware that’s made by people who really care. You can also mod an HDMI port into an old N64 if you aren’t a coward. If you want something FPGA-based that plays cartridges, the Analogue 3D works. <a href="https://retroremake.co/pages/superstation%E1%B5%92%E2%81%BF%E1%B5%89?ref=aftermath.site">The Superstation One</a> runs FPGA cores just fine. Or to hell with it, just get a <a href="https://aftermath.site/mister-pi-de-10-nano-clone-board-taki-udon/"><u>MiSTer</u></a> or even just download a god-fearing emulator. Nobody needs Palmer Luckey’s evil little device, particularly when the market has never been more saturated.</p>
