<p>Break out your bucket hats, it is once again time to <a href="https://www.vulture.com/2021/08/watch-evan-rachel-wood-cover-you-get-what-you-give.html">get what you give</a>. New Radicals have a new song, and for some reason it’s on the <em>One Night Only </em>soundtrack. It’s not just the soundtrack — it’s the title song,“One Night Only (Break Loose Break Free!). This marks the second new song from New Radicals this month following their guest spot on Michelle Branch’s re-record of “The Game of Love,” replacing Carlos Santana. That made a little more sense, as New Rad frontman Gregg Alexander was one of the main songwriters. But writing a song for the movie about <a href="https://www.vulture.com/article/2025-oscars-monica-barbaro-on-her-surprise-nomination.html">Monica Barbaro</a> and <a href="https://www.vulture.com/article/review-eternitys-afterlife-will-drive-you-crazy.html">Callum Turner</a> finding love in a world where pre-marital sex is illegal except for one night a year feels truly baffling. But then again, <em>One Night Only </em>is a baffling movie.</p>
<p>Since <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AvKoSR3O4NQ">the trailer</a> dropped the immortal lines “Finding love is hard enough. Try doing it on the one night a year single people were legally allowed to have sex,” the internet has been ablaze with confusion and occasional outrage about this cinematic world where premarital sex is all but illegal. One wonders how this law is enforced, what the clandestine singleton sex scene is like (and how closely it mirrors <a href="https://www.nyclgbtsites.org/site/greenwich-village-waterfront/">the real world</a>), and whether engaged couples can do hand stuff. Once you throw in a score by <a href="https://www.vulture.com/article/haim-best-songs-band-interview.html">Este Haim</a> and a cover of “Love Is in the Air” by King Princess, and the whole film has a “movie Jenna Maroney is third-billed in on <em>30 Rock</em>” vibe to it. All our questions will hopefully be answered when <em>One Night Only </em>hits theaters August 7.</p>
