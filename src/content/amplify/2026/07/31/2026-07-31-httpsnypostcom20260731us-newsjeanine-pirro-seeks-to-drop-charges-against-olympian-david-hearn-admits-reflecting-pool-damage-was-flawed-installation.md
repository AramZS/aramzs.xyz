<p>WASHINGTON — DC US Attorney Jeanine Pirro is moving to dismiss charges against former Olympic canoeist David Hearn for allegedly <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/07/02/us-news/american-olympic-canoeist-indicted-for-alleged-vandalism-of-lincoln-memorial-reflecting-pool/">vandalizing the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool</a>, revealing in a Friday court filing that the damage was, in fact, “the result of flawed installation by the contractor.”</p>
<p>Pirro’s office submitted the motion to dismiss after Hearn had pleaded not guilty earlier this month to <a href="https://nypost.com/2026/07/09/us-news/ex-olympic-canoeist-david-hearn-pleads-not-guilty-to-vandalizing-lincoln-memorial-reflecting-pool-not-a-crime-to-touch-water/">one felony count of destruction of property</a> for the alleged June 19 incident.</p>
<p>But the July 31 court filing reveals that the sealant at the bottom of the Reflecting Pool had “[a]lmost immediately” begun peeling after the work was completed after the Virginia-based company got a no-bid contract for the renovations.</p>
