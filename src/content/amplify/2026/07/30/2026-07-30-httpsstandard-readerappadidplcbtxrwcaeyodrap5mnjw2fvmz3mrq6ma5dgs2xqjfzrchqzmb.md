<h2>Intro and TLDR: What is the Atmosphere </h2>
<p><strong>The Atmosphere is an ecosystem of social networking apps that all use Atmosphere accounts so that:</strong></p>
<ul><li><strong>One account logs you in to all Atmosphere apps</strong></li><li><strong>Apps can seamlessly integrate with each other</strong></li><li><strong>Apps cannot lock you in and extract you for profit</strong></li></ul>
<p>Currently, Bluesky is the flagship social network in the Atmosphere but many exist and are growing! Start using any of these today by logging in with your Atmosphere/Bluesky account.</p>
<h2>Atmosphere Accounts </h2>
<p>Your Atmosphere account (also called a PDS) is:</p>
<ol start="1"><li>Your handle/username</li><li>A place that stores the content you create on the apps and the people you have relationships with (follows, followers, mutuals, etc.)</li></ol>
<p>If you're curious about what this looks like, go to <strong><a href="https://pdsls.dev/">PDSLS</a></strong> and search for your handle. You'll be looking at a big data dump of your content. Click around and you'll see everyone you follow and all the posts, likes, reposts, etc you've ever made on Bluesky!</p>
<p>If you use other Atmosphere apps, those will show up here too!</p>

<h3>How do I make an account? </h3>
<p><em>Your Bluesky account IS an Atmosphere account!</em> You can (and should) use it to log into any Atmosphere app!</p>
<p>If you don't want to make your account on Bluesky, you can also create one on some other Atmosphere apps like <strong><a href="https://tangled.org/">Tangled</a></strong>, <strong><a href="https://surf.social/">Surf</a></strong>, <strong><a href="https://blacksky.community/">Blacksky</a></strong>, and <strong><a href="https://pckt.blog/">Pckt</a></strong>.</p>
<h3>A note about your content</h3>
<p>You own your account! <em>Not</em> the app you made the account on. Only you can create and change things in it. <em>However</em>, anyone can read and see those things.</p>
<p><strong><a href="https://dholms.leaflet.pub/3meluqcwky22a">Work is being done</a></strong> on private content, but for now think of your content as living in a glass house.</p>
<p>This enables a lot of cool things (which we'll get into), and blocking and moderation tools exist, but it's worth just keeping in mind.</p>

<h2>Why is the Atmosphere so good?</h2>
<h3>Unified Log In</h3>
<p>Once you've made an Atmosphere account, use it for ANY Atmosphere app. It's so convenient!</p>
<p>If you made an account on Bluesky, that's an Atmosphere account. Just use the handle you made there to log into Leaflet, Semble, Tangled, or anywhere else.</p>

<h3>True freedom of choice</h3>
<p>Legacy social media companies seem amazing at first, but once they get enough users, it's all about profit extraction. They're no longer making a product in your best interest. You want to leave but they've locked in all your friends and content, so you're stuck. Twitter/X and Instagram stand out as recent examples.</p>
<p>But in the Atmosphere, <em>all your data is public</em>. Let's say an app stops serving you, gets bogged down in ads, changes its algorithm, or supports questionable causes. A competitor will emerge and you can just start using that app instead. Your same content and your same friends, no starting over again. Just totally seamless.</p>
<h4><strong>It's already possible today!</strong></h4>
<ul><li>Bluesky is popular, but maybe you'd rather not patron US companies. Or maybe you NEED that edit button. Then use Europe based <strong><a href="https://mu.social/">Mu Social</a></strong>. </li><li>Maybe you'd prefer to hear more black voices, in a safe space for black community. Try out <strong><a href="https://blacksky.community/">Blacksky</a></strong>.</li><li>If you want a totally new experience that's mysterious an</li></ul>
<p>All your posts, follows, and followers are just there. In fact, give it a try right now. Just log into any of these alternatives with your Atmosphere account. If you don't like them, just head back to Bluesky!</p>

<h2>Let's sum it up!</h2>
<p>The Atmosphere is an ecosystem of apps that use Atmosphere accounts.</p>
<p>re account exists outside of any single app. It consists of a username and a big folder of all the content and relationships a person has. It's public!</p>
<p>Atmosphere apps use this folder to create social experiences like blogging, tweeting, and sharing. Because everything is public, Atmosphere apps can create uniquely seamless experiences between themselves!</p>
<p>Finally, if an app fails to meet your standards, you can always leave the app without leaving your connections and your content.</p>
