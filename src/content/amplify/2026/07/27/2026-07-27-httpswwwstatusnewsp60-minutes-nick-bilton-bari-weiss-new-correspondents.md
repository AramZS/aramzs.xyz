<p>Last Thursday, <strong>"60 Minutes"</strong> Executive Producer <strong>Nick Bilton </strong>arrived at <strong>1600 Pennsylvania Avenue</strong> for an important meeting ahead of the newsmagazine's fall season. Bilton, I'm told, had scheduled an off-the-record with the <strong>White House</strong> communications office for a meet-and-greet session in which he introduced himself to officials as he settles into his new position.</p>
<p>Contrary to what some <strong>CBS News</strong> staffers had speculated upon learning about the meeting from colleagues, I’m told that Bilton did not explicitly ask for an interview with <strong>Donald Trump</strong> or any other administration official, though my understanding is the show would like to secure a sit-down with <strong>Marco Rubio</strong>. Instead, Bilton, who was in town for the <strong>White House Correspondents' Dinner</strong>, simply sought to establish a relationship with those who will ultimately decide whether to grant his team access down the road.</p>
<p>A CBS News spokesperson declined to comment for this story. A White House spokesperson also declined to comment.</p>
