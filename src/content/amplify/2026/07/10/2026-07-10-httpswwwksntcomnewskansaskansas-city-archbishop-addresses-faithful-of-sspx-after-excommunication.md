<p>TOPEKA (<a data-id="https://www.ksnt.com/" data-type="link" href="https://www.ksnt.com/">KSNT</a>) – The archbishop of Kansas City in Kansas has weighed in on the recent excommunication of a traditionalist group that has parishioners in the Sunflower State and around the world.</p>
<p>Archbishop Shawn McKnight issued his letter on July 2, addressing the decision made by Pope Leo XIV to excommunicate the bishops and priests of the Society of Saint Pius X (<a data-id="https://www.ksnt.com/news/international/ap-what-to-know-about-the-schism-by-traditionalist-catholics-who-defied-pope-leo-xiv/" data-type="link" href="https://www.ksnt.com/news/international/ap-what-to-know-about-the-schism-by-traditionalist-catholics-who-defied-pope-leo-xiv/">SSPX</a>). The Holy See declared the SSPX had formally broken with the Catholic Church in early July after the SSPX consecrated four new bishops in Switzerland without Pope Leo’s consent.</p>
<p>McKnight said the action taken by the SSPX to consecrate the new bishops “is a source of profound sorrow” for the Catholic Church. He said the consecration of the new bishops without the authorization of the pope is a major offense that carries with it serious consequences under the church’s law.</p>
<blockquote> <p>“Successive Roman Pontiffs have demonstrated patience, generosity, and a sincere desire to heal divisions within the Church. The Holy Father himself, in <a data-id="https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/letters/2026/documents/20260629-lettera-fraternita-sanpiox.html" data-type="link" href="https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiv/en/letters/2026/documents/20260629-lettera-fraternita-sanpiox.html">his letter on June 29, 2026</a>, has recognized the sincere devotion to Catholic tradition, priestly formation, and missionary zeal found among the many members of the Society. Precisely because these are genuine gifts, the Church has continued to hope that they will flourish within full ecclesial communion. The actions taken by the Society’s leadership stand in contrast to our repeated efforts at reconciliation.”</p> </blockquote>
<p>The SSPX maintains places of worship around the world and dozens in the U.S. alone, according to its <a data-id="https://sspx.org/en/list-sspx-chapels" data-type="link" href="https://sspx.org/en/list-sspx-chapels">website</a>. Two can be found in Kansas: these include the Mary Immaculate Church in Wichita and the <a data-id="https://www.ksnt.com/news/local-news/nearly-5-hour-consecration-ceremony-to-be-held-for-new-mega-church-in-kansas/" data-type="link" href="https://www.ksnt.com/news/local-news/nearly-5-hour-consecration-ceremony-to-be-held-for-new-mega-church-in-kansas/">Immaculata in Saint Marys</a>.</p>
<p>Archbishop Marcel Lefebvre founded the SSPX in 1970 and has around 720 priests and half-a-million faithful around the world, according to its <a data-id="https://fsspx.org/en/beautiful-mystery-history-society-st-pius-x-30949" data-type="link" href="https://fsspx.org/en/beautiful-mystery-history-society-st-pius-x-30949">website</a>. The organization generally opposes modernization reforms implemented by the Catholic Church and celebrates the ancient Latin Mass, according to <a data-id="https://www.ksnt.com/news/ap-top-headlines/ap-vatican-declares-society-of-st-pius-x-in-schism-excommunicates-bishops-and-invalidates-sacraments/" data-type="link" href="https://www.ksnt.com/news/ap-top-headlines/ap-vatican-declares-society-of-st-pius-x-in-schism-excommunicates-bishops-and-invalidates-sacraments/">reporting from the Associated Press</a> (AP).</p>
<p>The SSPX has <a data-id="https://www.ksnt.com/news/ap-top-headlines/ap-catholic-group-that-defied-pope-and-caused-schism-defends-its-actions-and-casts-itself-as-the-victim/" data-type="link" href="https://www.ksnt.com/news/ap-top-headlines/ap-catholic-group-that-defied-pope-and-caused-schism-defends-its-actions-and-casts-itself-as-the-victim/">defended its actions</a> following the excommunication by the pope. Rev. Davide Pagliarani, the SSPX superior, sent a letter to Pope Leo explaining his organization’s position on the schism, according to reporting from the AP.</p>
<blockquote> <p>“What the Society of Saint Pius X has done, and will continue to do, is nothing other than an extraordinary initiative for the salvation of souls, amidst the doctrinal and moral confusion into which the church is plunged.”</p> </blockquote>
<p>McKnight took time in his letter to address those who have been attending SSPX church services. He said that, in the view of the Vatican, the sacraments celebrated by the priesthood of the SSPX are not valid.</p>
<blockquote> <p>“Nevertheless, the faithful should be aware that, as the Holy See has explained, the ministry exercised by the Society is not legitimate in the life of the Church: the sacraments celebrated by its ministers are illicit, and the sacraments of Penance and Matrimony administered by them are now invalid. Because participation in the worship and apostolic life of a schismatic community objectively expresses and fosters separation from the Church’s communion, Catholics may not knowingly take part in the Society’s liturgical celebrations, apostolates, or other activities. Such participation is not merely discouraged but is gravely contrary to the unity of the Church and, when undertaken with full knowledge and deliberate consent, is sinful.”</p> </blockquote>
<p>McKnight encourages both the members of the SSPX’s priesthood and those who attend its churches to “not adhere to the Society’s schism” to avoid further penalties. He said people who have attended SSPX services should seek out Catholic priests if they have any questions about the validity of sacraments they may have received.</p>
