title: What It Was Like Inside Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s Wedding
---
<p>Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce married in an elaborately planned wedding with garden themes and plenty of personalized “T&amp;T” logo details. Both were wearing Dior, though pictures have not been released yet.</p><p>Adam Sandler officiated a ceremony where the couple read their own vows. The couple’s brothers stood by their sides as the man of honor and best man.</p><p>Though few details have been confirmed by the couple, a picture was beginning to emerge <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/03/style/taylor-swift-wedding-celebrity-guests.html">of what the most star-studded night</a> in New York in recent memory looked like.</p><h2>A-list guests in black-tie wear.</h2><p>It was quickly clear that the red carpet for the wedding events was an impressive lineup, with 1,000 guests — and a wide-ranging mix of celebrities — invited to the wedding. <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/30/style/taylor-swift-wedding-kelce-dress-code.html?eafs_enabled=false">The dress code was black-tie</a>, and guests showed playful variations of it.</p><figure><img alt="Karlie Kloss, in a gold strapless dress, walks down some stairs while holding the hand of Joshua Kushner." src="https://static01.nyt.com/images/2026/07/03/multimedia/03swift-wedding-starstudded-02-lzfv-copy/03swift-wedding-starstudded-02-lzfv-articleLarge.jpg"/><figcaption>Karlie Kloss and Josh Kushner.  TheStewartofNY/GC Images, via Getty Images</figcaption></figure><p>Camila Cabello wore an off-the-shoulder hot pink lace Zimmermann dress, and Gigi Hadid also went pink, while her date, Bradley Cooper, wore a classic black tuxedo. Karlie Kloss, who attended with her husband, Josh Kushner, shone in a long, gold column dress. MGK put his own spin on things, with a steel wallet chain as part of his suit, and Ed Sheeran went full sky blue.</p><p>See more <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/03/style/taylor-swift-wedding-guest-list.html">about the guests here</a>.</p><h2>What was on the menu?</h2><p>The menu for the star-studded events on Friday has not yet been released, but Sartiano’s, an Italian restaurant owned by Scott Sartiano at the Mercer Hotel in SoHo, catered the 100-person rehearsal dinner Thursday night, according to a person familiar with the event.</p><p>The menu included meatballs, caviar cannoli, stuffed paccheri pasta, spaghetti arrabiata and focaccia with whipped ricotta.</p><p>The dinner was family-style, according to the source, who spoke with The Times on condition of anonymity because the person had not been authorized to speak publicly.</p><p>A Sartiano’s van was seen pulling into Madison Square Garden by The Times on Wednesday night. </p><p>Swift and Kelce were spotted leaving the restaurant in May. They have also been known to spend time at Zero Bond, the members-only club that is also owned by Sartiano in the NoHo neighborhood.</p><h2>The décor had a garden theme.</h2><p><a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/02/style/swift-kelce-wedding-msg-rehearsal-dinner.html">In the days leading up</a> to the wedding, trees and plant life were seen being delivered to the arena, raising speculation that the design might lean heavily into a floral, earthy vibe.</p><p>Several anchors on “Good Morning America” attended the wedding and described the event the next day; George Stephanopoulos said the aesthetic was a “garden inside the Garden.”</p><figure><img alt="Some branches are seen in a large semi truck parked next to other semi trucks. " src="https://static01.nyt.com/images/2026/07/03/multimedia/03tnt-photos-zhcb-copy/03tnt-photos-zhcb-articleLarge.jpg"/><figcaption>A truck delivering décor to Madison Square Garden last week.  Dave Sanders for The New York Times</figcaption></figure><p>“It was just so beautiful,” he said. Despite the size of the guest list and the arena, which can hold around 20,000 people, “it’s hard to imagine that a place that big and a wedding with such stars could feel so personal and so intimate,” Stephanopoulos said. Robin Roberts, his co-anchor, added that the wedding couple had personalized vows.</p><p>That same day, arcade games were seen being removed from the Garden. A maroon vintage car with a license plate reading “JST&amp;T MRD” was also spotted being loaded into a truck, according <a href="https://pagesix.com/2026/07/04/entertainment/taylor-swift-travis-kelce-customize-car-with-special-license-plate-at-msg-wedding/">to Page Six photos</a>.</p><h2>Wedding favors also included T&amp;T logos.</h2><p><a href="https://x.com/MarenMorris/status/2073426235267469687">The singer Maren Morris posted what appeared</a> to be a wedding favor: a white embroidered handkerchief with a T&amp;T logo, in which the T’s overlapped with a dual heart. On either side of the logo were the date — July 3, 2026 — and the location, New York City. Underneath was the phrase “So it’s gonna be forever …,” a lyric from Swift’s song “Blank Space.”</p><p>In 2024, Kelce, a tight end for the Kansas City Chiefs, said on the podcast “<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GBtNU3b9H-I&amp;t=4744s">Bussin’ With the Boys</a>” that “Blank Space” was his favorite Swift track, and that “1989” was the album that got him listening to her music.</p><p><a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/03/fashion/weddings/taylor-swift-travis-kelce-married-wedding.html">The couple’s T&amp;T graphic was also spotted</a> on boxes some guests were carrying as they left Swift and Kelce’s rehearsal dinner Thursday night. The stylized letters were evocative of the logo for Swift’s album “The Tortured Poets Department.” </p><h2>Stevie Nicks performed.</h2><p>Stevie Nicks, a friend and mentor of Swift, performed at the wedding, <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/03/style/taylor-swift-stevie-nicks-performance.html">as The Times previously reported</a>. On “Good Morning America,” Roberts confirmed without offering details.</p><p>Donna Kelce, the mother of the groom, described the night <a href="https://www.instagram.com/reels/DaZZmAyA3hW/">in an Instagram video posted by Macy’s</a>: “I really can’t say a heck of a lot except it was magical, man, magical.”</p>
