<h2>Adapts to your Usecase</h2>
<p> Hyper 8 meets many needs, from the public and professional to the personal and private </p>
<ul> <li> <strong>  Public events and institutions </strong> Publish conference recordings, media libraries, video archives, ... </li> <li> <strong>  Artists, producers, studios </strong> Present your work to your audience, clients or team </li> <li> <strong>  Personal use </strong> Share vlogs or personal videos with friends and family </li> </ul>
<figure><img alt="Kristoffer Lislegaard's video page" src="https://hyper8.org/thumb_lislegaard_oy.jpg?1779906932954"/><figcaption>Kristoffer Lislegaard's video page</figcaption></figure>

<h2>Hybrid Editing Model</h2>
<p> Create sites using the built-in graphical user interface, your own text editor and tools, or both. </p>
<ul> <li> <strong>  Built-in graphical editor </strong> Comfortable and beginner friendly </li> <li> <strong>  File-based editing </strong> Custom workflows with any tool(s) of your choice </li> <li> <strong>  Versatile structure </strong> From a single video to any number of videos, playlists and nested collections </li> <li> <strong>  Portable building blocks </strong> Videos, playlists and collections can be moved within and across sites </li> </ul>
<figure><img alt="Parts of the editor's splash screen and some form field in the editor" src="https://hyper8.org/thumb_editor_crop.jpg?1779906932954"/><figcaption>Parts of the editor's splash screen and some form field in the editor</figcaption></figure>
<h2>Visually Adaptive</h2>
<p> Hyper 8's design adjusts on many levels. </p>
<ul> <li> <strong>  Responsive </strong> Functional on desktop, tablet and mobile </li> <li> <strong>  Adaptive brightness </strong> Dark/light color scheme, fixed or adapting to a visitor's system. </li> <li> <strong>  From wide cinema to mobile shorts </strong> Customizable aspect ratio (cinematic/portrait/...) for video content on your site </li> </ul>

<h2>Browse &amp; Search</h2>
<p> A fast, easily accessible navigation system </p>
<ul> <li> <strong>  Browse </strong> Directly jump to any collection, playlist or video </li> <li> <strong>  Search </strong> Zero-delay, client-side search </li> </ul>
<figure><img alt="PohoFusion's former video page" src="https://hyper8.org/thumb_pohofusion_search.jpg?1779906932954"/><figcaption>PohoFusion's former video page</figcaption></figure>
<h2>And more</h2>
<ul> <li> <strong>  RSS &amp; Atom feeds </strong> Allows visitors to subscribe to your updates </li> <li> <strong>  Platform integration (opt-in) </strong> Link previews and content embeds in timelines (e.g. Fediverse) </li> <li> <strong>  Embeds </strong> Allow your videos to be featured in articles and posts </li> <li> <strong>  Subtitles and interactive transcripts </strong> Allows integrating subtitles in multiple languages </li> <li> <strong>  Unlisted content </strong> Share videos and playlists with peers by flagging them as unlisted </li> <li> <strong>  Audio-only streaming </strong> Allow viewers to stream just the audio if desired </li> <li> <strong>  Built-in deployment </strong> Deploy using FTP (and soon SFTP) directly from the graphical editor </li> </ul>
