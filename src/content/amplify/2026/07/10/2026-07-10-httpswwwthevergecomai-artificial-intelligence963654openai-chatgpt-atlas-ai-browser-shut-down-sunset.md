<p>OpenAI is already shutting down ChatGPT Atlas, its browser that could do tasks for you on your behalf, less than a year after launching it. Atlas was announced <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/803475/openais-ai-powered-browser-chatgpt-atlas-google-chrome-competition-agent">in October</a>, but as part of its wave of news <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/963464/openai-gpt-5-6-codex-chatgpt-work">about ChatGPT Work today</a>, the company confirmed that it will be “sunsetting” Atlas and is targeting an August 9th date for deprecation.</p>
<p>In recent months, OpenAI has also shut down the <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/899850/openai-sora-ai-chatgpt">video generation app Sora</a> and paused plans for a <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/901293/openai-adult-mode-erotic-chatbot-shelved-indefinitely">ChatGPT “adult mode”</a> as it works to <a href="http://reddit.com/">reduce “side quests”</a> and catch up with Anthropic <a href="https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/911118/openai-memo-cro-ai-competition-anthropic">on productivity features</a>.</p>
<p>In <a href="https://x.com/JamesZmSun/status/2075290224327057644?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw%7Ctwcamp%5Etweetembed%7Ctwterm%5E2075290224327057644%7Ctwgr%5E6c8e130455b5aaa9840a043d94cfd131c19e96d8%7Ctwcon%5Es1_&amp;ref_url=https%3A%2F%2F9to5mac.com%2F2026%2F07%2F09%2Fopenai-is-discontinuing-chatgpt-atlas-its-standalone-desktop-browser%2F">a thread about</a> the ChatGPT Work announcements, which includes an updated browser in the desktop ChatGPT app and a cloud browser for work mode, OpenAI’s James Sun said that: “All these capabilities were built on what we learned from Atlas users who took a leap of faith on a new browser. You taught us how agents can help make browsing and doing work on the open web better, and we are applying these learnings to these new products.”</p>
