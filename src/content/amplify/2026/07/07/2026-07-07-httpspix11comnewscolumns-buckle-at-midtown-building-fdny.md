<p>NEW YORK CITY (PIX11) — A large FDNY response is underway in Midtown after two columns buckled and five floors caved in at a building under construction Tuesday morning.</p>
<p>FDNY said the call came in at 7:57 a.m. for reports of bricks falling from a 33-story high-rise at 235 East 42nd Street.</p>
<p>Floors 21 through 26 of the building caved in, according to FDNY. The building is the site of the former Pfizer location.</p>
<p>The site is an active, permitted construction project to convert the existing commercial office building into residential units, the city’s Department of Buildings press secretary told PIX11 News.</p>
<p>Multiple cracks and sagging floors were reported throughout the building, according to FDNY.</p>

<p>Second Avenue is closed, and East 42nd Street is blocked for access, according to the FDNY. Tape has also been placed on East 43rd Street as crews work to secure the area. Officials have also evacuated buildings around the area.</p>
<p>Workers who were doing construction at the buckled building were evacuated, and no injuries to workers or civilians were reported by FDNY.</p>

<p>A worker told PIX11 News’ Angelo Bavaro that his family called him, fearing the building had collapsed.</p>
<figure><picture><figure><img alt="" src="https://h104216-ucdn.mp.lura.live/iupl_lin/608/F60/608F601E061063F4A5FC8AB14FD92D83.jpg?aktaexp=2082787200&amp;aktasgn=5f07b616cd5c8ad3ce3bab82fdfaa566"/></figure></picture></figure>
<p>Cliff Johnson of Steamfitters Local 638, who was working inside the building, told PIX11 News that his members were inside when the structure began to fail.</p>
<p>“The general contractor chose to go non‑union for this project. All we want is responsible construction in the City of New York,” Johnson said. “They did not shore up the job correctly. The beams started crumbling, the floors started crumbling, and they might have to evacuate the other side.”</p>
<p>Johnson said the project was a total renovation, with crews gutting the 33‑story building and planning to add another 16 stories.</p>

<h2>Commute affected after columns buckle, floors cave in at Midtown building</h2>
<p>Evacuations and street closures caused major delays for commuters trying to get to their homes and workplaces.</p>
<p>MTA says the M42 buses are being detoured in both directions. While detoured, eastbound buses will not serve stops on East 42nd Street from Madison Avenue to Second Avenue. Stops will instead be made, as requested, along East 50th Street.</p>
<p>Northbound M101, M102 and M103 buses and M42 buses in both directions are running with delays.</p>
<p>City inspectors are on site investigating and they’re telling commuters to avoid the area.</p>
