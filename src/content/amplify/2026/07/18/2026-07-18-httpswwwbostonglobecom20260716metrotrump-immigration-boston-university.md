<p>The Trump administration changed a <a href="https://www.bostonglobe.com/2025/08/29/metro/trump-administration-international-student-visas/?p1=Article_Inline_Text_Link">longstanding policy</a> for international student visas Thursday, limiting how long they can stay in the country without approval from the federal government.</p>
<p>The new rule caps visa holders’ duration of stay at four years unless they receive an extension from US Citizenship and Immigration Services, amending a decades-old policy that said as long as the students made progress toward their degree or research purpose, there was no defined expiration date.</p>
<p>The new rule, which also restricts the international students’ ability to transfer schools or change majors without approval in their first year, will go into effect in mid-September.</p>
