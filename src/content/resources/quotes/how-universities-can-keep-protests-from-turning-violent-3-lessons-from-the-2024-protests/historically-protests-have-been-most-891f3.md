> Historically, protests [have been most likely to escalate](https://doi.org/10.1177/0022002719887491) when students feel unheard. In contrast, institutions that [adopt proactive strategies](https://scl.cornell.edu/sites/scl/files/documents/Navigating%20Campus%20Protests.pdf), such as facilitating conversations or including students in decision-making, often experience better outcomes.

As our survey data shows, the pro-Palestinian protests illustrate this dynamic.
