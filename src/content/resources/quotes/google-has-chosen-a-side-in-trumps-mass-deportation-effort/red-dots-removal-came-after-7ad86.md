> Red Dot’s removal came after a cascading series of events starting in September. That month 29-year-old Joshua Jahn [opened fire at an ICE facility in Dallas](https://www.cnn.com/2025/10/13/us/dallas-ice-shooter-radiation-practiced-shooting-hnk?ref=404media.co), killing two detainees and wounding another. Authorities say Jahn used his phone to search for ICE-spotting apps, including ICEBlock, before the shooting, [Fox reported](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/apple-takes-down-ice-tracking-app-after-pressure-from-ag-bondi?ref=404media.co). A short while after, the Department of Justice contacted Apple and demanded it remove ICEBlock, which Apple did, despite such an [app being First Amendment protected speech](https://www.npr.org/2025/10/23/nx-s1-5570794/creator-of-app-that-tracked-ice-talks-about-its-removal-and-the-first-amendment?ref=404media.co).
