> The affected projects all use mRNA technology, an advance that saved [millions](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-023-03119-x) of people from death by COVID alone. That is reality, and that’s not where Kennedy lives. The secretary of Health and Human Services [said](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/08/05/hhs-plans-to-terminate-22-mrna-vaccine-projects-00494922) that mRNA research “poses more risk than benefits for these respiratory viruses” although it doesn’t. Within the “Make America Healthy Again” universe, the Kennedy [brain worm](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/05/08/us/rfk-jr-brain-health-memory-loss.html) rules. Outside it, the Earth is still round. Vaccines do not cause autism, antidepressants do not behave like heroin, and seed oils [are not](https://www.newyorker.com/news/the-lede/the-junk-science-of-robert-f-kennedy-jr) dangerous. Raw milk can make you [sick](https://www.cnn.com/2025/08/05/health/raw-milk-florida-outbreak), or even kill you, and cod-liver oil will never cure the measles. MAHA cannot undo these facts, but it can bury the truth in so many lies that reality becomes elusive. The brain worm is in power, via Kennedy, and science is on the defense. By May, the Trump administration had cut [$2.3 billion](https://kffhealthnews.org/news/article/health-care-spending-cuts-research-trump-administration-tariffs-public-health/) in new grants from the National Institutes of Health, and it has [frozen](https://www.politico.com/news/2025/08/06/ucla-has-more-than-half-a-billion-dollars-in-funding-suspended-by-trump-administration-00495071) billions in research funds to public universities.

The war on science has entered a grave new era,
