> New Atheism is something else altogether. As the historian Erik Baker [wrote](https://defector.com/the-ghosts-of-new-atheism-still-haunt-us) for Defector last year, the brand, or tendency, was “about science,” not theology, and it was political from the start. Their first enemies were not creationists “​​but a group of atheist Marxist biologists” in the 1970s, as Baker wrote. The conflict was ideological. Sociobiologists said that our genes explained our behavior, choices, and capacity to reason. Opponents like the late biologist Stephen Jay Gould i[dentified](https://www.nybooks.com/articles/1975/11/13/against-sociobiology/) sociobiology as biological determinism by another name and linked it to eugenics.
