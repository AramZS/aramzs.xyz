> the polling on this is so strong, I think it’s fair to say that a lot of people *hate* AI, and that [Gen Z in particular seems to hate AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/909687/gen-z-doesnt-like-ai-gallup) more and more as they encounter it. There’s that *NBC News* poll [showing AI with worse favorability than ICE](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/891724/nbc-news-march-2026-poll-ai-ice) and only a little bit above the war in Iran and the Democrats generally. That’s with nearly two thirds of respondents saying they used ChatGPT or Copilot in the last month. Quinnipiac just found that [over half of Americans think AI will do more harm than good](https://poll.qu.edu/poll-release?releaseid=3955), while more than 80 percent of people were either very concerned or somewhat concerned about the technology. Only 35 percent of people were excited about it.
