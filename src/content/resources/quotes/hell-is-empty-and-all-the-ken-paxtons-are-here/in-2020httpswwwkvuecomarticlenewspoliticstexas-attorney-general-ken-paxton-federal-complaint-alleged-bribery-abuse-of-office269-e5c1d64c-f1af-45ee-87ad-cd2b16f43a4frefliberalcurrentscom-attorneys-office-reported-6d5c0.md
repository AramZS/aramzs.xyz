> In [2020](https://www.kvue.com/article/news/politics/texas-attorney-general-ken-paxton-federal-complaint-alleged-bribery-abuse-of-office/269-e5c1d64c-f1af-45ee-87ad-cd2b16f43a4f?ref=liberalcurrents.com), attorneys in his office reported Paxton for a litany of abuses, including taking bribes. When he fired those [whistleblowers](https://thebarbedwire.com/2026/02/17/ken-paxton-scandal-timeline/?ref=liberalcurrents.com), they sued him and ultimately won in 2025. During this time, Paxton was impeached, an astonishing achievement for a powerful Republican in deep red Texas.
