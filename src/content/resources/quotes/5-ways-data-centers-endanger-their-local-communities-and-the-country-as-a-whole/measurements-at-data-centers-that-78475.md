> Measurements at the data centers that were the subject of complaints found noise levels were between [40 and 59 decibels](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598-2.pdf#page=93) on residential property.

Those noise levels are [quieter than a conversation with someone 3 feet away](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598-2.pdf#page=94) and not loud enough to damage people’s hearing or violate local noise ordinances. But they are close to levels the EPA says [reduce people’s ability to work, sleep and exercise](https://www.epa.gov/archive/epa/aboutepa/epa-identifies-noise-levels-affecting-health-and-welfare.html). Some people have complained that [data center noise has given them trouble sleeping](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598-2.pdf#page=93) and concentrating, and some have said they avoid using their homes’ outdoor spaces, where the noise is louder.
