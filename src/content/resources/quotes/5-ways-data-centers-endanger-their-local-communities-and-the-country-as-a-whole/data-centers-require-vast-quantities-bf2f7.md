> Data centers require [vast quantities of water](https://theconversation.com/data-centers-consume-massive-amounts-of-water-companies-rarely-tell-the-public-exactly-how-much-262901) to cool their servers. Globally, they are projected to consume between [4.2 billion and 6.6 billion cubic meters](https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/3724499) of water annually by 2027. In the United States, data centers already rank among the [top 10](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1748-9326/abfba1) industrial water users.

In northern Virginia, data center water use has risen sharply. In Loudoun County alone, just northwest of D.C., potable water use by data centers [more than doubled between 2019 and 2023](https://www.sierraclub.org/virginia/press-kit-virginia-s-data-center-burden), while facilities across northern Virginia consumed [nearly 2 billion gallons of water in 2023](https://jlarc.virginia.gov/pdfs/reports/Rpt598-2.pdf).
