> King Gizzard’s experience suggests just how easy it may remain for an impersonator to find fortune in the long tail of Spotify’s catalog. And at some point, that may undermine trust in the platform overall. Especially when, according to a survey of 9,000 people from streaming service Deezer this month, 97 percent of people [can’t tell the difference](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/are-you-listening-bots-survey-shows-ai-music-is-virtually-undetectable-2025-11-12/?ref=platformer.news) between AI-generated music and the real thing.
