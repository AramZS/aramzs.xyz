> Some of CNBC’s reporting about Kalshi includes the disclosure, “[CNBC and Kalshi have a commercial relationship that includes customer acquisition and a minority investment](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/02/bass-and-pratt-will-advance-in-la-mayoral-race-traders-say.html).” This means CNBC is paid every time it can convert a viewer to a Kalshi user. As an investor, the network also benefits if Kalshi’s overall valuation increases. CNBC is also paid directly by Kalshi for using its data, according to [The Wrap](https://www.thewrap.com/media-platforms/journalism/polymarket-kalshi-cnn-cnbc-dow-jones/).
