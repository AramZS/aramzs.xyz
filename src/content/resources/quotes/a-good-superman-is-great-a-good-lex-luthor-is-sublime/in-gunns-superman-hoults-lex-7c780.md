> In Gunn’s *Superman*, Hoult’s Lex, in his sky fortress, rails against Superman as an alien who took what he sees as his, undermining his incredible strides in human achievement and the acclaim he believes would otherwise go to him. He looks at the hero in flight and seethes with rage, too small to realize that he is also in the clouds, and that he could have taken us all with him.
