> The strains on international enrollment only add to distress for schools already on the financial brink.

Colleges around the country [have been closing](https://apnews.com/article/college-closing-university-of-arts-philadelphia-7c884439f16f24d99a61544381f49c23) as they cope with declines in domestic enrollment, a consequence of changing demographics and the [effects of the pandemic](https://apnews.com/article/skipping-college-student-loans-trade-jobs-efc1f6d6067ab770f6e512b3f7719cc0). Nationwide, private colleges have been closing at a rate of about two per month, according to the State Higher Education Executive Officers Association.
