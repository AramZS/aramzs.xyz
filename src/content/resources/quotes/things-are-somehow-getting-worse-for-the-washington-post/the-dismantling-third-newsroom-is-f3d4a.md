> the dismantling of the third newsroom is one more reason for skepticism among staff. “The question is, What has Will Lewis accomplished? That was the question going around all of the group chats this week,” said one *Post* staffer. “The only thing he seems to have accomplished is driving off talented people and reducing head count. Maybe that was the point. But all of his flowery vows about what the *Post* was going to do, and his ‘Fix it, build it, scale it’ campaign — that led to nothing. And the third newsroom is now defunct.”
