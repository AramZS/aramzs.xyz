> To me, the episode demonstrated that there is no vision, no game plan, and no commitment to build on existing traffic. Instead, the buyouts have removed some of *The Post*’s biggest traffic generators — and I don’t see a strategy to replace what has been lost. Lewis invested time and resources in creating what he called “a third newsroom” — his one big idea — but the effort was [abruptly dropped](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/22/washington-post-third-newsroom) last month, and the manager who had led it also took the buyout.
