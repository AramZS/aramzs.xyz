> A staff-wide meeting led by new executive editor Matt Murray didn’t provide much clarity. As far as I could understand, *The Post* was going to try to appeal to people who weren’t necessarily committed to news. Well, *The New York Times* figured that out ten years ago, expanding into games, sports, cooking and product recommendations. The Post was just eating its dust.
