> “Whistleblowers, we discovered, are conservative people devoted to their work and their organizations,” the Glazers wrote. “Invariably, they believed that they were defending the true mission of their organization by resisting illicit practices and could not comprehend how their superiors could risk the good name of their company by producing defective products, the reputation of their hospital by abusing and neglecting patients, or the integrity of their agency by allowing their safety reports to be tampered with or distorted.”
