> As after the Enron scandal, Leyla thought about switching careers. And, as back then, she decided instead to use her voice, calling the head of her firm to explain what had happened and mentioning how it would look if Fox News aired a follow-up segment about how the Stanford whistleblower the network had just interviewed had been fired by another company for speaking out. The broker-dealer quickly reversed course and raised no objections when Leyla’s lawyer subsequently informed the company that she was planning to attend the Senate Banking Committee hearing.
