> This uncertainty costs money: A power plant could be built in advance, only to find out that [some or all of its capacity isn’t needed](https://www.utilitydive.com/news/georgia-power-large-load-data-centers/806300/). Or no power plant is built, and a data center pops up,[competing for a limited supply of electricity](https://ceea.us/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Virginias-Energy-Crossroads-CEEA-Oct-2025-Final.pdf).

Either way, someone needs to pay—for the excess capacity or for the increased price of what power is available. There are three possible groups that might pay: the utilities that provide electricity, the data center customers, and the rest of the customers on the system.
