> Lemley’s life as an intellectual property lawyer was orderly enough. He’s a professor at Stanford University and has consulted for [Amazon](https://www.wired.com/tag/amazon/), [Google](https://www.wired.com/tag/google/), and [Meta](https://www.wired.com/tag/meta/). “I always enjoyed that the area I practice in has largely been apolitical,” Lemley tells me. What’s more, his democratic values neatly aligned with those of the companies that hired him.

But in January, Lemley made a radical move. “I have struggled with how to respond to [Mark Zuckerberg](https://www.wired.com/tag/mark-zuckerberg/) and Facebook’s descent into toxic [masculinity](https://www.wired.com/story/uncanny-valley-1-mark-zuckerberg-midlife-crisis/) and Neo-Nazi madness,” he posted on LinkedIn. “I have fired Meta as a client.”
