> This is where regional coordination becomes essential. I've written before about what I've called [the New England Option](https://foundhistory.org/the-new-england-option/): the recognition that in an era of federal dysfunction, the most effective path forward for the Northeast may not run through Washington at all. And I've noted [examples of this approach already in action](https://foundhistory.org/the-new-england-option-in-action/), including the Northeast Public Health Collaborative, announced last year by eight states in response to shifting federal health policy.
