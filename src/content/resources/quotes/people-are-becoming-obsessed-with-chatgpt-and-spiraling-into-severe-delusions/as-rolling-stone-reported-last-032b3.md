> As Rolling Stone reported last month, parts of social media are being overrun with what's being referred to as "ChatGPT-induced psychosis," or by the impolitic term "AI schizoposting": delusional, meandering screeds about godlike entities unlocked from ChatGPT, fantastical hidden spiritual realms, or nonsensical new theories about math, physics and reality. An entire AI subreddit recently banned the practice, calling chatbots "ego-reinforcing glazing machines that reinforce unstable and narcissistic personalities."
