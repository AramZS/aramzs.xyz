> The lesson to any journalist attempting to reveal the highest-stakes stories is that you must fight your outlet if you're going to do the work your audience needs to understand the situation it's in. (More fighting than is typical, I mean; IYKYK.) That your editor considers their job to be stopping you from doing yours. At that point, you either fight a battle you know you're likely to lose, or you quit.
