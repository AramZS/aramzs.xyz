> But to many of the faculty I spoke to, Penn’s capitulation to Trumpism became official on July 1 of last year, when, following closed-door negotiations, it was announced that the university would [fully comply](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/upenn-updates-swimming-records-after-settlement-with-feds-on-transgender-athletes-case) with Department of Education demands to ban transgender women athletes from women’s sports, which included stripping trans swimmer Lia Thomas of her swimming awards and records from the 2021–2022 season.
