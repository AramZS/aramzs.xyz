> 2018, four Virginia voters whom PILF falsely accused of voting illegally [filed a lawsuit](https://southerncoalition.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/LULAC-of-Richmond-v.-PILF.pdf) against Adams and PILF, accusing them of violating the Voting Rights Act and the 1871 Ku Klux Klan Act—which empowers individuals to sue when vigilante groups threaten their 14th Amendment rights—through “a modern, covert, and particularly insidious method of voter intimidation.” PILF [settled the case](https://southerncoalition.org/voters-strike-back-and-win-settlement-in-virginia/) before it went to trial, with Adams agreeing to apologize to the plaintiffs and PILF pledging to redact sensitive personal information.
