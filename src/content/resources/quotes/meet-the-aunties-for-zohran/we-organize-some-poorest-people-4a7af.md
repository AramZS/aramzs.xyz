> "We organize some of the poorest people in New York, and it's really hard for them to stay here even though they're the ones taking care of our children, cleaning apartments, doing the work that often goes unnoticed," explains Winsome Pendergrass, a NYCC organizer in her 70s who works with predominantly Black and Latino communities and immigrated to New York from Jamaica in the early 2000s.
