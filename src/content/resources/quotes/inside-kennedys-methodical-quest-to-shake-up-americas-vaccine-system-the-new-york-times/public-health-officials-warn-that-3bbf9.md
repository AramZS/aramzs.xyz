> Public health officials warn that if Mr. Kennedy succeeds, measles and other infectious diseases will come roaring back, jeopardizing the health of all American children. Six medical organizations [have taken Mr. Kennedy to court](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/07/07/health/vaccines-kennedy-lawsuit.html), accusing him of “a clear pattern of hostility toward established scientific processes” that has resulted in chaos throughout the health care system.
