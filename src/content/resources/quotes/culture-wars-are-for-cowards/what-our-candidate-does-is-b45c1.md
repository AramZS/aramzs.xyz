> What our candidate does is: To uphold the principles that they believe in. Solidarity means everyone. We are, all of us, family, and we will help and love one another, and fight for one another. We don’t let screeching snakes slither in our midst and try to divide us and pick people off and convince us to sell each other out. We are one, a family, an army. We leave no one behind, we fight for everyone, we look out for one another. We are wise to your tricks, fuckers!
