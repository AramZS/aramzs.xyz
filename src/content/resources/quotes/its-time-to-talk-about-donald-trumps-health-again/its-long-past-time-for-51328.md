> It’s long past time for this to be a sustained front-page story in the US. One of my [consistent refrains](https://www.doomsdayscenario.co/p/has-america-reached-the-end-of-the-road-a1d4feac8041058a) this year has been that the US media knows how to provide wall-to-wall coverage and how to elevate a “news story” to a “news event.” (Think Hunter Biden or Hillary’s emails.) It’s clear that the media hasn’t yet decided that the president’s health and fitness is worthy of a “news event.” But it should be.
