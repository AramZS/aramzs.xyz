> This anthropomorphization is beneficial to big tech companies in several ways. On one level, it markets their product as far more capable (and sentient) than it actually is, justifying absurd company valuations. On another, it creates a layer of distance between their [consistently bad choices](https://www.npr.org/2025/07/09/nx-s1-5462609/grok-elon-musk-antisemitic-racist-content?ref=karlbode.com) and any personal responsibility.
