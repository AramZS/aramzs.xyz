> Meta’s legal loss in New Mexico this week is part of a broader attack on digital freedoms in the US right now. There are age verification laws [popping up](https://action.freespeechcoalition.com/age-verification-bills/) in states across the country, bills that would [eliminate](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2023/07/amended-cooper-davis-act-direct-threat-encryption) end-to-end encryption entirely, and the Kids Online Safety Act (KOSA), which would force platforms [to censor content](https://www.salon.com/2025/12/22/congress-may-be-about-to-create-the-bad-internet/) for minors, which would mean building tools that could determine who is a minor and who is not. All of these bills are meant to bypass or dismantle Section 230, the statute that basically makes the internet in the US work.
