> The following day, Matthew Ball spoke with Zuckerberg in a live audio session that has [since been pulled](https://www.facebook.com/zuck/videos/572525803829402/) from Zuckerberg’s Facebook page, though [clips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Ls3AX4nFng) remain available on YouTube. A [transcript](https://epublications.marquette.edu/zuckerberg_files_transcripts/1461/) of the conversation reads like a context-free time capsule of that era, with praise for meme stocks, NFTs, and Web3 in concept more than in practice — and, of course, Ball’s writing on the metaverse. (Six months after this interview, the NFT market would [well and truly collapse](https://www.wsj.com/finance/nft-sales-are-flatlining-11651552616), with peak transactions occurring the month before Ball and Zuckerberg spoke.)
