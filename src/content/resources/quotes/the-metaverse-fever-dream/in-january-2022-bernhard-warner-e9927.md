> In January 2022, Bernhard Warner of *Fortune* was getting excited about the [possibilities of the metaverse, writing](https://fortune.com/longform/wall-street-metaverse-web3-investors-roblox-meta-platforms-microsoft/) it “might be the most important trend in tech since the iPhone”, perhaps “a tectonic shift in tech that they [big tech and big investors] can’t afford to miss”. The way Roblox was “monetizing the metaverse” was a key piece of evidence, with [virtual concerts](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLHTYdtZAKEtjqIOb-iFYMx0s3XMUXmuPi) and — most importantly — brands.
