> He said “remote work is here to stay for a lot of people” in this keynote, less than two years before [ordering in-office work three days per week](https://www.businessinsider.com/all-rules-of-metas-new-aggressive-strict-rto-mandate-2023-8?op=1); two years after that, Instagram [demanded five days per week](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/01/meta-instagram-rto-return-to-office.html) in-office. I guess “a lot of people” does not include the people who are building the products that let a lot of *other* people work remotely. That is a little weird.
