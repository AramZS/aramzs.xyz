> Despite, or because of, his demonstrable lack of expertise in 21st-century literary studies, Appiah wants to recall literary studies to its supposed roots in close reading and the autonomy of the aesthetic. This is an odd position for a writer interested in Du Bois, who famously wrote and lived by the dictum “all Art is propaganda and ever must be.”
