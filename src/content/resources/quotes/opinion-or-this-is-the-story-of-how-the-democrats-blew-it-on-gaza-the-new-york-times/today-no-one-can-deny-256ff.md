> Today, no one can deny that the Israeli government [prevented aid](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cj6ynz22871o) from reaching Gaza, used force against civilians well beyond the [laws of war](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/israel-palestine-gaza-war-crimes-genocide.html) and destroyed most of the Gaza Strip. Those facts led many [scholars](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/07/15/opinion/israel-gaza-holocaust-genocide-palestinians.html?searchResultPosition=15), [human rights](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/07/28/world/middleeast/israel-genocide-gaza-rights-groups.html) organizations and [U.N. bodies](https://www.ohchr.org/en/press-releases/2025/09/israel-has-committed-genocide-gaza-strip-un-commission-finds) to conclude that Israel committed genocide, using weapons supplied by the U.S. — a moral stain that cannot be removed.
