> Danny DeVito, one of the union world’s favorite celebrities, tweeted, “NO CONTRACTS NO SNACKS,” a phrase that quickly became a motto of the strike. By the fourth week, the strikers had arranged for a projector to beam images onto the side of the factory at night, including an enormous circular picture of DeVito’s smiling face with “NO CONTRACTS NO SNACKS” surrounding it in a motivational garland.
