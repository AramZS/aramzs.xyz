> There are countless potential union campaigns that never come into being because of the failure of one of the links in this chain of events. The most common, by far, is the simple fact that most people in bad jobs do not decide to take it upon themselves to try to organize a union. Of those who do, many of them don’t know how to do it, or who to even ask.
