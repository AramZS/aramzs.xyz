> “They’re trying to show us our place. It’s not about money. It’s about where we belong in society. We ‘have a place.’ But we don’t,” she said, her right hand gesturing back toward the factory for emphasis. “I got up today to be here because I needed my brothers and sisters to see that they will not break me. They will not break me. I’m here. And I’ll be here tomorrow. And I’ll be here every day, until we win.”
