> The Pope himself has been a vocal friend to unions since he assumed office. In 2017 he told a group of Italian unionists that “There is no good society without a union,” and then warned that “Unions over time have ended up resembling politicians too much, or rather political parties,” and encouraged unions to look out for the most oppressed workers even if they weren’t members.
