> I keep asking myself:

What happened to the principles that were professed a decade ago? To address climate change? To reduce racial, gender, and economic inequality? To “[don’t be evil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_be_evil)”?

Were these principles abandoned, or were they merely born of convenience?

Has tech always been like this? Was I just blind to it before?

When I say that I am burnt out I do not mean simply that I am tired. I’m referring to the “[emotional experience of political defeat](https://aworkinglibrary.com/writing/loss-of-an-ideal)”
