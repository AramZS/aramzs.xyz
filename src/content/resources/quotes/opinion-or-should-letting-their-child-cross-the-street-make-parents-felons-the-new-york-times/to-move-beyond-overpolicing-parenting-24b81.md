> to move beyond the overpolicing of parenting we’ve seen in the last 10 or 15 years. A Georgia [mother who didn’t realize her 10-year-old boy had walked](https://www.freerangekids.com/free-range-kids-brittany-patterson-and-arresting-moms-whose-kids-take-a-walk/) to the center of their small town and a South Carolina woman who let her 9-year-old daughter [play in a well-populated park while she worked](https://www.theatlantic.com/national/archive/2014/07/arrested-for-letting-a-9-year-old-play-at-the-park-alone/374436/) at a McDonald’s nearby faced criminal charges.
