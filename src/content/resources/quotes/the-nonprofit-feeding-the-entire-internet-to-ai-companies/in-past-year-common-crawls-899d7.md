> In the past year, Common Crawl’s CCBot has become [the scraper most widely blocked](https://originality.ai/ai-bot-blocking) by the top 1,000 websites, surpassing even OpenAI’s GPTBot, which collects content for ChatGPT. However, blocking only prevents future content from being scraped. It doesn’t affect the webpages Common Crawl has already collected and stored in its archives.
