> The calculation is easy to see, if difficult to credit. Americans, like the man said, love a winner, and will not tolerate a loser; a party that is manifestly out of power might think that it would be best not to be seen at all, and to let the party in charge take the blame for all the awful and destructive things that it is doing. The idea that this should somehow be left to happen naturally, through people noticing it on their own, reflects a strategic passivity that is, depending on your perspective, either criminal or merely nauseating. We already know how well it is working; we're soaking in it.
