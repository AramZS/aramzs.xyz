> Deaths in ICE detention centers have occurred with [increasing frequency](https://www.washingtonpost.com/immigration/2025/12/20/ice-detainee-deaths/) in recent months, as President Donald Trump’s immigration crackdown floods these facilities with record numbers of detainees. At least 30 people died in detention last year — the highest in two decades — and Lunas Campos is one of four who died in the first nine days of 2026 alone, according to ICE, which posts information about all detainee deaths on its website.
