> maintainers can reduce their up-front costs by making certain actions more expensive. They could charge money to open an issue or pull request, similar to how highways charge a higher toll during commute hours to reduce the number of cars on the road. We haven’t yet seen many developers experiment with these ideas, partly because it’s still seen as violating the participatory norms of open source, and partly because it would require platform support to pull off well.
