> Citing a senior law enforcement official, [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/08/08/us/emory-university-cdc-shooting.html) reported the shooter, identified as 30-year-old Patrick Joseph White, was fixated on the Covid vaccine, which he blamed for his health problems. In response to the shooting and reports of White’s motivations, newly appointed CDC Director Susan Monarez convened an online all-hands meeting of the agency division that focuses on vaccines — the National Center for Immunization and Respiratory Diseases.
