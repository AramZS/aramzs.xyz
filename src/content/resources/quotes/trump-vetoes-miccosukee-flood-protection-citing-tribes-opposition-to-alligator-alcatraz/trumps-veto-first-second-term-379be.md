> “Trump’s veto, the first of his second term, was roundly criticized by Colorado Democrats as another act of retaliation against the state [for its ongoing incarceration](https://coloradonewsline.com/2025/12/02/colorado-governor-rejection-tina-peters/) of former Mesa County Clerk Tina Peters, who was convicted on state charges related to breaking into the county’s voting machines to prove unfounded claims of widespread election fraud in 2020.

“The veto followed the administration’s [announcement earlier this month](https://coloradonewsline.com/2025/12/26/trump-ncar-dismembering-facts/) that it would dismantle Boulder’s National Center for Atmospheric Research, and its denial of [two disaster declaration requests](https://coloradonewsline.com/briefs/trump-colorado-fema-disaster/) that would have opened up federal funding to support wildfire and flood recovery in the state.”
