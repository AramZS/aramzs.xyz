> the kitchen are *material*, and tools. The difference between a 5lb sack of flour and an individually-packaged snack cake is the difference between potential energy and the bill for energy consumed. At the end, we still eat. The difference is not the overall topology of the system, but our place in it. We come to the kitchen to take up knives, not coins or tokens.
