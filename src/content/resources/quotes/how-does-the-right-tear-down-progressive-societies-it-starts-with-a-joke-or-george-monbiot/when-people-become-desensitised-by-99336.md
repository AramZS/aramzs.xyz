> When people become desensitised by ironic calls for violence, the difference between a humorous position and an ideological conviction can begin to break down. They are said by some researchers to suffer from “[irony poisoning](https://www.antihate.ca/understanding_defining_irony_poisoning)”. If, for example, people are repeatedly exposed to racial stereotypes in “humorous” form, they are likely to lose perspective, and [start to absorb and affirm them](https://www.dandc.eu/en/article/when-hatred-wrapped-satire-xenophobia-irony-and-fascism-jokes-it-leaves-mainstream-society). The results are anything but amusing.
