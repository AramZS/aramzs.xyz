> Not only does Salesforce not actually sell "agents," its own research shows that agents only achieve around a 58% success rate on **single-step tasks**, meaning, [to quote The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/06/16/salesforce_llm_agents_benchmark/?ref=wheresyoured.at), "tasks that can be completed in a single step without needing follow-up actions or more information." On multi-step tasks — so, you know, *most tasks* — they succeed a depressing 35% of the time.
