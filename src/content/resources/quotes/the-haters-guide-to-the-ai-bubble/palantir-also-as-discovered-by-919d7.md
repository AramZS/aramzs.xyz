> Palantir also, [as discovered by the Autonomy Institute’s recent study](https://adu.autonomy.work/posts/2025_05_21_ai_risk/?ref=wheresyoured.at), [recently added to the following to its public disclosures](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1321655/000132165525000022/pltr-20241231.htm?ref=wheresyoured.at):

> *There are significant risks involved in deploying AI and there can be no assurance that using AI in our platforms and products will enhance or be beneficial to our business, including our profitability.*
