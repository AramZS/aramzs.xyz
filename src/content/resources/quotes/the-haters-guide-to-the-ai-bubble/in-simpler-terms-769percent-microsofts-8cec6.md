> In simpler terms, 76.9% of Microsoft's AI revenue comes from OpenAI, and is sold at just above or at cost, making Microsoft's "real" AI revenue about $3 billion, [or around 3.75% of this year's capital expenditures](https://www.cfodive.com/news/microsoft-capex-grow-slower-rate-cfo-ai/746947/?ref=wheresyoured.at), or 16.25% if you count OpenAI's revenue, which costs Microsoft more money than it earns.
