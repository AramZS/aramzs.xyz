> Social media, [James O’Sullivan writes in Noema](https://www.noemamag.com/the-last-days-of-social-media/?ref=torment-nexus.mathewingram.com), was “built on the romance of authenticity. Early platforms sold themselves as conduits for genuine connection: stuff you wanted to see, like your friend’s wedding and your cousin’s dog. But the attention economy, and more recently, the generative AI-fueled late attention economy, have broken whatever social contract underpinned that illusion.”
