> Meme House LA and its occupants represent one of the stranger phenomena of the postpandemic internet: the emergence of a financial ecosystem cobbled together from Telegram group chats, Discord channels, and Twitter DMswhere millions are made and lost by people who in a previous era would have had little access to or interest in the markets. It is all centered on an asset that has no clear function or utility. But it is powered by the most valuable tender on the planet: attention.
