> 100 million is *far* more than the total number of immigrants in the United States (which is [estimated at around 52 million](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/08/21/key-findings-about-us-immigrants/#:~:text=As%20of%20June%202025%2C%2051.9,immigrants%20than%20any%20other%20country.)). Instead, it’s close to [the total number of nonwhite people in the country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Americans). So the idea of “100 million deportations” clearly goes well beyond the idea of deporting illegal immigrants, and well beyond the idea of deporting *all* immigrants, into the territory of ethnic cleansing.

The DHS is posting these memes as a recruitment tactic, and polls about the “Great Replacement” show that there’s a large pool of potential recruits to whom this rhetoric is likely to appeal. In other words, many of the ICE agents now going around kicking in doors, beating up and threatening protesters, arresting citizens on pure suspicion, and occasionally shooting people believe that *they are engaged in a race war*.
