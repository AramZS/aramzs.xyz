> Because COVID destabilizes the immune system, researchers are now beginning to see a link between repeat COVID infections and rising cancer rates in young people. One study recently [found](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S092544392400557X#:~:text=SARS-CoV-2) that a COVID infection can accelerate or increase cancer risk, while another study [revealed](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12354360/) that a COVID infection substantially heightened the risk of six cancers caused by the sexually transmitted human papillomavirus.
