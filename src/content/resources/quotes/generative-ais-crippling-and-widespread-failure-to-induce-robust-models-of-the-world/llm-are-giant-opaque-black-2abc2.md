> LLM are giant, opaque black boxes with no explicit models of at all. Part of what it means to say that an LLM is a black box is to say that you can’t point to a model of a particular set of facts etc inside. (Many people realize that LLMs are “black boxes”, but don’t quite understand this important implication.)
A whole field known as “mechanistic interpretability” has tried to derive (or infer) world models from LLMs, but with very limited success.
