> There’s a reason 1999’s [*Pirates of Silicon Valley*](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0168122/?ref=fansplaining.com) retains an outsized role in the cultural memory given that it initially aired on TNT (and it’s not just because [it stars Noah Wyle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lEyrivrjAuU&ref=fansplaining.com)). Centering on the early careers of Bill Gates and Steve Jobs, it was a precursor for much of tech realism to come: namely, biopics upon biopics, Hollywood’s responses to the narratives Silicon Valley’s stars spun about themselves.
