> Data compiled by local news outlets shows that courthouse detentions [are uniquely prevalent](https://ctmirror.org/2025/08/11/nyc-immigration-courthouse-arrests-data-analysis/) in New York. In the city, there are several organized efforts to offer immigrants at the courthouses legal assistance or simply accompaniment as they face the threat stalking the halls. Some of the groups volunteering in the courts decline to speak to the press or identify themselves for fear of retaliation.
