> The reality is that chatbots like OpenAI’s ChatGPT, Google’s Gemini, or Anthropic’s Claude still make regular mistakes. According to an [October study by the *BBC*](https://www.bbc.co.uk/mediacentre/2025/new-ebu-research-ai-assistants-news-content), even the most advanced AI chatbots gave wrong answers a whopping 45 percent of the time.
